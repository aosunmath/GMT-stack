# Stack Project of Geometric Measure Theory and Variational Problems rules

- This project is the Stack Project of Geometric Measure Theory and Variational Problems, using Xia’s Pome framework. Its subject scope includes minimal surfaces, CMC surfaces, min-max theory, obstacle problems, mean curvature flows, phase transitions, and related geometric PDE. The active manuscript is in `content/gmt-foundations/`, based on the user-supplied references. Add content only when requested; do not restore Pome’s books, proofs, chapters, historical prose, or source-specific adapters.
- Read [the integration contract](documentation/integration-layout.md) before adding or reorganizing content.
- Arrange future content by mathematical subject. Generate reader numbers after chapter placement, section promotion, and canonical deduplication. Source numbers are provenance; never hard-code reader locators.
- Preserve permanent GMT tags and source labels through later reorganizations. The initial removal of inherited Pome content deliberately removes its reading routes instead of redirecting them into unrelated GMT content.
- Keep complete theorem hypotheses and source attribution. Separate fixed-class widths from volume-spectrum widths; state dimensions, coefficients, topology, and multiplicity assumptions. Distinguish introductory scope notes from completed theory. Only reviewed mathematical duplicates may share a canonical entry. A badge is verified only with exact proof evidence.
- Keep the shared renderer, numbering, cross-reference, search, citation, and proof-review machinery. Small synthetic test fixtures are infrastructure, not published mathematical content.
- Use this fork’s identity and URLs from `scripts/project.py`. Comments remain disabled until the fork has its own valid configuration. Never use Xia’s discussion identifiers or publish to his repository.
- Preserve the original infrastructure license and acknowledge Pome and Gerby.
- Run Python and JavaScript tests, a complete build with link checks, and desktop/mobile reader checks before publication. For an empty edition, verify that collection, search, source, and formalization inventories are empty and old generated routes are absent.
- Keep documentation and interface text in English. Keep private research files out of exports. Updates remain on request; do not add schedules or GitHub Actions CI.
