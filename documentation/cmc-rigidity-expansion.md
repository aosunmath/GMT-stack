## Integral identities and Euclidean rigidity

Tangential position calculus and the divergence-free first Newton tensor yield both Minkowski identities. The support function satisfies an inhomogeneous Jacobi equation. These identities give the CMC area-volume balance and a zero-mean test whose second variation is minus the integrated umbilicity defect. An explicit parallel displacement followed by dilation realizes this speed with exactly fixed volume. The resulting proof covers connected smooth compact embedded CMC boundaries in Euclidean space, without star-shapedness or convexity assumptions. It is explicitly a case of the Barbosa–do Carmo theorem; the broader immersed statement is not silently asserted using enclosed-region arguments.

## Constraints and spectral tests

The bilinear constrained nullspace is characterized by Jf being constant, not necessarily zero. Zero quadratic form implies membership in that nullspace only under stability. Tangents to actual fixed-volume CMC families and ambient Killing speeds are treated separately; no integrability of arbitrary Jacobi fields is asserted. The constant-potential criterion is q <= lambda1. Equality requires a nonzero Killing normal mode under the stated bounding hypotheses. Compact Laplace spectral theory remains an external analytic input for eigenfunction existence, index, and nullity.

## Space forms and examples

Radial Jacobi fields give the metric, curvature, and Jacobi potential of geodesic spheres in every simply connected space form. Sharp Poincare proves weak stability and unrestricted index one; the explicit external spherical harmonic decomposition gives nullity n+1. Position/normal Laplace equations produce a finite family of zero-mean tests in the round sphere whose summed second variation is minus the umbilicity defect. This proves spherical CMC rigidity, including the equator. Product tori supply n+2 independent volume-preserving negative directions and one additional constant direction. The full hyperbolic rigidity theorem and projective-space classifications remain outside this batch.
