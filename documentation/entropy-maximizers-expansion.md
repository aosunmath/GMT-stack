## Convergence and global parameters

The measure-level section proves lower semicontinuity of Gaussian area and entropy, uniform polynomial Gaussian tails under an entropy bound, convergence of Gaussian integrals uniformly on compact center/scale sets, the Gaussian second-moment estimate, and invariance of entropy under Euclidean products. Static small-scale Gaussian limits are proved for proper embedded hypersurfaces with polynomial area growth.

Two parallel planes, one moving to infinity, give local convergence to a single plane while their entropy remains two. This shows why fixed-parameter Gaussian convergence does not imply continuity of entropy, even under uniform entropy bounds. The example distinguishes lower semicontinuity from an invalid upper-semicontinuity assertion.

## Shrinker maximizers

Shifted weighted integration by parts yields the exact negative-square derivative along `(c_s,t_s)=(s y,1+a s²)`. This proves that a normalized shrinker attains its entropy at center zero and scale one. The initially assumed polynomial area-growth exponent then improves to the uniform dimension-n ball estimate.

A separate geometric argument identifies constant tangent vectors with Euclidean translation factors. A proper smooth embedded minimal shrinker is proved to be a plane by homotheties and smoothness at the origin. The equality analysis identifies every maximizing parameter: for a nonplanar shrinker, the scale is one and its center lies in the constant tangent subspace; a plane permits every positive scale. When this subspace is zero, the center/scale Hessian is negative definite. A quadratic local deficit and the parabolic parameter paths give a uniform global gap outside each neighborhood of the normalized parameters.

## From F-instability to entropy decrease

The perturbation section derives an exact Gaussian scale identity and its lower differential bound. Compact shape support bounds the changed area and curvature while permitting unbounded curvature on the unchanged noncompact part. Gaussian second moments then give a scale inequality uniform on bounded center sets. Far centers, large scales, and small scales are excluded separately; no unproved interchange of a supremum with a limit is used.

For an F-negative shape direction on a shrinker without a Euclidean line factor, the joint shape/center/scale Hessian is negative definite. The local quadratic deficit, parameter exclusion, and compact-parameter continuity prove an actual entropy decrease for every sufficiently small nonzero graph amplitude. A corollary covers closed embedded shrinkers with sign-changing mean curvature, using the explicitly external compact spectral input already recorded in the previous expansion. The theorem is not applied to cylinders, and no general mean-convex classification or generic-flow conclusion is asserted.
