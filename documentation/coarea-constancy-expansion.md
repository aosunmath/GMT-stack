## Integration on rectifiable sets

The weighted area formula is derived from the existing Euclidean theorem. A separate definition explains tangential differentiation on rectifiable sets without assuming ambient differentiability along the set. The rectifiable coarea and fiber-rectifiability theorem remains external; its weighted version and a radial-slice selection estimate have local proofs. Examples distinguish ambient exceptional sets from tangential differentiability and identify Fubini as the projection case.

## BV coarea and calculus

The proof chain includes signed layer cake, almost-everywhere level-set convergence, smooth coarea, whole-space BV coarea, and localization to a measure identity with Borel weights. The proof records measurability of perimeter as a function of the level and the localization through balls with no derivative mass on their boundary.

Further local proofs establish Lipschitz composition, exact variation of truncations, positive/negative variation splitting, the lattice inequality, perimeter submodularity, and smooth approximation of bounded finite-perimeter sets. The latter selects regular levels with a quantitative symmetric-difference bound. Examples show how jump height enters perimeter and why convergence at every fixed level is false even under strict BV convergence. Negative superlevel sets may have infinite volume; the definition of finite perimeter does not impose integrability of their characteristic functions on the whole space.

## Smooth support and multiplicity

A mollification lemma proves that a vanishing distributional gradient forces constancy. The manifold version includes the coordinate volume density explicitly. Stationarity then identifies tangent planes and forces one integer multiplicity on each connected smooth support, with minimality when that integer is positive. Separate computations describe a jump in multiplicity and a smoothly varying positive weight. The latter can produce tangential generalized mean curvature for a nonintegral rectifiable varifold.
