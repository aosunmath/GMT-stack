## Mathematical coverage

- Measures: dense atoms, concentration to a Dirac mass, escape of mass, discontinuity on Borel sets, oscillatory averaging, construction of the Cantor probability measure, ball bounds, Hausdorff dimension, its singular BV distribution function, and diffuse approximations.
- Minimal surfaces: the conformal harmonic criterion and curvature formula; complete catenoids and helicoids; total curvature; an explicit unstable direction for the catenoid; stable graphical halves; higher-dimensional catenoid height; the associate family's period obstruction; and Enneper's proper complete immersion with an explicit crossing.
- Solitons: scale and speed conventions, Euclidean products, plane/sphere/cylinder comparisons, translating graph equations, the full speed family of grim reapers, tilted grim-reaper cylinders, height identities, weighted area, and a paraboloid counterexample.
- Bowl construction: a contraction argument at the singular radial endpoint, global slope barriers, properness and completeness, rotational uniqueness, quadratic growth, the inverse-radius slope correction, a logarithmic height term with its additive constant, and speed scaling.
- Expanders: a global even convex graphical family, bounded slope, asymptotic rays, uniform convergence of the expanding flow to a corner, and total and maximum curvature. No classification by prescribed angle or weak-flow uniqueness is claimed.
- Ancient oval: direct verification from its implicit defining function, changing curvature ratio, enclosed area, smooth shrinking-circle limit, and two local grim-reaper limits backward in time. Ancient does not imply self-similar.

## Source evidence and conventions

Previously supplied sources checked here include Chodosh's March 15, 2025 minimal-surface notes, Sections 13--15, pp. 35--45; Simon's 2017/2018 GMT edition, Chapter 1, Sections 1--2; and White's 2015 flow notes recorded by Chodosh, Section 1.1, pp. 2--3. Four additional primary references are recorded by exact SHA-256, version, and URL:

1. Hoffman--Ilmanen--Martín--White, *Notes on Translating Solitons for Mean Curvature Flow*, author-hosted January 15, 2019 manuscript, 20 pages: Example 1.6 and Sections 3--4, pp. 4, 7--8, 10.
2. Clutterbuck--Schnürer--Schulze, *Stability of translating solutions to mean curvature flow*, author-hosted September 2005 manuscript, 12 pages, later published in 2007: Section 2, pp. 3--5. The dynamical stability theorem is not imported.
3. Halldorsson, *Self-similar solutions to the curve shortening flow*, arXiv:1007.1617v2, December 14, 2012, 25 pages: Section 6, pp. 14--15. We construct graphical expanders directly rather than assert the full classification.
4. Broadbridge--Vassiliou, *The Role of Symmetry and Separation in Surface Evolution and Curve Shortening*, SIGMA 7 (2011), 052: Section 3.1, pp. 6--7, equation (14).

All flow calculations use the mean curvature vector as the trace, scalar H = div(nu), and vector H = -H nu. Upward translators have velocity e_(n+1), whereas the Hoffman--Ilmanen--Martín--White source primarily uses downward translation. The expander coefficient is 1/2. The bowl's height asymptotic retains its additive constant; the ancient oval's backward error is rederived directly rather than copying an incorrect smaller error from a source.

Elementary integration, ODE continuation and uniqueness, the contraction principle, basic holomorphic-function closure, the coframe curvature identity, and the turning-angle and implicit-function theorems remain background tools. No independent human review or formal verification is claimed. The new exposition and proofs are written for this project; source PDFs and extracted full text are not exported.
