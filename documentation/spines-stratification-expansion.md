## Qualitative stratification

The covering proof has separate entries for one-sided approximation, refinement with centers retained in the set, Hausdorff-measure decay, density concentration near a tangent spine, and countable decomposition by scale and density. It proves the existing Hausdorff-dimension bound without assuming tangent-cone uniqueness. The zero stratum is additionally shown to be countable; local finiteness of the entire stratum is not asserted. Flat singular points are identified with the top stratum difference and have integer density at least two. The singular set below density two therefore lies in the next stratum.

## Explicit models

The ray-cone section computes the balance condition and proves that every stationary integral one-dimensional cone is a finite weighted ray cone. Its proof uses polar disintegration and density to force finite integer angular atoms. Products give half-integer densities for cones whose spine has codimension one. Examples include the 120-degree triple junction and its products; two orthogonal planes in four dimensions with disjoint smooth great-circle links; and a Scherk graph combined with its tangent plane, giving a flat singular point with a unique double-plane tangent cone. This last example involves crossing graphs and does not assert one-sided contact.
