7.5 Elliptic estimates for weak solutions57735E602BFC

57735E602BFC

The estimates below separate three levels of information: energy control, continuity with merely measurable coefficients, and higher regularity with continuous coefficients. They supply the analytic inputs used in the minimal graph and Jacobi-field sections. All balls in this section are Euclidean balls in $\mathbb R^ n$, with $n\geq 2$.

Definition 7.5.1 (Sobolev spaces and weak derivatives).848E8C69703F For an open $U\subset \mathbb R^ n$, $1\leq p<\infty $, and integer $k\geq 0$, the space $W^{k,p}(U)$ consists of functions whose distributional derivatives $D^\beta u$, $|\beta |\leq k$, belong to $L^ p(U)$. Its norm is $\sum _{|\beta |\leq k}\| D^\beta u\| _{L^ p(U)}$. A first weak derivative is characterized by $\int uD_ i\phi =-\int D_ i u\, \phi $ for all $\phi \in C_ c^\infty (U)$. We write $H^1=W^{1,2}$, and $W^{1,p}_0(U)$ for the closure of $C_ c^\infty (U)$ in $W^{1,p}(U)$. The subscript $\mathrm{loc}$ requires the corresponding property on every relatively compact subdomain. See [SimonPDE], Chapter 5.

Definition 7.5.2 (Uniform ellipticity and weak divergence equations).5454001B58EE Let $A:U\to \mathbb R^{n\times n}$ be measurable and symmetric. We assume that constants $0<\lambda \leq \Lambda <\infty $ satisfy

\[ \lambda |\xi |^2\leq A(x)\xi \cdot \xi \leq \Lambda |\xi |^2 \quad \text{for almost every }x\in U,\ \xi \in \mathbb R^ n. \]

A function $v\in H^1_{\mathrm{loc}}(U)$ solves $\operatorname {div}(A Dv)=0$ weakly if $\int A Dv\cdot D\phi =0$ for all $\phi \in C_ c^\infty (U)$. A weak subsolution of this equation satisfies $\int A Dv\cdot D\phi \leq 0$ for nonnegative test functions. No derivatives or continuity of $A$ are assumed. This is the homogeneous, symmetric, zero-lower-order-term case of [SimonPDE], Chapters 6 and 17.

Definition 7.5.3 (Hölder seminorms).9B57A69E7130 For $0<\alpha <1$, define

\[ [f]_{\alpha ;U}=\sup _{x,y\in U,\ x\ne y}\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|}{|x-y|^\alpha }. \]

The space $C^{k,\alpha }(U)$ consists of $C^ k$ functions with finite norm $\sum _{|\beta |\leq k}\| D^\beta f\| _{C^0(U)}+\sum _{|\beta |=k}[D^\beta f]_{\alpha ;U}$. Local versions are defined on relatively compact subdomains. On a ball of radius $R$, the scale-invariant combination for the $k$th derivative is $R^ k\| D^ kf\| _\infty +R^{k+\alpha }[D^ kf]_\alpha $. See [SimonPDE], Chapter 12.

Lemma 7.5.4 (Caccioppoli energy estimate).494C2D3530AB If $v$ solves the equation in Definition 7.5.2, $\eta \in C_ c^1(U)$, and $c\in \mathbb R$, then

\[ \int \eta ^2|Dv|^2\leq 4(\Lambda /\lambda )^2\int |D\eta |^2|v-c|^2. \]

In particular, if $B_ R\subset U$ and $0<r<R$, then $\int _{B_ r}|Dv|^2\leq C(R-r)^{-2}\int _{B_ R}|v-c|^2$, with $C$ depending only on $\Lambda /\lambda $ and the cutoff choice.

Proof

By density we may test with $\eta ^2(v-c)$. Ellipticity and the operator-norm bound $|A|\leq \Lambda $ give

\[ \lambda \int \eta ^2|Dv|^2 \leq 2\Lambda \int |\eta ||v-c||Dv||D\eta | \leq \frac\lambda 2\int \eta ^2|Dv|^2+ \frac{2\Lambda ^2}{\lambda }\int |v-c|^2|D\eta |^2. \]

Absorb the first term. A cutoff equal to one on $B_ r$ and with $|D\eta |\leq C/(R-r)$ gives the last assertion. Compare the energy-test method in [SimonPDE], Chapter 17. $\square$

Theorem 7.5.5 (Local boundedness of weak solutions).C56302366B3D Let $v\in H^1(B_{2R}(a))$ solve the equation in Definition 7.5.2. Then

\[ \operatorname *{ess\, sup}_{B_ R(a)}|v| \leq C\left(R^{-n}\int _{B_{2R}(a)}|v|^2\right)^{1/2}, \]

where $C=C(n,\Lambda /\lambda )$. This is [SimonPDE], Chapter 18, Theorem 2, p. 212, with zero forcing and lower-order terms. The iteration proof remains external.

Theorem 7.5.6 (Interior Harnack inequality).4D92018C184B If $v\in H^1(B_{2R}(a))$ is nonnegative and solves the equation in Definition 7.5.2, then

\[ \operatorname *{ess\, sup}_{B_ R(a)}v\leq C\operatorname *{ess\, inf}_{B_ R(a)}v, \qquad C=C(n,\Lambda /\lambda ). \]

The sign assumption is essential, and the estimate is on a smaller ball. This is the zero-lower-order-term specialization of [SimonPDE], Chapter 18, Theorem 1, p. 211. The proof remains external.

Corollary 7.5.7 (Hölder continuity from oscillation decay).482FCE0EEBCC There are $\alpha =\alpha (n,\Lambda /\lambda )\in (0,1)$ and $C=C(n,\Lambda /\lambda )$ such that every weak solution on $B_{2R}(a)$ has a $C^{0,\alpha }$ representative on $B_ R(a)$ and

\[ \| v\| _{C^0(B_ R(a))}+R^\alpha [v]_{\alpha ;B_ R(a)} \leq C\left(R^{-n}\int _{B_{2R}(a)}|v|^2\right)^{1/2}. \]
Proof

Local boundedness first bounds $v$ on $B_{3R/2}(a)$. On any ball $B_{2s}$ compactly contained there, let $M$ and $m$ be its essential supremum and infimum. Apply Harnack to $M-v$ and $v-m$. Adding the resulting inequalities gives

\[ \operatorname *{ess\, osc}_{B_ s}v\leq \frac{C_ H-1}{C_ H+1}\operatorname *{ess\, osc}_{B_{2s}}v, \]

where we may take $C_ H>1$. Iteration on concentric dyadic balls gives a bound $C(s/R)^\alpha $ for the oscillation, choosing a smaller exponent if necessary to have $\alpha <1$. The essential oscillations therefore define a unique continuous representative. Balls centered in $B_ R(a)$ of radius at most $R/4$, followed by the bound for pairs farther apart, give the stated seminorm estimate. This is the oscillation mechanism of De Giorgi–Nash–Moser theory; see [SimonPDE], Chapters 17–18. $\square$

Theorem 7.5.8 (Interior Schauder regularity).275ADD902409 Let $0<\alpha <1$, and let $a^{ij}=a^{ji}$, $b^ i$, and $c$ belong to $C^{0,\alpha }(B_1)$, with $\lambda I\leq (a^{ij})\leq \Lambda I$ and the sum of their $C^{0,\alpha }$ norms at most $K$. If $u\in C^2(B_1)\cap C^0(\overline{B_1})$ solves

\[ a^{ij}D_{ij}u+b^ iD_ i u+cu=f,\qquad f\in C^{0,\alpha }(B_1), \]

then $u\in C^{2,\alpha }(B_{1/2})$ and

\[ \| u\| _{C^{2,\alpha }(B_{1/2})}\leq C\bigl (\| u\| _{C^0(B_1)}+\| f\| _{C^{0,\alpha }(B_1)}\bigr ), \]

with $C=C(n,\alpha ,\lambda ,\Lambda ,K)$. The corresponding higher interior estimates hold when the coefficients and forcing have higher Hölder regularity. No sign assumption on $c$ is needed for this interior estimate. Sources: [SimonPDE], Chapter 12; [ChMin], Appendix A, equation (A.2), printed p. 86. The proof remains external.

Theorem 7.5.9 (Interior Sobolev estimate for the Laplacian).B926EC268DB9 Let $1<p<\infty $ and $u\in W^{2,p}(B_1)$. Then

\[ \| u\| _{W^{2,p}(B_{1/2})}\leq C(n,p) \bigl (\| u\| _{L^ p(B_1)}+\| \Delta u\| _{L^ p(B_1)}\bigr ). \]

This is the $k=0$ case of [ChMin], Appendix A, equation (A.1), printed p. 86. It is an a priori estimate in the stated Sobolev class; neither endpoint $p=1,\infty $ is included. The proof remains external.
