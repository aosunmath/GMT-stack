2.1 Radon measures2AD208A5263B

2AD208A5263B

Weak convergence is useful because geometric limits need not remain smooth. We first specify the measures and the topology used in the later chapters. The reference is [Sim18], Chapter 1, Sections 1 and 5.

Definition 2.1.1 (Radon measure).5B8B37A79F3A A nonnegative Borel measure $\mu $ on an open set $U\subset \mathbb {R}^ n$ is a Radon measure if it is finite on compact subsets of $U$ and, for every Borel set $A\subset U$,

\[ \mu (A)=\inf \{ \mu (O):A\subset O\subset U,\ O\text{ open}\} =\sup \{ \mu (K):K\subset A,\ K\text{ compact}\} . \]

We use its completion when integrating measurable functions. Its support consists of the points $x\in U$ for which every neighborhood has positive measure. Local finiteness does not require $\mu (U)<\infty $. See [Sim18], Chapter 1, Section 5, Definition 5.6, p. 31, for the corresponding outer-measure formulation.

Definition 2.1.2 (Weak convergence of measures).D58956DB1AAB Radon measures $\mu _ j$ converge weakly in $U$ to a Radon measure $\mu $, written $\mu _ j\rightharpoonup \mu $, if

\[ \lim _{j\to \infty }\int _ U\varphi \, d\mu _ j=\int _ U\varphi \, d\mu \qquad \text{for every }\varphi \in C_ c(U). \]

This is also called vague convergence. Testing only compactly supported functions allows mass to escape toward infinity or the boundary of $U$. See [Sim18], Chapter 1, Section 5.

Theorem 2.1.3 (Local compactness of Radon measures).EC3F5C88A198 Let $\mu _ j$ be nonnegative Radon measures on $U$ and suppose that

\[ \sup _ j\mu _ j(K)<\infty \qquad \text{for every compact }K\subset U. \]

There are a subsequence $\mu _{j_ k}$ and a Radon measure $\mu $ on $U$ such that $\mu _{j_ k}\rightharpoonup \mu $ in $U$.

Cited theorem; proof to be added. See [Sim18], Chapter 1, Section 5, Theorem 5.15, pp. 38–39. The construction uses a diagonal subsequence on compact subsets and the Riesz representation theorem, Theorem 5.12 in that section.

Proposition 2.1.4 (Lower semicontinuity on open sets).E6E21F29D7CA If $\mu _ j\rightharpoonup \mu $ in $U$, then

\[ \mu (O)\leq \liminf _{j\to \infty }\mu _ j(O) \qquad \text{for every open }O\subset U. \]

This is the open-set inequality accompanying weak convergence in [Sim18], Chapter 1, Section 5.

Proof

Fix $0\leq \varphi \leq 1$ with $\varphi \in C_ c(O)$. Weak convergence gives

\[ \int \varphi \, d\mu =\lim _ j\int \varphi \, d\mu _ j\leq \liminf _ j\mu _ j(O). \]

For every compact $K\subset O$ we can choose such a function with $\varphi =1$ on $K$. Taking the supremum over $K$ and using inner regularity proves the assertion, including the case $\mu (O)=\infty $. $\square$
