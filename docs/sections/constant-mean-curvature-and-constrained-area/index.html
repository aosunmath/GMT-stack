11.1 Constant mean curvature and constrained areaCA1D0C381103

CA1D0C381103

Finite-perimeter sets retain the enclosed region as well as its boundary. This is essential for the area-minus-volume functional, and connects the BV foundations with the CMC min-max construction of Zhou–Zhu [ZZCMC].

Definition 11.1.1 (The area-minus-volume functional).858933BEFE56 For a finite-perimeter set $\Omega $ in a smooth closed $(M^{n+1},g)$ and a prescribed number $c$, define

\[ \mathcal A^ c(\Omega )=P(\Omega ;M)-c\operatorname {Vol}(\Omega ). \]

On a smooth boundary $\Sigma =\partial \Omega $ we use the outward unit normal and $H=\operatorname {div}_\Sigma \nu $. A CMC hypersurface has constant scalar $H$. Prescribing $c$ and finding a critical point of $\mathcal A^ c$ is a different problem from minimizing perimeter at a fixed volume. See [ZZCMC], Introduction and Section 1.

Proposition 11.1.2 (The smooth CMC Euler-Lagrange equation).DEFFDC26B8E9 For a smooth compact region $\Omega \subset M$ with smooth boundary, a variation with boundary velocity $f\nu $ satisfies

\[ \delta \mathcal A^ c(f\nu )=\int _{\partial \Omega }(H-c)f\, dA. \]

It is critical under all smooth variations if and only if $H=c$ everywhere.

Proof. Proposition 7.1.2 gives the area derivative $\int Hf$. The divergence theorem applied to an extension $X$ of $f\nu $ gives the volume derivative $\int _\Omega \operatorname {div}_ M X=\int _{\partial \Omega }f$. Subtract $c$ times this derivative. Every smooth $f$ extends to a variation, so vanishing for all $f$ is equivalent to $H-c=0$. $\square$

Definition 11.1.3 (Almost embedded hypersurfaces).D37CE32A4FFA An almost embedded hypersurface is a smooth immersion whose local sheets are embedded and, when they meet, lie on one side of each other: tangential touching is allowed but transverse crossing is excluded. This is the notion used in [ZZCMC], following Theorem 0.1. It is weaker than embeddedness. The multiplicity of a parametrized sheet and the density at a touching point should not be confused.

Theorem 11.1.4 (Prescribed positive constant mean curvature).BEF02630A1D3 Let $M^{n+1}$ be a smooth closed connected Riemannian manifold with $2\leq n\leq 6$. For each $c>0$ there exists a nonempty smooth closed almost embedded hypersurface with constant mean curvature $c$, in the boundary orientation of the Zhou–Zhu construction. The min-max hypersurface has sheet multiplicity one. Source: [ZZCMC], Theorem 0.1 and Remark 0.2, in the supplied arXiv v2 of August 11, 2017. Proof external. The conclusion permits touching; it does not assert global embeddedness or connectedness.
