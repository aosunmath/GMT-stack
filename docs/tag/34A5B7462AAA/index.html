Proposition 3.14.3 (A coarse Lipschitz slicing estimate)34A5B7462AAA

Proposition 3.14.3 (A coarse Lipschitz slicing estimate).34A5B7462AAA Let $1\leq k<m\leq N$, let $f:\mathbb R^ N\to \mathbb R^ k$ have Lipschitz constant $L$, and let $A$ be $\mathcal H^ m$-measurable with finite measure. Then

\[ \int _{\mathbb R^ k}\mathcal H^{m-k}(A\cap f^{-1}(y))\, dy \leq \frac{2^ m\omega _ k\omega _{m-k}}{\omega _ m}L^ k\mathcal H^ m(A). \]

The constant is not sharp. This is a diameter-cover version of [Sim18], Chapter 2, Theorem 1.10, pp. 49–51; it is sufficient for the critical-set argument below.

Proof

Put $d=m-k>0$. Choose diameter covers $E_{li}$ of $A$ with positive diameters $s_{li}<1/l$ and normalized $m$-cost at most $\mathcal H^ m(A)+1/l$. Countably many zero-diameter members have zero $d$-measure in every fiber and can be discarded. For each remaining member meeting $A$, select $x_{li}\in A\cap E_{li}$ and replace it by the closed ball $Q_{li}=\overline B_{s_{li}}(x_{li})$. These balls still cover $A$ apart from the harmless countable set. Their images under $f$ are compact and lie in target balls of radius $Ls_{li}$. The measurable function

\[ F_ l(y)=\sum _ i\omega _ d s_{li}^ d\chi _{f(Q_{li})}(y) \]

therefore satisfies

\[ \int F_ l\, dy\leq \omega _ d\omega _ kL^ k\sum _ i s_{li}^{m} \leq \frac{2^ m\omega _ d\omega _ k}{\omega _ m}L^ k \bigl (\mathcal H^ m(A)+1/l\bigr ). \]

The balls contributing to $F_ l(y)$ cover the fiber and have diameter less than $2/l$, so its Hausdorff content at that scale is at most $F_ l(y)$. Hence the fiber measure is bounded by $\liminf _ lF_ l(y)$. It is measurable by Lemma 3.14.2. Fatou's lemma gives the stated integral bound. The factor $2^ m$ records the passage from diameter sets to enclosing balls; no sharp isodiametric inequality is being asserted. For $L=0$, every image is a point of zero target Lebesgue measure and the same argument gives zero. $\square$
