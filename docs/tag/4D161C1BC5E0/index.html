Proposition 13.2.6 (The equation away from contact)4D161C1BC5E0

Proposition 13.2.6 (The equation away from contact).4D161C1BC5E0 Suppose additionally that $u$ and $\psi $ have continuous representatives. On the open set $O=\{ u>\psi \} $,

\[ -\Delta u=f, \]

and the reaction measure vanishes. Consequently its support in $U$ is contained in the contact set $\{ u=\psi \} $.

Proof

A smooth test function compactly supported in $O$ has support on which $u-\psi $ has a positive minimum. Thus both $u+t\phi $ and $u-t\phi $ are admissible for sufficiently small $t>0$. Their variational inequalities give equality in the weak equation. Since the reaction vanishes on every such test function, it vanishes as a measure on $O$. The contact set is relatively closed by continuity. Compare [Fig18], Corollary 3.4. Without a continuous representative, a statement involving pointwise contact sets requires additional care. $\square$
