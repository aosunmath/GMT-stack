Definition 8.1.2 (A fixed homotopy-class width)073350371C3C

Definition 8.1.2 (A fixed homotopy-class width).073350371C3C Let $X$ be a finite simplicial complex and $\Phi :X\to \mathcal Z_ n^0(M;\mathbb Z_2)$ be $\mathbf F$-continuous. Let $\Pi $ contain all $\mathbf F$-continuous maps from $X$ that are homotopic to $\Phi $ in the flat topology. Its width is

\[ \mathbf L(\Pi )=\inf _{\Psi \in \Pi }\sup _{x\in X}\mathbf M(\Psi (x)). \]

A minimizing sequence $\Phi _ i\in \Pi $ has maximal masses converging to $\mathbf L(\Pi )$. Its critical set consists of varifold limits of slices $|\Phi _{i_ j}(x_ j)|$ whose masses converge to $\mathbf L(\Pi )$. This is the convention of [MNIndex], Section 1. The discrete homotopy formulation in [Pitts81], Section 4.1, requires a separate interpolation argument to compare the constructions.
