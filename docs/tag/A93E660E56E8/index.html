Proposition 14.6.6 (One-sided limits of localized mass)A93E660E56E8

Proposition 14.6.6 (One-sided limits of localized mass).A93E660E56E8 For $\eta \in C_ c^1(U)$ and a compact time interval $[a,b]\subset I$, there is $C<\infty $ such that

\[ t\longmapsto \mu _ t(\eta ^2)-Ct \]

is nonincreasing on $[a,b]$. Consequently its left and right limits exist at interior times, and

\[ \lim _{s\uparrow t}\mu _ s(\eta ^2)\geq \mu _ t(\eta ^2) \geq \lim _{s\downarrow t}\mu _ s(\eta ^2). \]

These inequalities allow downward jumps. They are not continuity in time. Compare [WhiMCF15], Theorems 9.6–9.7.

Proof

Complete the square in the time-independent Brakke integrand:

\[ -\eta ^2|\vec H|^2+2\eta \nabla \eta \cdot \vec H =-|\eta \vec H-\nabla \eta |^2+|\nabla \eta |^2\leq |\nabla \eta |^2. \]

Let $M$ bound $\mu _ t(\operatorname {spt}\eta )$ on the interval, and take $C=M\| D\eta \| _\infty ^2$. The integrated inequality between arbitrary times gives the monotonicity. The one-sided conclusions follow from elementary properties of a monotone real function. $\square$
