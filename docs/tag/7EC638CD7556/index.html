Lemma 16.2.1 (A conformal harmonic immersion is minimal)7EC638CD7556

Lemma 16.2.1 (A conformal harmonic immersion is minimal).7EC638CD7556 Let $X:U\subset \mathbb R^2\to \mathbb R^ N$ be a smooth immersion with $|X_ u|^2=|X_ v|^2=\lambda ^2>0$ and $\langle X_ u,X_ v\rangle =0$. Then

\[ \vec H=\lambda ^{-2}(X_{uu}+X_{vv}). \]

Consequently $X$ is minimal exactly when all its coordinate functions are harmonic.

Proof

The induced metric is $g=\lambda ^2(du^2+dv^2)$. Its Laplace–Beltrami operator is $\Delta _ g=\lambda ^{-2}(\partial _ u^2+\partial _ v^2)$, by the coordinate divergence formula. For an immersion, tracing the normal second derivative gives $\Delta _ gX=\vec H$: the tangential Christoffel terms cancel when taking the covariant Hessian. Substitution proves the formula. This is [ChMin], Lemma 15.1. Our mean curvature vector is the trace, with no factor of one half. $\square$
