Remark 15.3.3 (Existence and the exact minimum were separate achievements)F2980A8D4351

Remark 15.3.3 (Existence and the exact minimum were separate achievements).F2980A8D4351 Simon's 1993 paper [SimW93], pp. 281–283, established a smooth embedded torus minimizing Willmore energy among embedded tori in each Euclidean ambient dimension at least three. This supplied an optimizer without identifying its sharp energy in three-space. The 2014 paper of Marques–Neves [MNWill], Theorem A, p. 685, determined the positive-genus bound in $S^3$ and its equality case through min-max theory. Their Corollary D, p. 687, realizes the Clifford torus through a five-parameter min-max family. The local square identities developed here illuminate Simon's estimates; neither his full existence proof nor the five-parameter min-max proof has been replaced by those local calculations.
