Proposition 12.11.1 (An energy identity for every closed critical point)1605E7AF7DC3

Proposition 12.11.1 (An energy identity for every closed critical point).1605E7AF7DC3 A smooth solution of $\varepsilon ^2\Delta _ g u=u^3-u$ satisfies

\[ \varepsilon ^2\int _ M|\nabla u|^2=\int _ M(u^2-u^4),\qquad E_\varepsilon (u;M)=\frac{\operatorname {Vol}(M)-\int _ Mu^4}{4\varepsilon }. \]

In particular $0\leq E_\varepsilon (u;M)\leq \operatorname {Vol}(M)/(4\varepsilon )$ and $\int _ M|\nabla u|^2\leq \operatorname {Vol}(M)/(4\varepsilon ^2)$. Equality in the energy upper bound occurs precisely for $u=0$.

Proof

Multiply the equation by $u$ and integrate by parts on the closed manifold. This gives the first identity. Substitute its gradient term into $E_\varepsilon =\varepsilon \int |\nabla u|^2/2+(4\varepsilon )^{-1}\int (1-2u^2+u^4)$; the $u^2$ terms cancel and give the second. Nonnegativity follows from the original energy definition. Proposition 12.2.3 gives $|u|\leq 1$, and $u^2-u^4\leq 1/4$, proving the gradient bound. The energy upper bound follows from $u^4\geq 0$, and equality forces $u=0$ by continuity. The identities concern critical points, not arbitrary functions with the same pointwise bound. $\square$
