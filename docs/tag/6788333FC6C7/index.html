Theorem 14.23.5 (Interior curvature in a fixed base ball)6788333FC6C7

Theorem 14.23.5 (Interior curvature in a fixed base ball).6788333FC6C7 Suppose a smooth graphical flow is defined on a neighborhood of $\overline B_ R(y_0)\times [0,T]$ and $1\leq v\leq V$ there. For $0\leq \theta <1$ and $0<t\leq T$,

\[ \sup _{|x-y_0|\leq \theta R}|A(x,t)|^2 \leq \frac{C(n)V^4}{(1-\theta ^2)^2} \left(\frac1t+\frac1{R^2}\right). \]

No bound on the initial curvature occurs in this estimate. This is [EH91], Theorem 3.1 and Corollary 3.2(ii), pp. 554–557.

Proof

Use $k=(2V^2)^{-1}$ and $f,b$ from Proposition 14.23.4. Let $\zeta =R^2-|x-y_0|^2$, and maximize $Z=\tau \zeta ^2 f$ over the closed base ball and $0\leq \tau \leq t$. It is zero at initial time and on the lateral boundary; all factors are bounded there by smoothness. If its maximum is positive, it occurs at an interior spatial point and a time $\tau >0$. There $\nabla f=-2f\nabla \zeta /\zeta $. Writing $D=\mathcal P+b\cdot \nabla $, the product rule at this point gives

\[ 0\leq DZ\leq \zeta ^2 f-2k\tau \zeta ^2f^2 +2\tau \zeta f\mathcal P\zeta +6\tau f|\nabla \zeta |^2 +2\tau \zeta f\langle b,\nabla \zeta \rangle . \]

The left inequality remains true in graph coordinates since the total tangential drift vanishes at the maximum. Divide by $\tau f$. The drift bound and Young's inequality give

\[ 2\zeta |b||\nabla \zeta | \leq k\zeta ^2f+\frac8k|\nabla \zeta |^2. \]

Lemma 14.23.1 therefore yields

\[ k\zeta ^2 f\leq \frac{\zeta ^2}{\tau } +4nR^2+4(6+8/k)R^2. \]

Multiplying by $\tau /k$ bounds the maximum of $Z$ by $R^4/k+(4n+24+32/k)tR^2/k$. At the requested point $\zeta \geq (1-\theta ^2)R^2$, and $a\leq f$ because $\phi \geq 1$. Divide by $t\zeta ^2$ and use $k^{-1}=2V^2$, $V\geq 1$. This proves the asserted constant form. The argument is an interior estimate for an existing smooth flow, not a continuation theorem. $\square$
