Corollary 4.8.3 (Stieltjes integration by parts with traces)87F88951FED0

Corollary 4.8.3 (Stieltjes integration by parts with traces).87F88951FED0 For $u,v\in BV(a,b)$,

\[ \int _{(a,b)}\overline u\, dDv+\int _{(a,b)}\overline v\, dDu =u(b-)v(b-)-u(a+)v(a+). \]

In particular, for $\phi \in C^1([a,b])$,

\[ \int _{(a,b)}\phi \, dDu+\int _ a^ bu\phi '\, dx =u(b-)\phi (b)-u(a+)\phi (a). \]
Proof

The total mass of $Dw$ for an interval BV function is $w(b-)-w(a+)$ by its cumulative representation. Apply this to $w=uv$ in Proposition 4.8.2. The bounded midpoint representatives are Borel, so both integrals against finite signed measures are well defined. For smooth $\phi $, its two one-sided representatives agree, $D\phi =\phi '\mathcal L^1$, and $\overline u=u$ almost everywhere for Lebesgue measure. This gives the second formula with the stated signs. $\square$
