Proposition 11.5.7 (Index and nullity for a constant potential)8A971915C319

Proposition 11.5.7 (Index and nullity for a constant potential).8A971915C319 Under the hypotheses of Proposition 11.5.6, let the eigenvalues of $-\Delta $ be $0=\lambda _0<\lambda _1\leq \lambda _2\leq \cdots $, counted with multiplicity. The constrained index is the number of $\lambda _ j<q$ with $j\geq 1$. Its constrained nullspace is the span of the eigenfunctions with $\lambda _ j=q$, $j\geq 1$. If $q>0$, the unrestricted index is the constrained index plus one.

Proof

Use the external compact spectral theorem to expand $f=\sum _ j a_ j\phi _ j$ in an orthonormal Laplace eigenbasis, with $\phi _0$ constant. The second-variation form is diagonal:

\[ Q(f,f)=\sum _ j(\lambda _ j-q)a_ j^2. \]

For smooth $f$ this follows from integration by parts and the spectral expansion of the Dirichlet energy. Zero mean removes exactly $a_0$. The negative eigenspace gives a negative subspace of the asserted dimension; any larger subspace intersects its orthogonal complement and hence contains a vector with nonnegative quadratic form. Proposition 11.5.2 gives $Jf=0$ in the constant-potential case, so the constrained nullspace consists exactly of the eigenmodes with eigenvalue $q$. Finally the constant mode is negative precisely when $q>0$. The discreteness and divergence of the Laplace spectrum ensure finiteness of these counts. $\square$
