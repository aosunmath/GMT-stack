Theorem 8.7.2 (Pull-tight preserves the width and decreases the critical set)CD1277EA8D53

Theorem 8.7.2 (Pull-tight preserves the width and decreases the critical set).CD1277EA8D53 Let $M^{n+1}$ be smooth, closed and connected, $n\geq 1$, and let $\Phi _ i\in \Pi $ be a minimizing sequence in the mod-two boundary-cycle setting of Definition 8.1.2, with finite width $L$. There is a minimizing sequence $\Psi _ i\in \Pi $ such that

\[ \mathcal C(\Psi _ i)\subset \mathcal C(\Phi _ i)\cap \{ V:\delta V=0\} . \]

Moreover $\Psi _ i$ may be chosen uniformly as close to $\Phi _ i$ in $\mathbf F$ as prescribed. This is the continuous pull-tight conclusion of [MNIndex], Section 2.8; the discrete version discussed in [LiAP], Section 4.2, additionally requires interpolation.

Proof

Choose $C>\sup _{i,x}\mathbf M(\Phi _ i(x))$, finite because the maximal masses converge. Use one fixed homotopy from Theorem 8.6.8 and set $\Psi _ i(x)=H(1,\Phi _ i(x))$. The composition is $\mathbf F$-continuous and the homotopy $H(s,\Phi _ i(x))$ is flat-continuous, so $\Psi _ i\in \Pi $. By the definition of the width and mass decrease,

\[ L\leq \max _ x\mathbf M(\Psi _ i(x))\leq \max _ x\mathbf M(\Phi _ i(x))\longrightarrow L. \]

Let $W$ be critical for the new sequence, represented by $|\Psi _{i_ j}(x_ j)|\to W$ with masses tending to $L$. Pass to a subsequence with $V_ j=|\Phi _{i_ j}(x_ j)|\to V$ in $K_ C$. The old masses lie between the new masses and the old maximal masses, so they also tend to $L$. Thus $V\in \mathcal C(\Phi _ i)$ and the mass losses tend to zero. Corollary 8.6.6 gives $V\in S_ C$. Continuity of the varifold deformation then gives

\[ W=\lim _ j\mathscr H(1,V_ j)=\mathscr H(1,V)=V. \]

This proves both stationarity and inclusion in the original critical set. The prescribed closeness follows from the same homotopy, without letting its positive defect vary with $i$. $\square$
