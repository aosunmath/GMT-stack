Proposition 5.2.3 (Boundary is continuous in the flat norm)FEAB4217D841

Proposition 5.2.3 (Boundary is continuous in the flat norm).FEAB4217D841 For every integer polyhedral chain $P$,

\[ \mathcal{F}(P)\leq \mathbf{M}(P),\qquad \mathcal{F}(\partial P)\leq \mathcal{F}(P). \]

The boundary therefore extends continuously to the completions in Definition 5.2.2. See [WhiCho14], Section 2.3, pp. 6–8.

Proof

Take $R=P$ and $S=0$ for the first inequality. If $P=R+\partial S$, then $\partial P=\partial R$. In the definition of $\mathcal{F}(\partial P)$ use residual chain zero and filling $R$. Thus $\mathcal{F}(\partial P)\leq \mathbf{M}(R)\leq \mathbf{M}(R)+\mathbf{M}(S)$. Take the infimum over decompositions of $P$. Applying the same estimate to differences gives the continuous extension on each completion. $\square$
