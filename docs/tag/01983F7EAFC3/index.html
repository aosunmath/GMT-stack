Proposition 14.4.8 (Entropy is nonincreasing along a closed smooth flow)01983F7EAFC3

Proposition 14.4.8 (Entropy is nonincreasing along a closed smooth flow).01983F7EAFC3 If $t_1<t_2$ are smooth times of a closed Euclidean mean curvature flow, then

\[ \lambda (\Sigma _{t_2})\leq \lambda (\Sigma _{t_1}). \]

The supremum in the entropy definition need not be attained for this argument.

Proof

Fix $x_0$ and $s>0$ and apply Theorem 14.4.3 with terminal time $t_2+s$. It gives

\[ F_{x_0,s}(\Sigma _{t_2})\leq F_{x_0,s+t_2-t_1}(\Sigma _{t_1})\leq \lambda (\Sigma _{t_1}). \]

Take the supremum over $x_0,s$. This is the entropy consequence discussed in [WhiMCF15], Section 10. $\square$
