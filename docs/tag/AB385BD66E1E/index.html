Theorem 6.4.3 (Allard regularity with supercritical mean curvature)AB385BD66E1E

Theorem 6.4.3 (Allard regularity with supercritical mean curvature).AB385BD66E1E Fix $n,k\geq 1$ and $n<p<\infty $. There are $\varepsilon >0$ and $\gamma \in (0,1)$, depending only on $n,k,p$, with the following property. Let $V$ be an integral $n$-varifold in an open neighborhood of $\overline{B_ R(a)}\subset \mathbb R^{n+k}$, with $a\in \operatorname {spt}\| V\| $, and suppose its first variation in $B_ R(a)$ has no singular part:

\[ \delta V(X)=-\int H\cdot X\, d\| V\| \quad (X\in C_ c^1(B_ R(a);\mathbb R^{n+k})). \]

Assume $H\in L^ p(\| V\| \llcorner B_ R(a))$ and

\[ \frac{\| V\| (B_ R(a))}{\omega _ n R^ n}\leq 1+\varepsilon , \qquad R^{1-n/p}\left(\int _{B_ R(a)}|H|^ p\, d\| V\| \right)^{1/p}\leq \varepsilon . \]

Then in $B_{\gamma R}(a)$ the support of $V$ is a single $C^{1,1-n/p}$ graph over an $n$-plane, and $V$ has multiplicity one there. This is the integral-varifold specialization of [Sim18], Chapter 5, hypotheses (5.1) and Theorem 5.2, pp. 145–146, following [Allard72]. The source's density-lower-bound hypothesis is supplied here by integrality almost everywhere. The proof, including affine approximation and excess decay, remains external. General bounded first variation with a singular boundary term does not satisfy the stated hypothesis.
