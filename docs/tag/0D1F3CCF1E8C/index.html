Theorem 4.4.4 (Whole-space BV coarea)0D1F3CCF1E8C

Theorem 4.4.4 (Whole-space BV coarea).0D1F3CCF1E8C For $u\in BV(\mathbb R^ n)$,

\[ |Du|(\mathbb R^ n)=\int _{\mathbb R}P(E_ t;\mathbb R^ n)\, dt, \qquad E_ t=\{ u>t\} . \]

In particular almost every $E_ t$ has finite perimeter. Its volume may be infinite, especially when $t<0$.

Proof

For a compactly supported smooth vector field $X$, the signed layer-cake representation gives

\[ \int u\operatorname {div}X =\int _{\mathbb R}\int _{E_ t}\operatorname {div}X\, dx\, dt, \]

because the baseline term integrates to zero. Fubini is justified before this cancellation by the bound $|u||\operatorname {div}X|$ on the compact support. If $|X|\leq 1$, the right side is at most $\int P(E_ t)dt$. Taking the supremum proves one inequality. Measurability of $t\mapsto P(E_ t;W)$ for an open $W$ follows by taking the supremum over a countable dense family of compactly supported $C^1$ test fields in the variational definition.

For the reverse inequality, let $u_ j$ be smooth mollifications converging strictly as in Corollary 4.2.5. By Lemma 4.4.3, pass to a subsequence whose superlevel sets converge in symmetric difference for almost every $t$. Perimeter lower semicontinuity, Fatou, and smooth coarea give

\[ \int P(E_ t)\, dt\leq \liminf _ j\int P(\{ u_ j>t\} )\, dt =\lim _ j\int |\nabla u_ j|=|Du|(\mathbb R^ n). \]

This proves the identity. The argument is the approximation proof of [Giu84], Theorem 1.23, with strict approximation supplied here. $\square$
