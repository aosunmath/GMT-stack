Theorem 3.5.5 (Lipschitz functions agree with a smooth function off a small set)23ACEA4A9DD0

Theorem 3.5.5 (Lipschitz functions agree with a smooth function off a small set).23ACEA4A9DD0 If $f:\mathbb R^ m\to \mathbb R$ is Lipschitz and $\varepsilon >0$, there exists $g\in C^1(\mathbb R^ m)$ such that

\[ \mathcal L^ m\bigl (\{ f\ne g\} \cup \{ Df\ne Dg\} \bigr )<\varepsilon , \]

where the derivative comparison ignores the null nondifferentiability set of $f$. This is [Sim18], Chapter 2, Theorem 1.5, with the Whitney extension step isolated above.

Proof

Partition space into the bounded shells $A_ j=\{ j-1\leq |x|<j\} $, $j\geq 1$. Apply Lemma 3.5.4 to obtain compact $C_ j\subset A_ j$ with loss less than $\varepsilon 2^{-j}$, continuous restricted derivative, and uniform differentiability defects. The family $\{ C_ j\} $ is locally finite: a bounded neighborhood meets only finitely many shells. Hence $C=\bigcup _ jC_ j$ is closed. The compact sets are disjoint. In any bounded neighborhood there are only finitely many of them, so their pairwise distances are positive whenever both are nonempty. Consequently $Df|C$ is continuous locally and the defects tend uniformly to zero on each compact subset of $C$, being the maximum of finitely many uniform defects. The jet is compatible.

Theorem 3.5.2 supplies $g\in C^1$ with $g=f$ and $Dg=Df$ on $C$. The union of the two disagreement sets is contained, up to the stated null set, in $\mathbb R^ m\setminus C$. Its measure is less than $\sum _ j\varepsilon 2^{-j}=\varepsilon $. No finiteness of the total ambient measure or compact support of $f$ is assumed. $\square$
