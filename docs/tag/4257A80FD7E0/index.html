Definition 14.12.1 (A compact normal graph family)4257A80FD7E0

Definition 14.12.1 (A compact normal graph family).4257A80FD7E0 Let $\Sigma ^ n\subset \mathbb R^{n+1}$ be a smooth properly embedded two-sided hypersurface, and let $f\in C_ c^\infty (\Sigma )$. For sufficiently small $|s|$, set

\[ \Sigma _ s=\{ x+sf(x)\nu (x):x\in \Sigma \} ,\qquad G(c,t,s)=F_{c,t}(\Sigma _ s). \]

The graph is taken within a fixed tubular neighborhood of the compact support. A finite coordinate cover and compactness give an interval on which the maps remain embeddings. All changed points then lie in a fixed ambient compact set; their areas and curvatures are uniformly bounded on a smaller closed parameter interval. This is the variation class specified in [CM12], p. 763, footnote 6. The unchanged part may have unbounded curvature.
