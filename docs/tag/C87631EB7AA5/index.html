Definition 5.1.1 (Current and boundary)C87631EB7AA5

Definition 5.1.1 (Current and boundary).C87631EB7AA5 Let $\mathcal{D}^ m(U)=C_ c^\infty (U;\Lambda ^ m(\mathbb {R}^ n)^*)$ be the space of smooth compactly supported $m$-forms, with its usual test-function topology. An $m$-current is a continuous linear functional $T:\mathcal{D}^ m(U)\to \mathbb {R}$. For $m\geq 1$ its boundary is the $(m-1)$-current

\[ \partial T(\eta )=T(d\eta ),\qquad \eta \in \mathcal{D}^{m-1}(U). \]

For $m=0$ set $\partial T=0$. Weak convergence $T_ j\rightharpoonup T$ means $T_ j(\omega )\to T(\omega )$ for every $\omega \in \mathcal{D}^ m(U)$. See [Sim18], Chapter 6, Section 2, pp. 159–161.
