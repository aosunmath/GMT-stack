Proposition 9.4.3 (Bilateral metric bounds for every width)5B976BBC1CB5

Proposition 9.4.3 (Bilateral metric bounds for every width).5B976BBC1CB5 If $a^2g\leq h\leq b^2g$, then for every $p\geq 1$,

\[ a^ n\omega _ p(M,g)\leq \omega _ p(M,h)\leq b^ n\omega _ p(M,g). \]

This is the metric comparison used in [MNS19], proof of Lemma 1.

Proof

Proposition 9.4.2 identifies the two sets of competitors. Apply the $n$-mass comparison to every slice of each family, take the supremum in the parameter, and then take the infimum over families. This proves both bounds. In particular $g\leq h$ implies $\omega _ p(M,g)\leq \omega _ p(M,h)$. Taking $a=b$ recovers the scaling formula of Lemma 9.1.4. $\square$
