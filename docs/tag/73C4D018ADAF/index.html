Proposition 5.4.4 (Choosing a radius with controlled cutting boundary)73C4D018ADAF

Proposition 5.4.4 (Choosing a radius with controlled cutting boundary).73C4D018ADAF Let $T$ be locally integral near $\overline B_ R(a)$ and let $0<r<R$. For almost every $s\in (r,R)$,

\[ \partial (T\llcorner B_ s(a)) =(\partial T)\llcorner B_ s(a)+\langle T,|x-a|,s\rangle . \]

There is such an $s$ for which

\[ \mathbf M\bigl (\langle T,|x-a|,s\rangle \bigr ) \leq \frac{\| T\| (B_ R(a)\setminus \overline B_ r(a))}{R-r}. \]

Consequently the boundary mass of the restricted current is at most this quantity plus $\| \partial T\| (B_ R(a))$.

Proof

The distance function is one-Lipschitz. Apply the slicing theorem over the annulus and integrate over $s\in (r,R)$. The integral of the slice masses is no larger than the numerator, so some good level has mass no larger than their average. The restriction-boundary formula is the definition of the slice, and the boundary-mass estimate follows by the triangle inequality. A good level is chosen from the full-measure set where all slicing conclusions hold. $\square$
