Proposition 14.6.5 (Local curvature energy from the Brakke inequality)420AE237E649

Proposition 14.6.5 (Local curvature energy from the Brakke inequality).420AE237E649 Let $\eta \in C_ c^1(U)$ and $[a,b]\subset I$. Then

\[ \int _ a^ b\int \eta ^2|\vec H_ t|^2\, d\mu _ t\, dt \leq 2\mu _ a(\eta ^2)-2\mu _ b(\eta ^2) +4\int _ a^ b\int |D\eta |^2\, d\mu _ t\, dt. \]

The last integral is controlled by local mass bounds on the support of $\eta $. This is the energy estimate of [WhiMCF15], Theorem 9.5, written with a squared cutoff.

Proof

Use the time-independent test function $\phi =\eta ^2$. Young's inequality gives $2\eta \nabla \eta \cdot \vec H\leq \frac12\eta ^2|\vec H|^2+2|\nabla \eta |^2$. Move the remaining negative curvature term to the other side of the Brakke inequality and multiply by two. In particular a cutoff equal to one on a compact set bounds the spacetime curvature energy there. $\square$
