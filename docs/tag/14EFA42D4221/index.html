Lemma 4.4.3 (Almost-everywhere convergence of superlevel sets)14EFA42D4221

Lemma 4.4.3 (Almost-everywhere convergence of superlevel sets).14EFA42D4221 If $u_ j\to u$ in $L^1(U)$, there is a subsequence such that

\[ |(\{ u_ j>t\} \mathbin {\triangle }\{ u>t\} )\cap U|\longrightarrow 0 \quad \text{for almost every }t\in \mathbb R. \]

The same conclusion holds locally when the convergence is in $L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}(U)$, with a single exceptional null set for a countable exhaustion.

Proof

Choose a subsequence with $\sum _ j\| u_ j-u\| _{L^1(U)}<\infty $. The layer-cake identity and Tonelli show that for almost every $t$ the sum of the displayed symmetric-difference measures is finite; hence its terms tend to zero. For local convergence choose a diagonal subsequence whose errors are summable on each set of a countable relatively compact exhaustion. The union of its exceptional sets is still null. See [Giu84], p. 21. $\square$
