Corollary 6.8.4 (Density and mass lower bounds with bounded curvature)CECE32EC9E60

Corollary 6.8.4 (Density and mass lower bounds with bounded curvature).CECE32EC9E60 If $V$ is also integral, then for every $a\in \operatorname {spt}\mu $,

\[ \Theta ^ m(\mu ,a)\geq 1,\qquad \mu (B_ r(a))\geq e^{-\Lambda r}\omega _ m r^ m \]

whenever $\overline{B_ r(a)}\subset U$.

Proof

First density is upper semicontinuous in the center. Indeed, for $a_ j\to a$ and $0<\varepsilon <s$, the inclusion $B_{s-\varepsilon }(a_ j)\subset B_ s(a)$ and exponential monotonicity give

\[ \Theta ^ m(\mu ,a_ j)\leq e^{\Lambda (s-\varepsilon )}\frac{\mu (B_ s(a))}{\omega _ m(s-\varepsilon )^ m} \]

for large $j$. Let $\varepsilon \downarrow 0$, then $s\downarrow 0$. Integrality supplies density at least one almost everywhere. Choose such points approaching any support point; upper semicontinuity gives the same lower bound there. Finally compare the corrected ratio at $r$ with its limit at zero. This proof parallels Corollary 6.3.4 and specifies the curvature correction. $\square$
