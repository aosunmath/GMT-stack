Proposition 2.5.9 (Mollifiers converge at every Lebesgue point)C54A732E0D36

Proposition 2.5.9 (Mollifiers converge at every Lebesgue point).C54A732E0D36 Let $f\in L^1_{\rm loc}(\mathbb R^ n)$ and let $\rho $ be a bounded measurable function supported in $B_1$, with $\int \rho =1$. Put $\rho _\varepsilon (z)=\varepsilon ^{-n}\rho (z/\varepsilon )$. At every Lebesgue point $x$ of $f$,

\[ (\rho _\varepsilon *f)(x)\longrightarrow f(x). \]

Nonnegativity and smoothness of $\rho $ are not needed for this pointwise assertion.

Proof

Subtract $f(x)\int \rho _\varepsilon =f(x)$ from the convolution and estimate its absolute value by

\[ \| \rho \| _\infty \varepsilon ^{-n} \int _{B_\varepsilon (x)}|f(y)-f(x)|\, dy. \]

This is $\omega _ n\| \rho \| _\infty $ times a quantity tending to zero. The smooth-density approximation of measures in Proposition 2.4.8 concerns compact-test convergence; the present assertion describes values of mollified functions at individual Lebesgue points. $\square$
