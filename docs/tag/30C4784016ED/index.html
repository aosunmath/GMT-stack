Lemma 6.7.1 (Stationarity is closed under varifold convergence)30C4784016ED

Lemma 6.7.1 (Stationarity is closed under varifold convergence).30C4784016ED If stationary $m$-varifolds $V_ j$ converge weakly to $V$ in $U$, then $V$ is stationary in $U$.

Proof

For $X\in C_ c^1(U;\mathbb R^ N)$ the function $(x,S)\mapsto \operatorname {div}_ S X(x)$ is continuous and compactly supported on the Grassmann bundle. Thus $\delta V(X)=\lim _ j\delta V_ j(X)=0$. This assertion alone does not guarantee that an arbitrary limit is integral; integrality uses Theorem 6.2.1 under its hypotheses. $\square$
