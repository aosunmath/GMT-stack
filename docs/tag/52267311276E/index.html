Lemma 8.6.7 (Uniform closeness to the identity)52267311276E

Lemma 8.6.7 (Uniform closeness to the identity).52267311276E The choice of $\tau _0$ in Proposition 8.6.5 can make the deformation arbitrarily close to the identity in $\mathbf F_ V$, uniformly on $[0,1]\times K_ C$. For every integral mod-two cycle $T$ with mass at most $C$, the corresponding diffeomorphisms also satisfy

\[ \mathcal F((\varphi _ t^{|T|})_\# T-T)\leq A t\mathbf M(T),\qquad 0\leq t\leq 1, \]

with $A$ independent of $T$.

Proof

Uniform smooth bounds give $d(\varphi _ t(x),x)\leq A_1t$, displacement of the tangent plane at most $A_1t$, and $|J_ P\varphi _ t-1|\leq A_1t$, with a fixed smooth metric on $G_ n(M)$. For a test function of absolute value and Lipschitz constant at most one, subtract the pushforward and original integrals to bound their difference by $A_2Ct$. Taking the supremum gives the varifold estimate. For cycles, use the external mod-two homotopy formula for $h(u,x)=\varphi _ u^{|T|}(x)$ on $[0,t]\times M$. Its time derivative is uniformly bounded and its spatial derivatives are bounded, hence the swept $(n+1)$-chain has mass at most $At\mathbf M(T)$. Its boundary is the required difference because $\partial T=0$. Taking $t\leq \tau _0$ makes the sum of the two bounds arbitrarily small. No mass-distance bound follows from this argument; Example 8.5.6 rules one out in general. $\square$
