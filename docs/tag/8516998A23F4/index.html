Lemma 14.12.3 (Uniform bounds for the changed compact part)8516998A23F4

Lemma 14.12.3 (Uniform bounds for the changed compact part).8516998A23F4 Let $\Sigma _ s$ be a compact normal graph family over a smooth proper embedded hypersurface of polynomial area growth. On a sufficiently small fixed closed interval of $s$, there are constants $A_0,C_0$ such that

\[ G(c,t,s)\leq F_{c,t}(\Sigma )+A_0(4\pi t)^{-n/2} \exp \left(-\frac{\operatorname {dist}(c,B_{C_0}(0))^2}{4t}\right). \]

Consequently the added term is at most $A_0(4\pi t)^{-n/2}$, and for $|c|>C_0+R$ it is at most $C(n)A_0R^{-n}$ uniformly for all $t>0$. On each compact set of $(c,t)$, $G$ and its derivatives in $s$ are continuous, and $G(c,t,s)\to F_{c,t}(\Sigma )$ uniformly as $s\to 0$.

Proof

Choose a compact region in the parameter surface whose interior contains the support of $f$. Outside this region all embeddings are identical. Its perturbed image lies in $B_{C_0}$ and has area at most $A_0$, uniformly in $s$. Subtract the integrals on the common unchanged region and discard the nonnegative old contribution from the compact region to obtain the inequality.

For far centers the exponential is at most $e^{-R^2/(4t)}$. The substitution $t=R^2u$ gives

\[ \sup _{t>0}t^{-n/2}e^{-R^2/(4t)} =R^{-n}\sup _{u>0}u^{-n/2}e^{-1/(4u)}=C_ nR^{-n}. \]

The last supremum is finite since $n\geq 1$ and the function tends to zero at both ends. Shape derivatives involve only a fixed compact region and smooth bounded geometry. The unchanged part is controlled by polynomial Gaussian moments for parameters in compact positive-scale sets. Differentiation and uniform continuity therefore give the last assertions. Compare [CM12], (7.40)–(7.46). $\square$
