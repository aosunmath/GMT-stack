Proposition 14.5.4 (Equality in monotonicity gives self-similar motion)5900E7823B75

Proposition 14.5.4 (Equality in monotonicity gives self-similar motion).5900E7823B75 Suppose a smooth closed flow has constant Gaussian integral centered at $(x_0,T)$ on a time interval $(a,b)\subset (-\infty ,T)$. Then throughout that interval

\[ \vec H+\frac{(x-x_0)^\perp }{2(T-t)}=0. \]

Equivalently, the images $(T-t)^{-1/2}(\Sigma _ t-x_0)$ are independent of time and form a self-shrinker in the project's scale-one normalization.

Proof

Theorem 14.4.3 gives zero integral of a continuous nonnegative function at each time, and hence the displayed pointwise equation. By Proposition 14.5.1, the rescaled velocity is tangent everywhere. A time-dependent tangential vector field on the compact parameter manifold can be removed by reparametrization, so the rescaled images are constant. The equation then reads $\vec H+x^\perp /2=0$. Conversely that equation makes the monotonicity integrand zero. $\square$
