Corollary 4.4.10 (Union and intersection of finite-perimeter sets)3FF2415BEDED

Corollary 4.4.10 (Union and intersection of finite-perimeter sets).3FF2415BEDED If $E,F$ have locally finite perimeter in $U$, then so do $E\cup F$ and $E\cap F$, and

\[ P(E\cup F;A)+P(E\cap F;A)\leq P(E;A)+P(F;A) \]

for every Borel $A\subset U$.

Proof

The characteristic functions of the union and intersection are respectively the maximum and minimum of $\chi _ E,\chi _ F$. Apply Proposition 4.4.9. This argument is insensitive to changes on Lebesgue-null sets. $\square$
