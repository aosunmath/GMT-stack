Proposition 6.7.3 (Radial tangency gives homogeneous weight)A324E62A87A7

Proposition 6.7.3 (Radial tangency gives homogeneous weight).A324E62A87A7 Let $C$ be a stationary $m$-varifold on all of $\mathbb R^ N$, with $x\in S$ for $C$-almost every $(x,S)$. Its weight $\nu $ satisfies

\[ s^{-m}\int \phi (x/s)\, d\nu (x)=\int \phi (x)\, d\nu (x)\qquad (s>0) \]

for every $\phi \in C_ c^1(\mathbb R^ N)$. In particular $\nu (sA)=s^ m\nu (A)$ for every Borel set $A$.

Proof

Test stationarity with $X(x)=\phi (x)x$. Radial tangency gives $\int (m\phi +x\cdot D\phi )\, d\nu =0$. The derivative of $F(s)=s^{-m}\int \phi (x/s)\, d\nu $ is

\[ F'(s)=-s^{-m-1}\int \bigl (m\phi (x/s)+(x/s)\cdot D\phi (x/s)\bigr )\, d\nu =0, \]

using the same identity with the test function $x\mapsto \phi (x/s)$. Differentiation is justified by a common compact support and bounded derivatives when $s$ ranges in a compact subinterval of $(0,\infty )$. Thus $F(s)=F(1)$. Equality on smooth tests identifies the two Radon measures and extends to Borel sets. $\square$
