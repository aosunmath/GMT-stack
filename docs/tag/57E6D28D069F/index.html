Lemma 7.15.5 (Kato inequality and zeros of a tensor)57E6D28D069F

Lemma 7.15.5 (Kato inequality and zeros of a tensor).57E6D28D069F For a smooth tensor $T$ on a Riemannian manifold, $u=|T|$ is locally Lipschitz and has a weak gradient satisfying $|\nabla u|\leq |\nabla T|$ almost everywhere. On $\{ u>0\} $, $\partial _ i u=\langle \nabla _ iT,T\rangle /u$.

Proof

Parallel transport along short curves is an isometry; the difference of the norms of the endpoint values of $T$ is bounded by the integral of $|\nabla T|$. Local boundedness of $\nabla T$ proves local Lipschitz regularity. Theorem 3.1.2, in coordinate charts, supplies almost-everywhere differentiability and the weak gradient. At a differentiability point where $u=0$, the nonnegative function $u$ has a local minimum and its differential is zero. At the other points differentiate $u^2=\langle T,T\rangle $ and use Cauchy–Schwarz, then sum over an orthonormal frame. This also justifies the inequality on the zero set, where division by $u$ is unavailable. $\square$
