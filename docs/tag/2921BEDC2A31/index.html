Proposition 2.2.2 (Lipschitz distortion of Hausdorff measure)2921BEDC2A31

Proposition 2.2.2 (Lipschitz distortion of Hausdorff measure).2921BEDC2A31 Let $A\subset \mathbb {R}^ n$, let $f:A\to \mathbb {R}^ q$ be $L$-Lipschitz with $0<L<\infty $, and let $m\geq 1$. Then

\[ \mathcal{H}^ m(f(A))\leq L^ m\mathcal{H}^ m(A). \]

In particular, Lipschitz maps take $\mathcal{H}^ m$-null sets to $\mathcal{H}^ m$-null sets. A constant map also has this null-set property. This follows directly from the covering definition in [Sim18], Chapter 1, Section 2.

Proof

Intersect each set in a $\delta $-cover of $A$ with $A$ and take its image under $f$. The image diameters are at most $L$ times the original diameters. Thus

\[ \mathcal{H}^ m_{L\delta }(f(A))\leq L^ m\mathcal{H}^ m_\delta (A). \]

Let $\delta \downarrow 0$. For a constant map the image is empty or a singleton, whose $\mathcal{H}^ m$ measure is zero when $m\geq 1$. $\square$
