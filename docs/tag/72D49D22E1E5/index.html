Proposition 13.2.4 (Comparison for ordered data)72D49D22E1E5

Proposition 13.2.4 (Comparison for ordered data).72D49D22E1E5 On the same bounded Lipschitz domain, suppose $\psi _1\leq \psi _2$ almost everywhere, the boundary traces satisfy $\operatorname {Tr}g_1\leq \operatorname {Tr}g_2$, and $f_1\leq f_2$ almost everywhere. Assume both admissible classes are nonempty. Then their minimizers satisfy $u_1\leq u_2$ almost everywhere.

Proof

Sobolev truncation and the ordered traces give $w=(u_1-u_2)^+\in H^1_0(U)$. The functions $u_1-w=\min \{ u_1,u_2\} $ and $u_2+w=\max \{ u_1,u_2\} $ are admissible in the first and second problems respectively. The obstacle ordering is needed for the first assertion. Their variational inequalities imply

\[ \int _ U|Dw|^2 =\int _ U(Du_1-Du_2)\cdot Dw \leq \int _ U(f_1-f_2)w\leq 0. \]

Poincare gives $w=0$. The Sobolev positive-part chain rule and compatibility of traces with Lipschitz truncation are the functional-analytic inputs, with background in [SimonPDE], Chapters 5–9. $\square$
