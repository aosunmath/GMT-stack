Proposition 7.2.8 (Strong comparison for minimal graphs)EA3CB199A4FD

Proposition 7.2.8 (Strong comparison for minimal graphs).EA3CB199A4FD Let $U$ be connected, and let $u_1,u_2\in C^2(U)$ solve the minimal surface equation. If $u_1\leq u_2$ in $U$ and equality holds at one interior point, then $u_1=u_2$ throughout $U$.

Proof

The solutions are smooth by Proposition 7.2.7. Their nonnegative difference solves the locally uniformly elliptic equation in Proposition 7.2.5. On a sufficiently small ball about a zero, Harnack gives $\sup w\leq C\inf w=0$. Thus the zero set is open; it is also closed by continuity. Connectedness completes the proof. Compare [ChMin], Proposition 19.3. $\square$
