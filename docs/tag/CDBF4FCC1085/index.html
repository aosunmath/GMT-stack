Proposition 11.7.2 (The trace-free Simons equation for CMC hypersurfaces)CDBF4FCC1085

Proposition 11.7.2 (The trace-free Simons equation for CMC hypersurfaces).CDBF4FCC1085 Let $\Sigma ^ n$ be a smooth two-sided immersed hypersurface with constant $H$ in a space form of sectional curvature $c$. With the preceding notation and $\alpha =nc+H^2/n$,

\[ \Delta b=(\alpha -s^2)b+H\left(b^2-\frac{s^2}{n}g\right), \]
\[ \frac12\Delta s^2=|\nabla b|^2+\alpha s^2-s^4+H\operatorname {tr}(B^3). \]

Here $(b^2)_{ij}=b_ i{}^ kb_{kj}$. For $c=1$ the second equation is the normalization of [AdC94], equation (2.4).

Proof

Since $H$ is constant and $\nabla g=0$, one has $\Delta b=\Delta h$. In Theorem 7.15.3, substitute $h=b+(H/n)g$ and $|A|^2=s^2+H^2/n$. The coefficient of $b$ becomes $2H^2/n-s^2-H^2/n+nc=\alpha -s^2$. The scalar multiple of $g$ becomes $-Hs^2/n$, while the quadratic term is $Hb^2$. This proves the tensor equation. Contract it with $b$, use $\langle b,g\rangle =0$, and apply $\frac12\Delta |b|^2=|\nabla b|^2+\langle b,\Delta b\rangle $ to obtain the norm equation. $\square$
