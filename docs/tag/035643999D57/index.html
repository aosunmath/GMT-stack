Remark 15.2.1 (Existence, regularity, and the meaning of dimension)035643999D57

Remark 15.2.1 (Existence, regularity, and the meaning of dimension).035643999D57 Pitts' 1981 monograph [Pitts81] made discrete min-max and almost-minimizing constructions into a systematic existence theory. In the smooth closed setting, its original hypersurface range is $2\leq n\leq 5$, or ambient dimension $3\leq n+1\leq 6$. Schoen–Simon's stable regularity theory [SS81] supplies the extension used in the modern ambient-dimension-seven statement. This is why the project records Pitts' original range separately from later realization theorems. Song's introduction [Song18] gives a concise historical account of this distinction.
