Corollary 12.5.11 (Monotonicity and area-order energy growth).52022BB1B0F4 For an entire solution $u:\mathbb R^ n\to [-1,1]$ with fixed $\varepsilon >0$, the function $r^{1-n}E_\varepsilon (u;B_ r(a))$ is nondecreasing for every center $a$. In particular, for $R\geq r>0$,

\[ E_\varepsilon (u;B_ R(a))\geq (R/r)^{n-1}E_\varepsilon (u;B_ r(a)). \]
Proof

Corollary 12.5.5 makes the bulk term in Theorem 12.5.10 nonnegative; the boundary term is a square. Integrate the derivative inequality. This is a diffuse counterpart of stationary area-ratio monotonicity, with an additional discrepancy term. $\square$
