Proposition 11.10.4 (The first integral from area minus volume)70AC5E706A80

Proposition 11.10.4 (The first integral from area minus volume).70AC5E706A80 For fixed endpoint radii of a cylindrical graph, critical points of area minus $H$ times enclosed volume satisfy the ODE in Proposition 11.10.3. The translation-invariant one-dimensional integrand is

\[ L(r,p)=2\pi r\sqrt{1+p^2}-H\pi r^2, \]

and its Beltrami quantity $L-pL_ p$ equals $2\pi c$.

Proof

The lateral area element is $r\sqrt{1+r_ z^2}\, d\theta \, dz$, and the volume between fixed axial planes is $\int \pi r^2dz$. Endpoint-fixed variations kill boundary terms. Direct differentiation gives

\[ L_ r=2\pi \sqrt{1+p^2}-2\pi Hr,\qquad L_ p=\frac{2\pi rp}{\sqrt{1+p^2}}. \]

The equation $(L_ p)'=L_ r$ simplifies to the stated graph ODE. Because $L$ has no explicit $z$ dependence, $(L-r_ zL_ p)'=r_ z(L_ r-(L_ p)')=0$. Evaluating $L-r_ zL_ p$ gives $2\pi [r/\sqrt{1+r_ z^2}-Hr^2/2]$. This is a stationarity calculation; it does not assert that a critical graph minimizes area under the volume constraint. $\square$
