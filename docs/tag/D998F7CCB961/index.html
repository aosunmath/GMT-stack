Proposition 13.4.5 (Weiss energies of the two model families)D998F7CCB961

Proposition 13.4.5 (Weiss energies of the two model families).D998F7CCB961 For the quadratic polynomials $p_ A$ and half-parabolas $h_ e$,

\[ W(r,p_ A)=\frac{\omega _ n}{2(n+2)},\qquad W(r,h_ e)=\frac{\omega _ n}{4(n+2)} \quad (r>0). \]

Thus the limiting Weiss energy distinguishes regular and singular points once the dichotomy theorem is applied.

Proof

Both models are degree-two homogeneous and satisfy $u\Delta u=u$ almost everywhere. Integration by parts on $B_1$ therefore gives $\int _{B_1}|Du|^2=2\int _{\partial B_1}u^2-\int _{B_1}u$, so $W(1,u)=\int _{B_1}u$. Rotational symmetry gives $\int _{B_1}x_ i^2=\omega _ n/(n+2)$. Taking the trace of $A$ gives the first value; restriction to a half-ball gives the second. Scaling gives all radii, and $C^1$ blow-up convergence gives the corresponding limiting energies. The common quadratic value is the constant used in [Fig18], Proposition 8.2. $\square$
