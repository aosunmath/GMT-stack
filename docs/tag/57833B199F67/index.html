Remark 1.1.3 (The expanded variational reference)57833B199F67

Remark 1.1.3 (The expanded variational reference).57833B199F67 The first variational additions connect Pitts' book [Pitts81] to minimal hypersurfaces and stability, cycle-space min-max theory, the volume spectrum, Morse index, Willmore geometry, CMC hypersurfaces, curvature networks, and Allen–Cahn theory. They draw on the sixteen additional supplied files, with editions and roles recorded in the source inventory. Those first two batches contained twenty-one distinct works or editions in twenty-two supplied files. The later library expansion adds checked passages on compactness, geometric inequalities, multiplicity, abundance, and flow singularities, together with a larger catalogue of sources awaiting detailed study. The History chapter connects these developments to their original problems and to first-person recollections.

The current foundations include BV compactness, current operations and slicing, and the Plateau existence argument in Theorem 5.4.7. The minimal-surface chapters now connect stability to scalar curvature, the two-dimensional Bernstein theorem, and the spectral criterion for cones. The CMC chapter distinguishes total-volume constraints from unrestricted area-minus-volume stability. The mean-curvature-flow chapter develops evolution equations, comparison, Gaussian monotonicity, entropy, rescaling, and the Brakke formulation in Definition 14.6.1. The classical obstacle chapter now includes existence, comparison, reaction measures, quadratic blow-ups, and local proofs of the Weiss and Monneau formulas and uniqueness of singular blow-ups. Its optimal regularity, regular–singular dichotomy, and singular stratification retain external proof dependencies. Thin obstacles, level-set flows, finer singularity theory, free-boundary surfaces, and capillarity require further development. A cited theorem without a local proof remains an external dependency; a chapter in the table of contents does not mean that its subject is complete.
