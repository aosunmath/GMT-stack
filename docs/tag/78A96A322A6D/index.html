Lemma 3.7.3 (A ball forced outside a nearby plane)78A96A322A6D

Lemma 3.7.3 (A ball forced outside a nearby plane).78A96A322A6D Let $x\ne y$, $d_0=|y-x|$, and let $P,P_ x$ be $m$-planes with $d(P,P_ x)\leq 1/8$. If $|q_ P(y-x)|>d_0/2$, then

\[ B_{d_0/32}(y)\subset C_{1/4}(x,P_ x)\cap B_{2d_0}(x). \]

This is the vector-normal version of Lemma 4.13.3.

Proof

The projection estimate gives $|q_{P_ x}(y-x)|>3d_0/8$. For $|z-y|<d_0/32$, use $\| q_{P_ x}\| _{\mathrm{op}}=1$ to obtain $|q_{P_ x}(z-x)|>11d_0/32$, while $|z-x|<33d_0/32<2d_0$. Their ratio exceeds $1/3$ and hence $1/4$. No sign choice for a normal vector is involved, so the calculation works in every codimension. $\square$
