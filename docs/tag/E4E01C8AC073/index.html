Theorem 12.6.5 (The geometric stability inequality)E4E01C8AC073

Theorem 12.6.5 (The geometric stability inequality).E4E01C8AC073 For a stable smooth Allen–Cahn solution on a Riemannian manifold and every $\eta \in C_ c^\infty $,

\[ \int \left(|\nabla ^2u|^2-|\nabla |\nabla u||^2+ \operatorname {Ric}(\nabla u,\nabla u)\right)\eta ^2 \leq \int |\nabla u|^2|\nabla \eta |^2. \]
Proof

Put $w=|\nabla u|$. Bochner and the equation give

\[ \tfrac 12\Delta _ g w^2=|\nabla ^2u|^2+ \varepsilon ^{-2}W''(u)w^2+\operatorname {Ric}(\nabla u,\nabla u). \]

The compactly supported $H^1$ test $\eta w$ is admissible in stability. Expanding $|\nabla (\eta w)|^2$, use $w\nabla w=\tfrac 12\nabla w^2$ to integrate the cross term by parts. After division by $\varepsilon $, the potential term cancels the same term in the Bochner formula. The result is exactly the asserted inequality. All products are locally integrable; the norm test can equivalently be approximated by $\eta \sqrt{w^2+\delta ^2}$ and then $\delta \downarrow 0$. This proves [ChAC], Proposition 4.1 and Exercise 4.2, with the test-function justification explicit. $\square$
