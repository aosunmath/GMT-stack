Proposition 7.16.2 (The weighted curvature estimate from stability)BC774BF683CF

Proposition 7.16.2 (The weighted curvature estimate from stability).BC774BF683CF Assume the immersion is stable. For $0\leq q<\sqrt{2/n}$ there is a constant $C_{n,q}<\infty $ such that every smooth compactly supported real $\phi $ satisfies

\[ \int _\Sigma \phi ^2u^{2q+4} \leq C_{n,q}\int _\Sigma u^{2q+2}|\nabla \phi |^2. \]

The constants can deteriorate as $q\uparrow \sqrt{2/n}$. Compare [SSY75], equations (1.36)–(1.38), in zero ambient curvature.

Proof

Write $w=w_\varepsilon $ and introduce finite nonnegative integrals

\[ I=\int \phi ^2w^{2q}|\nabla w|^2,\quad J=\int w^{2q+2}|\nabla \phi |^2,\quad L=\int \phi ^2w^{2q+4},\quad E=\varepsilon ^2\int \phi ^2w^{2q+2}, \]

and the signed cross term $K=\int \phi w^{2q+1}\langle \nabla w,\nabla \phi \rangle $. Cauchy–Schwarz gives $|K|\leq \sqrt{IJ}$. Multiply Lemma 7.16.1 by $\phi ^2w^{2q}$ and integrate by parts to obtain

\[ (1+2q+2/n)I\leq L-2K. \]

Use the smooth compactly supported test $\zeta =\phi w^{q+1}$ in stability. Since $u^2=w^2-\varepsilon ^2$, this gives

\[ L\leq (q+1)^2I+J+2(q+1)K+E. \]

Combining the two inequalities, with $\delta =2/n-q^2>0$, yields

\[ \delta I\leq J+2qK+E \leq J+\frac\delta 2 I+\frac{2q^2}{\delta }J+E. \]

Thus $I\leq (2/\delta +4q^2/\delta ^2)J+(2/\delta )E$. A second Young inequality in the stability bound gives $L\leq 2(q+1)^2I+2J+E$, hence $L\leq C_{n,q}(J+E)$ after increasing the constant. On the fixed compact support, $u$ is bounded, $E\to 0$, and the other integrands converge by dominated convergence as $\varepsilon \downarrow 0$. The resulting limit is the statement. This argument never differentiates $u$ at a zero without regularization. $\square$
