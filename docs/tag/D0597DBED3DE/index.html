Proposition 12.4.9 (Limits of minimizers with a fixed local radius)D0597DBED3DE

Proposition 12.4.9 (Limits of minimizers with a fixed local radius).D0597DBED3DE Let $U$ be a bounded Lipschitz domain and $\delta >0$. Suppose $\varepsilon _ j\downarrow 0$, $E_{\varepsilon _ j}(u_ j;U)\leq C$, and

\[ E_{\varepsilon _ j}(u_ j;U)\leq E_{\varepsilon _ j}(v;U) \quad \text{whenever }\| v-u_ j\| _{L^1(U)}<\delta . \]

After a subsequence, $u_ j\to u_ E$ in $L^1(U)$, the energies converge to $c_ WP(E;U)$, and

\[ P(E;U)\leq P(F;U)\quad \text{if }|E\triangle F|<\delta /2. \]

Only non-strict local minimality is concluded, even if the approximating minima were strict. The radius $\delta $ is independent of $j$.

Proof

Compactness gives the phase limit. For a finite-perimeter competitor $F$ with $2|E\triangle F|<\delta $, choose its recovery family $v_ j$ from Theorem 12.4.7. Then $\| v_ j-u_ j\| _1\to \| u_ F-u_ E\| _1=2|E\triangle F|<\delta $, so for all large $j$ the comparison is admissible. There is a fixed positive margin below $\delta $. The lower bound for $u_ j$, the minimizing inequality, and recovery for $F$ yield $c_ WP(E;U)\leq \liminf _ jE_{\varepsilon _ j}(u_ j;U)\leq \limsup _ jE_{\varepsilon _ j}(u_ j;U)\leq c_ WP(F;U)$. Taking $F=E$ proves energy convergence; arbitrary $F$ proves local perimeter minimality. Infinite-perimeter competitors impose no restriction. This is the argument of [ChAC], Proposition 3.10, with the phase/indicator distance and strict comparison margin explicit, in the unconstrained Euclidean-domain setting. $\square$
