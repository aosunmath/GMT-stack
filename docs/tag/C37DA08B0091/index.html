Theorem 4.10.6 (Two-sided phase volume at a reduced-boundary point)C37DA08B0091

Theorem 4.10.6 (Two-sided phase volume at a reduced-boundary point).C37DA08B0091 If $x\in \partial ^*E$, then for some $r_0>0$ and all $0<r<r_0$,

\[ |E\cap B_ r(x)|\geq c_ n r^ n,\qquad |B_ r(x)\setminus E|\geq c_ n r^ n, \qquad c_ n=(3n)^{-n}. \]

The constant is not sharp. These are the two estimates (10)–(11) in [Sim18], p. 99; the limiting density $1/2$ requires the subsequent blow-up argument.

Proof

Use Proposition 4.9.5 and Proposition 4.10.3 to obtain $V^{(n-1)/n}\leq \mu (B_ r)+V'\leq 3V'$ for almost every small radius. Lemma 4.10.4 supplies positivity at every radius, and Lemma 4.9.3 supplies absolute continuity and $V(0)=0$. Apply Lemma 4.10.5 with $C=3$. The argument for $W$ uses the other flux bound and gives the same constant. The intersection of the full-measure sets of good radii suffices for the differential inequalities; their integrated conclusions hold for every small radius. $\square$
