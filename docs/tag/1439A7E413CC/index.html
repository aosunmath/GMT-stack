Theorem 14.26.2 (An explicit local height-to-gradient estimate)1439A7E413CC

Theorem 14.26.2 (An explicit local height-to-gradient estimate).1439A7E413CC Let a graphical flow be smooth on a neighborhood of $\overline B_ R(y)\times [0,T]$, $R>0$. Define

\[ V_0=\sup _{B_ R(y)}\sqrt{1+|Du(x,0)|^2},\quad M=\sup _{B_ R(y)\times [0,T]}u,\quad K=M-\inf _{0\leq t\leq T}u(y,t). \]

Then $K\geq 0$ and, for every $0\leq t\leq T$,

\[ v(y,t)\leq 42V_0\exp \left((32n+1)\max \{ 1,K^2/R^2\} \right). \]

This is an explicit version of [EH91], Theorem 2.3. The constants here are sufficient constants, not sharp ones. Only the initial slope in the base ball and a height oscillation over the cylinder enter the estimate.

Proof

Translate and parabolically rescale to $y=0$, $R=1$, scaling height by $R^{-1}$ as well. First subtract $M+\epsilon $ from the rescaled height, so that the resulting $h$ is strictly negative throughout the cylinder. Set

\[ \beta =\max \{ 1,K+\epsilon \} ,\qquad \lambda =64n\beta ^2,\qquad \eta =(e^{\lambda \varphi }-1)_+, \quad \varphi =1-|x|^2+h/(2\beta ). \]

The positive support of $\eta $ stays strictly inside the base ball, uniformly over the finite slab. At the center $\varphi (0,t)\geq 1/2$, whereas everywhere $\varphi <1$.

Consider a positive maximum of $v\eta $ over space and all times up to a chosen time. If it exceeds the initial supremum, it occurs at positive time and inside the positive support. There $\nabla (v\eta )=0$. The angle evolution from Proposition 14.18.2 and the product rule give

\[ 0\leq \mathcal P(v\eta ) =v\mathcal P\eta -|A|^2v\eta \leq v\mathcal P\eta . \]

The two gradient terms cancel at the maximum. The tangential coordinate drift also vanishes there. Since $\eta =e^{\lambda \varphi }-1$ in this positive region, it follows that

\[ \mathcal P\varphi \geq \lambda |\nabla \varphi |^2. \]

If $v\geq 4+16\beta $, Lemma 14.26.1 would give $2n\geq 4n$, a contradiction. Thus at such a maximum $v<4+16\beta \leq 20\beta $. Since $\eta \leq e^\lambda $, the complete maximum estimate is

\[ v\eta \leq e^\lambda \max \{ V_0,20\beta \} \leq e^\lambda (V_0+20\beta ). \]

At the center $\eta \geq e^{\lambda /2}-1\geq \tfrac 12 e^{\lambda /2}$, because $\lambda \geq 64$. It follows that

\[ v(0,t)\leq 2(V_0+20\beta )e^{32n\beta ^2} \leq 42V_0e^{(32n+1)\beta ^2}, \]

using $V_0\geq 1$, $\beta \geq 1$ and $\beta \leq e^{\beta ^2}$. Let $\epsilon \downarrow 0$ and restore the scale. In the rescaled problem $K$ is the original oscillation divided by $R$, giving the stated formula. Only the positive region of $\eta $ was differentiated, and the lateral zero set cannot carry a positive maximum. $\square$
