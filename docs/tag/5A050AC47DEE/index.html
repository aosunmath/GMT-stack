Theorem 6.2.1 (Allard compactness for integral varifolds)5A050AC47DEE

Theorem 6.2.1 (Allard compactness for integral varifolds).5A050AC47DEE Let $U\subset \mathbb R^ N$ be open, and let $V_ j$ be integral $m$-varifolds in $U$ with locally bounded first variation. Assume that for every relatively compact open $W\Subset U$,

\[ \sup _ j\bigl (\| V_ j\| (W)+\| \delta V_ j\| (W)\bigr )<\infty . \]

Here $\| \delta V_ j\| $ is the total variation measure of the first-variation functional. A subsequence converges as varifolds to an integral $m$-varifold $V$ with locally bounded first variation. Integrality of the limit is the substantive additional conclusion beyond weak compactness of Radon measures. Source: [Allard72], Theorem 6.4; see also [White09], Section 3. The proof remains external.
