Definition 5.2.2 (Flat norm and completion)394A15B53A4E

Definition 5.2.2 (Flat norm and completion).394A15B53A4E For $P\in \mathcal{P}_ m(\mathbb {R}^ n;\mathbb {Z})$, define

\[ \mathcal{F}(P)=\inf \{ \mathbf{M}(R)+\mathbf{M}(S): R\in \mathcal{P}_ m(\mathbb {R}^ n;\mathbb {Z}), S\in \mathcal{P}_{m+1}(\mathbb {R}^ n;\mathbb {Z}),\ P=R+\partial S\} . \]

When $m=n$, the group of $(n+1)$-chains is zero. This flat norm is symmetric, subadditive, and vanishes only at zero, so $d(P,Q)=\mathcal F(P-Q)$ is a translation-invariant metric; a proof is given in Proposition 5.3.16. For a compact set $K$, let $\mathcal{P}_ m(K)$ be the chains supported in $K$, with the ambient flat norm. The compactly supported flat chains are the union over compact $K$ of the metric completions of $\mathcal{P}_ m(K)$, using the natural inclusions. This is the convention of [WhiCho14], equation (2.2) and the discussion on pp. 5–6. The fillings in the displayed infimum are not required to lie in $K$.
