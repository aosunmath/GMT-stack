Theorem 7.9.10 (Stable cones with smooth links are flat in dimensions at most six)0574E4F9C647

Theorem 7.9.10 (Stable cones with smooth links are flat in dimensions at most six).0574E4F9C647 Let $2\leq n\leq 6$ and let $L^{n-1}\subset \mathbb S^ n$ be a smooth closed connected embedded two-sided minimal hypersurface. If $C^*(L)$ is stable away from its vertex, then $L$ is a great sphere and $C(L)$ is a hyperplane. This is the smooth-link version of the dimension bound in [ChMin], Corollary 26.6.

Proof

If $A_ L$ is not identically zero, the previous proposition and the stability criterion give

\[ -\frac{(n-2)^2}{4}\leq \lambda _0(L)\leq -(n-1), \]

which is impossible for $2\leq n\leq 6$. Hence $A_ L=0$. Its spherical unit normal has zero Euclidean derivative in every tangent direction, so it is constant on connected $L$. Thus $L$ lies in the equator orthogonal to that normal. It is open in that equator by equality of dimensions, and closed by compactness; hence it is the entire great sphere. The hypotheses include smoothness of the link. Extending this statement to cones with additional singularities requires a separate regularity or dimension-reduction argument. $\square$
