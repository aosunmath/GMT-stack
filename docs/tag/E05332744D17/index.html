Remark 8.1.4 (Smooth sweepouts in dimension three)E05332744D17

Remark 8.1.4 (Smooth sweepouts in dimension three).E05332744D17 Colding–De Lellis [CDL], Section 1.1, formulate one-parameter families of surfaces on a closed three-manifold and allow specified degenerations at finitely many points. Their saturated classes are closed under parameter-dependent ambient isotopies. This provides a geometric route to the pull-tight and replacement arguments, alongside Pitts' cycle-space formulation. The parameter space, allowed degenerations, and saturation condition are part of that construction; they must be checked before transferring a width or a genus conclusion to another setting. The author's publication page also lists an erratum, which should be consulted when importing detailed proofs from this survey.
