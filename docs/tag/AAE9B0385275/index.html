Remark 14.2.5 (The supplied generic-flow paper has a specific scope)AAE9B0385275

Remark 14.2.5 (The supplied generic-flow paper has a specific scope).AAE9B0385275 The supplied version of [CCMS20] is arXiv:2003.14344v1, dated March 31, 2020. It studies one-sided ancient flows and perturbations avoiding certain unstable singularity models. Its introduction explicitly retains possible obstructions involving higher multiplicity and noncylindrical models with cylindrical ends. We do not turn this version into an unconditional classification of all generic flow singularities. Definitions 14.6.1, 14.4.6, and 14.6.10 now provide the weak-flow, entropy, and regularity vocabulary. The precise perturbation theorems still require further development; Theorem 6.2.3 is one connection to the chain and varifold framework.
