Proposition 11.3.13 (First and second variation for prescribed mean curvature)2F067F1DC90E

Proposition 11.3.13 (First and second variation for prescribed mean curvature).2F067F1DC90E For the outward normal convention,

\[ \delta \mathcal A^ h(f\nu )=\int _\Sigma (H-h)f\, dA. \]

At a critical boundary $H=h|_\Sigma $, the second variation is

\[ Q_ h(f,f)=\int _\Sigma \left(|\nabla f|^2- (|A|^2+\operatorname {Ric}_ M(\nu ,\nu )+\partial _\nu h)f^2\right)dA. \]

The normal derivative of the ambient prescribed function is part of the potential.

Proof

The transport formula for the bulk integral gives $\frac d{dt}\int _{\Omega _ t}h=\int _{\Sigma _ t}hf$. Subtract this from the first area variation. At $H-h=0$, differentiation leaves $\int f(\dot H-f\partial _\nu h)$. Substitute Proposition 11.3.4 and integrate $-f\Delta f$ by parts. A tangential initial velocity differentiates $H-h=0$ along the hypersurface and contributes zero. The argument concerns a smooth critical boundary and supplies no regularity theorem for nonsmooth minimizers. $\square$
