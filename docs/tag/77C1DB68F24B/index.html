Proposition 4.14.3 (An upper density bound controls the restricted Radon measure)77C1DB68F24B

Proposition 4.14.3 (An upper density bound controls the restricted Radon measure).77C1DB68F24B Let $\mu $ be a positive Radon measure on $U$, and let $S\subset U$ be Borel. Suppose that for every $x\in S$ and all sufficiently small $r>0$,

\[ \mu (B_ r(x))\leq C\omega _ m r^ m, \]

where $1\leq m\leq n$ and $C$ is fixed, but the permitted scale may depend on $x$. Then for every Borel $A\subset S$,

\[ \mu (A)\leq C4^ m\mathcal H^ m(A). \]

This is the diameter-cover comparison used after equation (13) in [Sim18], p. 100, with a convenient enlarged radius.

Proof

Let $S_ j$ consist of points of $S$ for which $\operatorname {dist}(x,\mathbb R^ n\setminus U)>1/j$ and the stated upper bound holds for all $0<r<1/j$. These are Borel sets: it suffices to test positive rational radii, because continuity from below of open balls passes the bound to every real radius. They increase with $j$ and cover $S$.

Fix $j$ and cover $A\cap S_ j$ by sets $Q_ i$ of diameters $d_ i<\delta <1/(2j)$, with total normalized cost within $\varepsilon $ of $\mathcal H^ m_\delta (A\cap S_ j)$. Discard sets missing $A\cap S_ j$, and choose $x_ i\in Q_ i\cap A\cap S_ j$ for the others. When $d_ i>0$, $Q_ i\subset B_{2d_ i}(x_ i)$, and

\[ \mu (B_{2d_ i}(x_ i))\leq C\omega _ m(2d_ i)^ m =C4^ m\omega _ m(d_ i/2)^ m. \]

When $d_ i=0$, $Q_ i$ contains at most the single point $x_ i$, and $\mu (\{ x_ i\} )=0$ by the assumed upper bound and $m>0$. Subadditivity gives

\[ \mu (A\cap S_ j)\leq C4^ m \bigl (\mathcal H^ m_\delta (A\cap S_ j)+\varepsilon \bigr ) \leq C4^ m\bigl (\mathcal H^ m(A)+\varepsilon \bigr ). \]

If the Hausdorff measure on the right is infinite the result is immediate; otherwise such finite-cost covers exist. Let $\varepsilon \downarrow 0$ and then $j\to \infty $, using continuity from below of $\mu $. This proves the assertion without assuming a uniform radius on all of $A$. $\square$
