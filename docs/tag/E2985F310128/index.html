Lemma 4.5.2 (Removing one singular point from a flux identity)E2985F310128

Lemma 4.5.2 (Removing one singular point from a flux identity).E2985F310128 Let $D\geq 2$, $a\in U$, and let $X$ be bounded and $C^1$ on $U\setminus \{ a\} $, with its classical divergence in $L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}(U)$. Give $X(a)$ any bounded value. For every locally finite-perimeter set $F$ and $\phi \in C_ c^1(U)$,

\[ \int _ F\bigl (\phi \operatorname {div}X+D\phi \cdot X\bigr )\, dx =\int _{\partial ^*F}\phi X\cdot \nu _ F\, d\mathcal H^{D-1}. \]
Proof

Let $\eta _\varepsilon $ be zero on $B_\varepsilon (a)$, one outside $B_{2\varepsilon }(a)$, with $|D\eta _\varepsilon |\leq C/\varepsilon $. Apply Theorem 4.1.5 to the compactly supported $C^1$ field $\eta _\varepsilon \phi X$. The extra bulk term has magnitude at most $C\varepsilon ^{-1}\mathcal L^ D(B_{2\varepsilon })=O(\varepsilon ^{D-1})$, tending to zero. All other bulk terms converge by boundedness and the assumed integrability of the divergence. De Giorgi's representation implies that $|D\chi _ F|$ has no point atoms when $D\geq 2$. Dominated convergence therefore also applies to the boundary flux. This proves the formula without a point-supported divergence term. $\square$
