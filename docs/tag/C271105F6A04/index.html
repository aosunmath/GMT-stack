Corollary 17.1.5 (Finite total energy in dimensions above two)C271105F6A04

Corollary 17.1.5 (Finite total energy in dimensions above two).C271105F6A04 If $m\ge 3$ and $u:\mathbb R^ m\to S^{N-1}$ is a stationary weak harmonic map with $\int _{\mathbb R^ m}|Du|^2<\infty $, then $u$ is constant almost everywhere. The same conclusion holds for a smooth harmonic map under this energy hypothesis. Dimension two admits nonconstant finite-energy examples.

Proof

For any fixed $r>0$, monotonicity gives $0\le \Theta _ u(0,r)\le \Theta _ u(0,R)\le \frac12R^{2-m}\int _{\mathbb R^ m}|Du|^2\to 0$. Thus $Du=0$ almost everywhere on each centered ball and hence on all of $\mathbb R^ m$. The standard line characterization of Sobolev functions on connected domains gives an almost-everywhere constant representative. Smooth harmonic maps satisfy stationarity by Proposition 17.1.2. Example 16.9.3 below gives the dimension-two contrast. $\square$
