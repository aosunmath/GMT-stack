Lemma 6.5.1 (A distributionally constant function is constant)CB1D1F5465B7

Lemma 6.5.1 (A distributionally constant function is constant).CB1D1F5465B7 Let $W\subset \mathbb R^ m$ be connected and open, and let $h\in L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}(W)$. If

\[ \int _ W h\, \partial _ i\phi \, dx=0 \quad (\phi \in C_ c^\infty (W),\ i=1,\ldots ,m), \]

then $h$ is equal almost everywhere to one constant.

Proof

On a ball compactly contained in $W$, mollification gives smooth functions whose derivatives vanish on smaller concentric balls. Each mollification is constant on each such smaller ball. Its $L^1$ convergence shows that the limiting function is almost everywhere constant there as well: the constants converge, as seen by taking their averages. Overlapping balls have the same constant. Any two points of a connected open subset of Euclidean space can be joined by a finite chain of overlapping balls, so the local constants agree. This supplies the distributional step in [Minter], Lemma 0.7. $\square$
