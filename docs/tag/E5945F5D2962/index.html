Corollary 2.5.11 (Ball densities identify an absolutely continuous part)E5945F5D2962

Corollary 2.5.11 (Ball densities identify an absolutely continuous part).E5945F5D2962 Suppose a nonnegative Radon measure is written $\mu =f\, dx+\nu $ with $0\leq f\in L^1_{\rm loc}(\mathbb R^ n)$ and $\nu \perp \mathcal L^ n$. Then

\[ \lim _{r\downarrow 0}\frac{\mu (B_ r(x))}{\omega _ n r^ n}=f(x) \quad \text{for }\mathcal L^ n\text{-almost every }x. \]
Proof

Add the limits supplied by Theorem 2.5.6 and Proposition 2.5.10, outside the union of their null exceptional sets. The decomposition is a hypothesis of this statement; existence of the Lebesgue decomposition and the Radon–Nikodym theorem are not proved by this argument. Compare [Sim18], Section 4, especially Theorem 4.18. $\square$
