Lemma 4.4.1 (Layer cake and the distance between level sets)9CC91C075013

Lemma 4.4.1 (Layer cake and the distance between level sets).9CC91C075013 For real numbers $a,b$,

\[ a=\int _{\mathbb R}(\chi _{\{ a>t\} }-\chi _{\{ 0>t\} })\, dt, \qquad |a-b|=\int _{\mathbb R}|\chi _{\{ a>t\} }-\chi _{\{ b>t\} }|\, dt. \]

Consequently, for measurable finite-valued functions $u,v$ on an open set $W$,

\[ \| u-v\| _{L^1(W)} =\int _{\mathbb R}|(\{ u>t\} \mathbin {\triangle }\{ v>t\} )\cap W|\, dt, \]

including infinite values.

Proof

The indicators in the first formula differ only on the interval between $0$ and $a$, with the appropriate sign. The absolute difference in the second is the indicator of the interval between $a$ and $b$, up to its endpoints. Integrate the pointwise identity using Tonelli. This is the level-set identity used in [Giu84], the proof of Theorem 1.23, pp. 20–21. $\square$
