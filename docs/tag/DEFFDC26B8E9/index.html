Proposition 11.1.2 (The smooth CMC Euler-Lagrange equation)DEFFDC26B8E9

Proposition 11.1.2 (The smooth CMC Euler-Lagrange equation).DEFFDC26B8E9 For a smooth compact region $\Omega \subset M$ with smooth boundary, a variation with boundary velocity $f\nu $ satisfies

\[ \delta \mathcal A^ c(f\nu )=\int _{\partial \Omega }(H-c)f\, dA. \]

It is critical under all smooth variations if and only if $H=c$ everywhere.

Proof

Proposition 7.1.2 gives the area derivative $\int Hf$. The divergence theorem applied to an extension $X$ of $f\nu $ gives the volume derivative $\int _\Omega \operatorname {div}_ M X=\int _{\partial \Omega }f$. Subtract $c$ times this derivative. Every smooth $f$ extends to a variation, so vanishing for all $f$ is equivalent to $H-c=0$. $\square$
