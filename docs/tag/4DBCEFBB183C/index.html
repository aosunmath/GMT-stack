Proposition 11.6.3 (Nullity of a geodesic sphere)4DBCEFBB183C

Proposition 11.6.3 (Nullity of a geodesic sphere).4DBCEFBB183C The constrained Jacobi nullspace of $\Sigma _ r$ has dimension $n+1$ and consists of the first spherical harmonics for its round induced metric. The unrestricted Jacobi nullspace is the same space.

Proof

The potential is the positive constant $q=n/s_ c(r)^2$. By Proposition 11.5.7, the nullspace is the Laplace eigenspace with eigenvalue $q$. Proposition 7.19.5, scaled to the round induced radius $s_ c(r)$ as in Corollary 7.19.6, identifies every weak eigenfunction with a harmonic degree and gives eigenvalues $\ell (\ell +n-1)/s_ c(r)^2$. The value $q$ occurs exactly at $\ell =1$. Example 7.19.7 identifies this space as the restrictions of linear functions on the intrinsic sphere model; it has dimension $n+1$ and zero mean. Constants have Laplace eigenvalue zero, so they add no unrestricted Jacobi fields. This proves both nullity statements using the local spectral decomposition. It concerns infinitesimal nullity and does not assert uniqueness of a nonlinear CMC deformation. $\square$
