Theorem 7.14.2 (The first-power Sobolev inequality)3E1913FF6CC7

Theorem 7.14.2 (The first-power Sobolev inequality).3E1913FF6CC7 For every $u\in C_ c^1(\mathbb R^ n)$,

\[ \| u\| _{L^{n/(n-1)}} \leq \prod _{i=1}^ n\| \partial _ i u\| _{L^1}^{1/n} \leq \| Du\| _{L^1}. \]

This is the $p=1$ input in [SimonPDE], Lecture 17, Lemma 2, pp. 200–201; no sharp constant is claimed.

Proof

On every coordinate line, the fundamental theorem of calculus and compact support give

\[ |u(x)|\leq F_ i(\widehat x_ i),\qquad F_ i(\widehat x_ i)=\int _{\mathbb R}|\partial _ i u(x)|\, dx_ i. \]

Multiply these $n$ inequalities and raise the result to the power $1/(n-1)$. Lemma 7.14.1 gives

\[ \int |u|^{n/(n-1)}\leq \prod _ i\| \partial _ i u\| _1^{1/(n-1)}. \]

Taking the power $(n-1)/n$ proves the first inequality. Each $\| \partial _ i u\| _1$ is at most $\| Du\| _1$, proving the second. If one coordinate derivative vanishes identically, the same argument forces $u=0$, consistently with compact support. $\square$
