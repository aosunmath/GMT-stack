Lemma 7.15.2 (Commuting derivatives of a covariant two-tensor)25DCA2C10AD1

Lemma 7.15.2 (Commuting derivatives of a covariant two-tensor).25DCA2C10AD1 For a smooth covariant two-tensor $T$, define $(\nabla ^2T)(X,Y)=\nabla _ X\nabla _ YT-\nabla _{\nabla _ XY}T$. Then

\[ \bigl ((\nabla ^2T)(X,Y)-(\nabla ^2T)(Y,X)\bigr )(Z,W) =-T(R(X,Y)Z,W)-T(Z,R(X,Y)W). \]
Proof

Apply $XY-YX-[X,Y]$ to the scalar function $T(Z,W)$; the result is zero. Expand each derivative by the Leibniz rule for the connection. Terms with one derivative on each of two factors cancel in pairs. The terms with two derivatives on $Z$ and $W$ are respectively $T(R(X,Y)Z,W)$ and $T(Z,R(X,Y)W)$. The remaining terms give the derivative commutator on $T$. Moving the two curvature terms to the other side proves the signs in the formula. Torsion-freeness gives $\nabla _ XY-\nabla _ YX=[X,Y]$. $\square$
