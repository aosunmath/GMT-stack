Lemma 3.4.4 (Vanishing distribution tests determine a locally integrable function)7737EDA4D935

Lemma 3.4.4 (Vanishing distribution tests determine a locally integrable function).7737EDA4D935 If $h\in L^1_{\rm loc}(U)$ and $\int _ Uh\phi =0$ for every $\phi \in C_ c^\infty (U)$, then $h=0$ almost everywhere.

Proof

Fix a smooth nonnegative mollifier of integral one. At a point $x$ whose distance from the complement of $U$ exceeds $\varepsilon $, the function $y\mapsto \rho _\varepsilon (x-y)$ is an admissible test, so the local mollification is zero. At almost every point, Theorem 2.5.6 and Proposition 2.5.9, localized inside $U$, say that these mollifications tend to $h(x)$. Thus $h$ vanishes almost everywhere. $\square$
