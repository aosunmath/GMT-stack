Proposition 6.8.1 (Annular identity with mean-curvature error)11614439894F

Proposition 6.8.1 (Annular identity with mean-curvature error).11614439894F Let $\overline{B_ R(a)}\subset U$ and $0<\sigma <\rho <R$. With $f_ a,T_ a$ as in Definition 6.6.1, define

\[ \gamma _{\sigma ,\rho }(r)=\begin{cases} \max \{ r,\sigma \} ^{-m}-\rho ^{-m},& r<\rho ,\\ 0,& r\geq \rho . \end{cases} \]

Then

\[ f_ a(\rho )-f_ a(\sigma ) =\int _{G_ m(B_\rho (a)\setminus B_\sigma (a))}T_ a\, dV +\frac1m\int \gamma _{\sigma ,\rho }(|x-a|)H(x)\cdot (x-a)\, d\mu . \]

In particular

\[ f_ a(\rho )-f_ a(\sigma )\geq -\Lambda \int _\sigma ^\rho f_ a(t)\, dt. \]
Proof

Use the cutoff and divergence computation in the proof of Proposition 6.6.3. First variation is now $-\int \gamma H\cdot (x-a)\, d\mu $, giving the stated sign after rearrangement. Approximate arbitrary radii from below through continuity radii as before; the bounded curvature and local finiteness justify dominated convergence for the extra term.

The layer-cake formula

\[ \gamma _{\sigma ,\rho }(r)=\int _\sigma ^\rho m t^{-m-1}\boldsymbol 1_{\{ r<t\} }\, dt \]

shows, by Tonelli, that the magnitude of a negative curvature contribution is at most

\[ \Lambda \int _\sigma ^\rho t^{-m-1} \int _{B_ t(a)}|x-a|\, d\mu \, dt \leq \Lambda \int _\sigma ^\rho f_ a(t)\, dt. \]

The radial-tilt term is nonnegative. No differentiability of the ball-mass function was used. $\square$
