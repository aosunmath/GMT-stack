Proposition 5.5.5 (Weak limits of calibrated currents retain mass)4C4FD4B0981B

Proposition 5.5.5 (Weak limits of calibrated currents retain mass).4C4FD4B0981B Let $T_ j$ be calibrated finite-mass real $m$-currents with supports in a common compact $K\subset U$, and suppose $T_ j\rightharpoonup T$. Then $T$ is calibrated, is supported in $K$, and

\[ \mathbf M(T_ j)\longrightarrow \mathbf M(T). \]

This conclusion does not by itself prove integrality of $T$.

Proof

A fixed cutoff equal to one near $K$ gives $\mathbf M(T_ j)=T_ j(\omega )\to T(\omega )$, a finite limit. Tests outside $K$ show $\operatorname {spt}T\subset K$. Mass lower semicontinuity and the comass inequality give

\[ \mathbf M(T)\leq \liminf _ j\mathbf M(T_ j)=T(\omega )\leq \mathbf M(T). \]

Thus all terms agree. The finite mass of $T$ follows from the first inequality; the last evaluation is justified by that finite mass. Integrality requires an additional closure theorem, such as Theorem 5.1.6 with its boundary-mass hypothesis. $\square$
