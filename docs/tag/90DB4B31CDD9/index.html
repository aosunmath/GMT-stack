Theorem 8.6.8 (A continuous pull-tight homotopy on boundary cycles)90DB4B31CDD9

Theorem 8.6.8 (A continuous pull-tight homotopy on boundary cycles).90DB4B31CDD9 Let $M^{n+1}$ be smooth, closed and connected, $n\geq 1$, and let $C,\varepsilon >0$. On the mass-at-most-$C$ part of $\mathcal Z_ n^0(M;\mathbb Z_2)$ there is a jointly $\mathbf F$-continuous homotopy $H(s,T)$ which starts at $T$, fixes $T$ whenever $|T|$ is stationary, has nonincreasing mass in $s$, and satisfies

\[ \mathbf M(H(1,T))\leq \mathbf M(T)-D(|T|),\qquad \mathbf F(H(s,T),T)\leq \varepsilon , \]

where $D$ is continuous on $K_ C$ and positive precisely off $S_ C$. This proves the continuous deformation stated in [MNIndex], Section 2.8, from the current operations and ODE inputs specified above.

Proof

Choose $\tau _0$ using Lemma 8.6.7 and define $H(s,T)=(\varphi _{s\tau (|T|)}^{|T|})_\# T$. Diffeomorphic pushforward preserves integral mod-two cycles and commutes with boundary. In particular if $T=\partial U$, its image is the boundary of the image of $U$, so remains in the boundary component. The Jacobian formula with no cancellation identifies $|H(s,T)|=\mathscr H(s,|T|)$. Proposition 8.6.5 supplies mass monotonicity, the drop, and the fixed-point properties. Lemma 8.6.7 supplies the distance estimate. Finally $\mathbf F$ convergence of cycles gives weak convergence of their varifolds, hence $C^1$ convergence of the selected diffeomorphisms. Lemma 8.5.5 proves joint $\mathbf F$ continuity. No discrete mass-fineness assertion is used or proved. $\square$
