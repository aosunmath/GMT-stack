Proposition 6.11.2 (First variation and density of a ray junction)F9B23C2936BA

Proposition 6.11.2 (First variation and density of a ray junction).F9B23C2936BA For the cone of Definition 6.11.1,

\[ \delta C(X)=-X(0)\cdot \sum _{i=1}^ kq_ iv_ i. \]

Thus $C$ is stationary if and only if $\sum _ iq_ iv_ i=0$. Its vertex density is $\frac12\sum _ iq_ i$, and its density at a nonzero point of $R_{v_ i}$ is $q_ i$.

Proof

On one ray, tangential divergence is $\frac d{dt}(X(tv_ i)\cdot v_ i)$. Integration from zero to infinity gives $-X(0)\cdot v_ i$ by compact support. Arbitrary values of $X(0)$ prove the equivalence. The centered ball has mass $r\sum _ iq_ i$ and $\omega _1=2$. Near a nonzero point of one ray, all other finitely many rays are absent, so the local density is its multiplicity. $\square$
