Proposition 8.7.4 (Near-maximal slices of a pulled-tight sequence approach stationarity)E310F2DBD8F7

Proposition 8.7.4 (Near-maximal slices of a pulled-tight sequence approach stationarity).E310F2DBD8F7 For a pulled-tight minimizing sequence with finite width $L$, every $\eta >0$ admits $\delta >0$ and $i_0$ such that

\[ i\geq i_0,\quad \mathbf M(\Phi _ i(x))\geq L-\delta \quad \Longrightarrow \quad d(|\Phi _ i(x)|,S_ C)<\eta , \]

where $C$ bounds all masses. No rate in terms of $L-\mathbf M(\Phi _ i(x))$ is asserted.

Proof

The open $\eta $-neighborhood of $S_ C$ contains the critical set because the sequence is pulled-tight. Apply Lemma 8.5.4 to this neighborhood. The compactness argument supplies qualitative thresholds, with no quantitative lower bound on the auxiliary defect from first variation alone. $\square$
