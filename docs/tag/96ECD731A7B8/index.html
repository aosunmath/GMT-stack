Proposition 13.2.2 (The energy gap controls distance to the minimizer).96ECD731A7B8 For the minimizer $u$ and any $v\in K$,

\[ \mathcal E_ f(v)-\mathcal E_ f(u)\geq \frac12\| D(v-u)\| _{L^2(U)}^2. \]

In particular every minimizing sequence converges strongly to $u$ in $H^1(U)$.

Proof

Expand the quadratic energy as in Proposition 13.1.2. Its linear part is nonnegative by the variational inequality, leaving the displayed bound. Poincare controls the $L^2$ norm of $v-u$, since the boundary traces agree. Thus convergence of energies to the minimum implies strong convergence without taking a further subsequence. $\square$
