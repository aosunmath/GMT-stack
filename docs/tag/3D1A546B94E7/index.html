Lemma 14.20.6 (An area-excess dissipation inequality)3D1A546B94E7

Lemma 14.20.6 (An area-excess dissipation inequality).3D1A546B94E7 Let $u$ be any smooth function on the flat torus with $1\leq v\leq V$, and set

\[ \mathcal F(u)=\mathcal A(u)-\operatorname {Vol}(\mathbb T^ n),\qquad \lambda _1=\inf _{\substack {f\in H^1,\ \int f=0\\ f\ne 0}} \frac{\int |Df|^2}{\int f^2}>0. \]

Then

\[ \frac1{V+1}\int |Du|^2\leq \mathcal F(u)\leq \frac12\int |Du|^2, \qquad \int H^2v\, dx\geq \frac{2\lambda _1}{V^2}\mathcal F(u). \]
Proof

Use $v-1=|Du|^2/(v+1)$ to obtain the two-sided area comparison. If $Du=0$, the final estimate is immediate. Otherwise integration by parts gives

\[ \int H(u-\bar u)\, dx=\int \frac{|Du|^2}{v}\, dx \geq V^{-1}\int |Du|^2. \]

Cauchy–Schwarz and Corollary 7.18.3 bound its square above by $\lambda _1^{-1}\int H^2\int |Du|^2$. Cancel the positive gradient integral to get $\int H^2\geq \lambda _1V^{-2}\int |Du|^2$. Since $v\geq 1$ and $\int |Du|^2\geq 2\mathcal F$, this proves the dissipation bound. No critical-point assumption was made for this inequality. $\square$
