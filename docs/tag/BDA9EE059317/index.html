Proposition 5.6.3 (Holomorphic disks give calibrated integral currents)BDA9EE059317

Proposition 5.6.3 (Holomorphic disks give calibrated integral currents).BDA9EE059317 Let $D\subset \mathbb C$ be a bounded domain with smooth boundary, and suppose $F$ is holomorphic on a neighborhood of $\overline D$. Orient $D$ by $ds\wedge dt$ and put $T=F_\# \llbracket D\rrbracket $. Then $T$ is a compactly supported integral current,

\[ \partial T=F_\# \llbracket \partial D\rrbracket , \qquad \mathbf M(T)=T(\omega )=\int _ D|F'|^2\, ds\, dt. \]

It minimizes mass among compactly supported finite-mass real two-currents in $\mathbb C^ k$ with the same boundary. The formula counts multiplicity if $F$ is not injective.

Proof

The map is smooth and Lipschitz on a neighborhood of the compact closure; Theorem 5.3.9 gives integrality and the boundary formula. The pushforward representation gives $\mathbf M(T)\leq \int _ DJ_2DF$. Proposition 5.6.2 gives $T(\omega )=\int _ DJ_2DF$, whereas the comass bound gives $T(\omega )\leq \mathbf M(T)$. Equality follows, showing in particular that orientation cancellation cannot reduce the mass. Corollary 5.5.4 gives the minimizing property. Compactly supported cutoff forms justify each evaluation. $\square$
