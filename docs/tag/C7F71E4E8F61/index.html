Proposition 14.4.1 (Transport of a test function along a closed flow)C7F71E4E8F61

Proposition 14.4.1 (Transport of a test function along a closed flow).C7F71E4E8F61 Let $\Sigma _ t$ be a smooth closed mean curvature flow in $\mathbb R^{n+1}$, and let $\phi (x,t)$ be smooth in an ambient neighborhood of its spacetime track. Then

\[ \frac{d}{dt}\int _{\Sigma _ t}\phi \, d\mu _ t =\int _{\Sigma _ t}\left(\partial _ t\phi +\nabla \phi \cdot \vec H-\phi |\vec H|^2\right)d\mu _ t. \]

Here $\partial _ t\phi $ is the ambient partial derivative, with the spatial point held fixed.

Proof

Choose a normal parametrization. The chain rule gives $\frac{d}{dt}\phi (F(p,t),t)=\partial _ t\phi +\nabla \phi \cdot \vec H$, and Proposition 14.3.2 gives $\partial _ t d\mu =-|\vec H|^2d\mu $. Adding the two contributions proves the formula. A tangential velocity adds a tangential divergence, whose integral vanishes on a closed hypersurface. Compare [WhiMCF15], Sections 6 and 9. $\square$
