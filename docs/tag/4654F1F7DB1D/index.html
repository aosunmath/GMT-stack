Corollary 4.14.1 (The perimeter measure is concentrated on the reduced boundary)4654F1F7DB1D

Corollary 4.14.1 (The perimeter measure is concentrated on the reduced boundary).4654F1F7DB1D For a locally finite-perimeter set $E$ in $U$,

\[ |D\chi _ E|(U\setminus \partial ^*E)=0. \]

This is the concentration assertion at the beginning of the proof of [Sim18], Chapter 3, Theorem 4.4, p. 97.

Proof

Put $\mu =|D\chi _ E|$ and write $D\chi _ E=-\nu \mu $ with $|\nu |=1$ almost everywhere. The bounded polar field belongs locally to $L^1(\mu )$. Theorem 2.6.5, applied to its finitely many components, gives

\[ \frac{D\chi _ E(B_ r(x))}{\mu (B_ r(x))}\longrightarrow -\nu (x) \quad \text{for }\mu \text{-almost every }x. \]

The complement of the support of $\mu $ is a countable union of open balls with zero measure, so almost every such point also lies in that support. Since the limit has length one, these points belong to the reduced boundary by definition. Its Borel measurability follows from Lemma 4.13.2, so the displayed measure statement is well defined. $\square$
