Theorem 6.3.10 (Dimension bound for the strata)20B7E5509A93

Theorem 6.3.10 (Dimension bound for the strata).20B7E5509A93 For every stationary integral $n$-varifold and $0\leq j\leq n$,

\[ \dim _{\mathcal H}\mathcal S^ j(V)\leq j. \]

This is the qualitative stratification theorem stated in [Minter], Theorem 1.7, p. 17. Its covering proof is given below. The estimate alone does not remove the top stratum or give the codimension-seven bound available under stronger minimizing or stability hypotheses.

Proof

Fix $\beta >0$ and choose $\delta >0$ small enough for Proposition 6.10.3 in ambient dimension $n+k$ and plane dimension $j$. Proposition 6.10.5, proved by tangent-cone compactness and density upper semicontinuity, decomposes $\mathcal S^ j(V)$ into countably many sets with one-sided $\delta $ approximation by $j$-planes at all sufficiently small scales. Each piece has zero $\mathcal H^{j+\beta }$ outer measure by the covering proposition. Countable subadditivity gives the same conclusion for the stratum. Since $\beta >0$ is arbitrary, its Hausdorff dimension is at most $j$. This proves a dimension bound; it does not prove finite $\mathcal H^ j$ measure, rectifiability, or uniqueness of tangent cones. $\square$
