Definition 6.1.2 (Rectifiable and integral varifolds)46E46AD2EF79

Definition 6.1.2 (Rectifiable and integral varifolds).46E46AD2EF79 Let $M\subset U$ be $\mathcal{H}^ m$-measurable and countably $m$-rectifiable, with locally finite $\mathcal{H}^ m$ measure in $U$. Let $\theta :M\to (0,\infty )$ be measurable and satisfy $\int _{M\cap K}\theta \, d\mathcal{H}^ m<\infty $ for every compact $K\subset U$. The associated rectifiable varifold $V(M,\theta )$ is determined by

\[ \int \varphi (x,S)\, dV(M,\theta )(x,S) =\int _ M\varphi (x,T_ xM)\theta (x)\, d\mathcal{H}^ m(x). \]

It is integral if $\theta $ is integer-valued almost everywhere. Its weight is $\theta \mathcal{H}^ m\llcorner M$. The tangent planes exist almost everywhere by Theorem 3.1.4; changing the integrand on the exceptional null set has no effect. See [Sim18], Chapter 4, Section 1, and Chapter 8, p. 236.
