Theorem 12.4.3 (Recovery along a smooth compact interface)A3FF775127BE

Theorem 12.4.3 (Recovery along a smooth compact interface).A3FF775127BE Suppose $E\Subset U$ is open with $C^2$ boundary. For nonempty $E$, let $d$ be its signed distance, positive inside, and put $u_\varepsilon =q(d/\varepsilon )$ with $q(t)=\tanh (t/\sqrt2)$; for empty $E$, put $u_\varepsilon =-1$. Then $u_\varepsilon \in H^1(U)\cap L^4(U)$, $|u_\varepsilon |\leq 1$, and

\[ u_\varepsilon \to u_ E\text{ in every finite }L^ p(U),\qquad E_\varepsilon (u_\varepsilon ;U)\to c_ WP(E;U). \]

Moreover the energy measures converge to $c_ W\mathcal H^{n-1}\llcorner \partial E$ on compactly supported continuous tests.

Proof

Signed distance is Lipschitz with constant one, so the composition is Sobolev and bounded. It converges to the two phases off the measure-zero boundary. In the tube of Lemma 12.4.2, $|Dd|=1$ and the energy is

\[ \int _\Sigma \int _{-\rho /\varepsilon }^{\rho /\varepsilon } (q'(s))^2J(y,-\varepsilon s)\, ds\, d\mathcal H^{n-1}(y). \]

The bounded Jacobian and integrability of $(q')^2$ allow dominated convergence, giving $c_ W\mathcal H^{n-1}(\Sigma )$. Outside the tube, $|d|\geq \rho $. Both $(q'(d/\varepsilon ))^2$ and $W(q(d/\varepsilon ))$ are bounded by $Ce^{-c\rho /\varepsilon }$; the gradient bound $|Dd|\leq 1$ therefore makes the remaining energy at most $C|U|\varepsilon ^{-1}e^{-c\rho /\varepsilon }\to 0$. Insert a test function in the same calculation to obtain the measure limit. Bounded convergence proves all the stated phase convergences. This is the smooth-interface construction in [ChAC], Proposition 3.6, with the tail estimate retained. Empty $E$ is handled by $u_\varepsilon \equiv -1$. $\square$
