Theorem 4.5.8 (Strict perimeter minimization by the equal-factor Simons cone)BE9B00DE4A34

Theorem 4.5.8 (Strict perimeter minimization by the equal-factor Simons cone).BE9B00DE4A34 Let $k\geq 4$ and $E_ k=\{ |x|<|y|\} \subset \mathbb R^{2k}$. For every set $F$ of locally finite perimeter such that $\chi _ F=\chi _{E_ k}$ almost everywhere outside a compact set $K$, and every relatively compact open $W$ containing $K$,

\[ P(E_ k;W)\leq P(F;W). \]

The inequality is strict if $\mathcal L^{2k}(E_ k\mathbin {\triangle }F)>0$. This is the finite-perimeter-set version of [ChMin], Theorem 17.1 and Exercise 17.1, pp. 48–50, extending the source's worked one-sided comparison to arbitrary compact perturbations.

Proof

Proposition 4.5.6 supplies locally finite perimeter and its normal. The field in Proposition 4.5.7 saturates that normal flux, has norm one, and has integrable divergence near its only singular point. Set its value at the origin to zero. Lemma 4.5.2 therefore justifies its use for every competitor $F$. The divergence is negative in $E_ k$ and positive in its complement except on $C_ k$, a Lebesgue-null set. It is a signed subcalibration with the strict sign conditions. Corollary 4.5.5 gives both claims. This comparison class is that of finite-perimeter sets; an extension to all integral currents with arbitrary multiplicity would require an additional decomposition argument. $\square$
