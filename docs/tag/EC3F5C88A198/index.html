Theorem 2.1.3 (Local compactness of Radon measures)EC3F5C88A198

Theorem 2.1.3 (Local compactness of Radon measures).EC3F5C88A198 Let $\mu _ j$ be nonnegative Radon measures on $U$ and suppose that

\[ \sup _ j\mu _ j(K)<\infty \qquad \text{for every compact }K\subset U. \]

There are a subsequence $\mu _{j_ k}$ and a Radon measure $\mu $ on $U$ such that $\mu _{j_ k}\rightharpoonup \mu $ in $U$.

See [Sim18], Chapter 1, Section 5, Theorem 5.15, pp. 38–39. The Euclidean countable-test argument and positive representation theorem are developed below.

Proof

Lemma 2.4.2 extracts a subsequence and a positive linear functional $L$ such that $\int f\, d\mu _{j_ k}\to L(f)$ for every $f\in C_ c(U)$. Theorem 2.3.7 gives a Radon measure $\mu $ with $L(f)=\int f\, d\mu $. This is exactly the convergence in Definition 2.1.2. The countable family exists because $U$ is a Euclidean open set; we do not infer sequential extraction from weak-star compactness on a general nonmetrizable space. $\square$
