Theorem 7.5.5 (Local boundedness of weak solutions)C56302366B3D

Theorem 7.5.5 (Local boundedness of weak solutions).C56302366B3D Let $v\in H^1(B_{2R}(a))$ solve the equation in Definition 7.5.2. Then

\[ \operatorname *{ess\, sup}_{B_ R(a)}|v| \leq C\left(R^{-n}\int _{B_{2R}(a)}|v|^2\right)^{1/2}, \]

where $C=C(n,\Lambda /\lambda )$. This is [SimonPDE], Chapter 18, Theorem 2, p. 212, with zero forcing and lower-order terms. The local iteration proof is supplied below.

Proof

By Lemma 7.13.2, $v_+$ and $(-v)_+$ are nonnegative weak subsolutions of the same zero-lower-order equation. Apply Theorem 7.13.5 to each. Their $L^2$ norms are at most that of $v$, and $|v|$ is their pointwise maximum. This gives the displayed estimate. The supporting argument uses truncated power tests, the external Sobolev gain, an integrability-improvement step and a convergent product of iteration constants; see Lemmas 7.13.3 and 7.13.4. $\square$
