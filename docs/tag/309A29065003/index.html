Proposition 4.4.2 (Coarea for smooth superlevel sets)309A29065003

Proposition 4.4.2 (Coarea for smooth superlevel sets).309A29065003 If $u\in C^\infty (U)$ and $W\subset U$ is open, then

\[ \int _ W|\nabla u|\, dx=\int _{\mathbb R}P(\{ u>t\} ;W)\, dt. \]

For every regular value $t$, the superlevel boundary inside $U$ is the smooth level hypersurface, and its outward normal is $-\nabla u/|\nabla u|$.

Proof

At a regular value, the implicit function theorem and the local divergence theorem give $P(\{ u>t\} ;W)=\mathcal H^{n-1}(W\cap \{ u=t\} )$. The outward normal points toward decreasing $u$. Sard's theorem excludes the nonregular values up to a null set. Apply Theorem 3.2.4 with $f=u$, whose coarea Jacobian is $|\nabla u|$. Exhaustion handles noncompact $W$ and possibly infinite integrals. Compare the smooth step in [Giu84], Theorem 1.23. $\square$
