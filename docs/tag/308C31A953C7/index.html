Theorem 13.4.7 (Monneau monotonicity at a singular point)308C31A953C7

Theorem 13.4.7 (Monneau monotonicity at a singular point).308C31A953C7 Let $u$ be a normalized obstacle solution with zero singular and $\overline{B_ R}\subset U$. For every quadratic model $p=p_ A$, the function $M(r,u,p)$ is locally absolutely continuous and nondecreasing. More precisely, with $w=u-p$,

\[ M'(r)=\frac2r\bigl (W(r,u)-W(r,p)\bigr ) +\frac{2}{r^{n+3}}\int _{B_ r}p\chi _{\{ u=0\} }\, dx \geq 0 \]

for almost every $r\in (0,R)$.

Proof

Existence of one quadratic blow-up and Proposition 13.4.5 imply $W(0+,u)=W(r,p)=\omega _ n/(2(n+2))$. Thus the first term in the claimed derivative is nonnegative by Weiss monotonicity.

Because $\Delta p=1$ and $x\cdot Dp=2p$, expansion of the energy and integration by parts cancel the cross terms, leaving

\[ W(r,u)-W(r,p)=r^{-n-2}\int _{B_ r}|Dw|^2 -2r^{-n-3}\int _{\partial B_ r}w^2. \]

Also $\Delta w=-\chi _{\{ u=0\} }$ and $w=-p$ on contact, so $w\Delta w=p\chi _{\{ u=0\} }\geq 0$. Another integration by parts yields

\[ r^{-n-3}\int _{\partial B_ r}w(x\cdot Dw-2w) =W(r,u)-W(r,p)+r^{-n-2}\int _{B_ r}w\Delta w. \]

Differentiating the fixed-sphere formula for $M$ gives $M'(r)=2r^{-n-4}\int _{\partial B_ r}w(x\cdot Dw-2w)$. Combining the last two identities proves the formula and its sign. Local absolute continuity follows from $C^{1,1}$ regularity on compact annuli. This is the argument of [Fig18], Lemma 8.4, with the nonnegative terms retained. $\square$
