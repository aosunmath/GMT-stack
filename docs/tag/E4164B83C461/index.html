Corollary 4.5.5 (Perimeter minimality and strictness from a subcalibration)E4164B83C461

Corollary 4.5.5 (Perimeter minimality and strictness from a subcalibration).E4164B83C461 A set admitting a signed subcalibration minimizes perimeter under every compactly supported perturbation by a set of locally finite perimeter. If in addition $\operatorname {div}X<0$ almost everywhere in $E$ and $\operatorname {div}X>0$ almost everywhere outside $E$, then

\[ P(F;W)>P(E;W) \]

for every such competitor with $\mathcal L^ D(E\mathbin {\triangle }F)>0$, in each relatively compact open $W$ containing its compact perturbation support.

Proof

Use Proposition 4.5.4. Under the strict signs, its bulk integrand is strictly positive almost everywhere on the symmetric difference and zero elsewhere. Its integral is positive whenever that difference has positive measure. Equality almost everywhere is the appropriate exception because perimeter depends only on the characteristic function as an $L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}$ class. No nesting assumption between $E$ and $F$ is used. $\square$
