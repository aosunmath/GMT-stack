Theorem 9.4.6 (The Weyl limit is uniform on compact metric families)9EA7E95DFE3F

Theorem 9.4.6 (The Weyl limit is uniform on compact metric families).9EA7E95DFE3F Let $\mathcal G$ be a $C^0$-compact set of smooth Riemannian metrics on the fixed closed connected $M^{n+1}$, $n\geq 1$. Then

\[ \lim _{p\to \infty }\sup _{g\in \mathcal G} \left|p^{-1/(n+1)}\omega _ p(M,g) -a(n)\operatorname {Vol}(M,g)^{n/(n+1)}\right|=0. \]

This is a local uniformity consequence of the pointwise Weyl law in [LMN], Theorem 1.1, and the estimate of [MNS19], Lemma 1.

Proof

Fix $g_0$. Compactness and positive definiteness give common constants $A,B>0$ with $Ag_0\leq g\leq Bg_0$ for every $g\in \mathcal G$. Indeed the smallest and largest relative eigenvalues vary continuously with the metric in $C^0$, and their extrema over $M$ and the compact family are positive and finite. The functions $W_ p(g)=p^{-1/(n+1)}\omega _ p(M,g)$ have a common Lipschitz constant by Theorem 9.4.5. The candidate limit $W(g)=a(n)\operatorname {Vol}(M,g)^{n/(n+1)}$ is continuous: the volume density is the square root of the metric determinant, uniformly bounded above and below on this band. Given $\varepsilon >0$, choose a finite sufficiently fine $C^0$ net $g_1,\ldots ,g_ N$ in $\mathcal G$ so both the common Lipschitz error and the uniform-continuity error for $W$ are below $\varepsilon /3$. The pointwise external Weyl law makes $|W_ p(g_ i)-W(g_ i)|<\varepsilon /3$ for all net points once $p$ is large. The triangle inequality then bounds $|W_ p(g)-W(g)|$ by $\varepsilon $ for every $g\in \mathcal G$. No claim about nonsmooth limiting metrics or derivatives is made. $\square$
