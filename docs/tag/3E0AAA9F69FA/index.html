Lemma 7.3.1 (Disjoint unstable regions consume index)3E0AAA9F69FA

Lemma 7.3.1 (Disjoint unstable regions consume index).3E0AAA9F69FA Let $\Sigma $ be a smooth closed two-sided minimal hypersurface with index at most $I$. Among any $I+1$ open subsets of $\Sigma $ with pairwise disjoint closures, at least one is stable for normal variations compactly supported in that subset.

Proof

Otherwise choose nonzero smooth compactly supported functions $f_1,\ldots ,f_{I+1}$, one in each subset, with $Q(f_ i,f_ i)<0$. The disjoint supports make every cross term $Q(f_ i,f_ j)$ vanish for $i\ne j$. Hence

\[ Q\left(\sum _ i a_ if_ i,\sum _ i a_ if_ i\right)=\sum _ i a_ i^2Q(f_ i,f_ i)<0 \]

for every nonzero coefficient vector. The functions are linearly independent, producing an $(I+1)$-dimensional negative subspace, contrary to the index bound. This is the localization mechanism used in [Sharp17], Lemma 3.1. $\square$
