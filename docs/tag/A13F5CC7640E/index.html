Proposition 12.2.2 (Truncation to the two wells decreases energy)A13F5CC7640E

Proposition 12.2.2 (Truncation to the two wells decreases energy).A13F5CC7640E Let $T(s)=\max \{ -1,\min \{ s,1\} \} $. For finite-energy $u$ on $U$,

\[ E_\varepsilon (T(u);A)\leq E_\varepsilon (u;A) \]

for every Borel $A\subset U$. If a prescribed Sobolev trace already takes values in $[-1,1]$, truncation preserves that trace.

Proof

The Sobolev chain rule gives $|D(T(u))|\leq |Du|$ almost everywhere. The potential is unchanged where $|u|\leq 1$, and becomes zero where $|u|>1$. Thus both energy terms decrease pointwise. Trace compatibility with Lipschitz composition gives the final assertion. This is a useful variational consequence of the potential chosen in [ChAC], Definition 2.1; it does not require the Euler equation. $\square$
