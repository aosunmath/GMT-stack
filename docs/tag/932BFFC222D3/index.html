Proposition 16.6.4 (The two grim-reaper limits backward in time)932BFFC222D3

Proposition 16.6.4 (The two grim-reaper limits backward in time).932BFFC222D3 On every compact subinterval of $(-\pi /2,\pi /2)$, the upper and lower graph branches of the ancient oval satisfy, as $t\to -\infty $,

\[ y_+(x,t)+t-\log 2\longrightarrow \log \cos x, \qquad y_-(x,t)-t+\log 2\longrightarrow -\log \cos x \]

smoothly in $x$. These are the downward and upward grim-reaper profiles, respectively. The convergence is local in the horizontal strip, not uniform through its limiting endpoints.

Proof

The branches are $y_\pm =\pm \operatorname {arcosh}(e^{-t}\cos x)$. For large $z$, the identity $\operatorname {arcosh}z=\log (z+\sqrt{z^2-1})$ gives $\operatorname {arcosh}z=\log (2z)+O(z^{-2})$. On a compact subinterval the factor $\cos x$ stays positive, so substitution gives an $O(e^{2t})$ error with every fixed number of $x$ derivatives. This proves both limits. The signs of their vertical motions agree with Proposition 16.3.5 and reflection across the horizontal axis. This is the backward translator behavior discussed in [BV11], p. 7; the error bound here is derived from the exact inverse-hyperbolic-cosine expression. $\square$
