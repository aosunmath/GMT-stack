Corollary 10.5.5 (Willmore energy gives a Gaussian-area bound)779189C9B6ED

Corollary 10.5.5 (Willmore energy gives a Gaussian-area bound).779189C9B6ED Every smooth closed immersed surface in Euclidean space satisfies, for all centers $y$ and scales $t>0$,

\[ F_{y,t}(M)=\frac1{4\pi t}\int _ Me^{-|F-y|^2/(4t)}d\mu \leq \frac{\mathcal W(F)}\pi . \]

Thus the supremum over centers and scales obeys the same bound. This estimate is not asserted to be sharp.

Proof

For $r\geq 0$, write $e^{-r^2/(4t)}=\int _ r^\infty \frac{s}{2t}e^{-s^2/(4t)}ds$. Tonelli's theorem gives

\[ F_{y,t}=\frac1{8\pi t^2}\int _0^\infty s\, e^{-s^2/(4t)}A(s)\, ds. \]

The choice of open or closed sublevel sets does not affect this integral in $s$. Apply Theorem 10.5.4 and substitute $u=s^2/(4t)$. Since $\int _0^\infty s^3e^{-s^2/(4t)}ds=8t^2$, the result follows. Taking the supremum preserves the inequality. This connects an extrinsic bending-energy bound to the Gaussian functional used in mean curvature flow without claiming that Willmore energy is itself monotone along that flow. $\square$
