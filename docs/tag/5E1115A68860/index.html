Proposition 2.4.3 (Upper semicontinuity on compact sets)5E1115A68860

Proposition 2.4.3 (Upper semicontinuity on compact sets).5E1115A68860 If $\mu _ j\rightharpoonup \mu $ in $U$, then

\[ \limsup _ j\mu _ j(K)\leq \mu (K) \]

for every compact $K\subset U$.

Proof

By outer regularity and local finiteness, choose an open neighborhood $O$ of $K$ with compact closure in $U$ and $\mu (O\setminus K)<\varepsilon $. A cutoff $0\leq \eta \leq 1$ supported in $O$ and equal to one on $K$ gives

\[ \limsup _ j\mu _ j(K)\leq \lim _ j\int \eta \, d\mu _ j =\int \eta \, d\mu \leq \mu (O)\leq \mu (K)+\varepsilon . \]

Let $\varepsilon \downarrow 0$. Compactness both permits the cutoff and makes all these integrals finite. Compare the open-set inequality in Proposition 2.1.4. $\square$
