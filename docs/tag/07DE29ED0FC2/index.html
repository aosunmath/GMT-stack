Remark 15.3.2 (Conformal area separated the multiple-point obstruction)07DE29ED0FC2

Remark 15.3.2 (Conformal area separated the multiple-point obstruction).07DE29ED0FC2 In their 1982 paper [LY82], Li–Yau introduced conformal volume and proved a multiplicity bound for the bending energy of an immersed surface; see the introduction and Theorem 6, p. 286. A double point forces energy at least $8\pi $, already above the conjectured torus minimum $2\pi ^2$. Thus the unresolved low-energy problem concerned embeddings. Theorem 10.4.4 gives a direct radial proof of that obstruction, while Corollary 10.4.9 shows precisely how it combines with the later embedded theorem. The two numerical thresholds serve different purposes.
