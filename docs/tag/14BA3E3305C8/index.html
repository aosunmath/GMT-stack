Lemma 7.18.1 (The quadratic translation estimate)14BA3E3305C8

Lemma 7.18.1 (The quadratic translation estimate).14BA3E3305C8 For $f\in H^1(\mathbb R^ n)$ and $h\in \mathbb R^ n$,

\[ \| f(\, \cdot +h)-f\| _{L^2}\leq |h|\, \| Df\| _{L^2}. \]

If $\rho _\delta $ is a nonnegative smooth mollifier of integral one supported in $B_\delta $, then $\| \rho _\delta *f-f\| _2\leq \delta \| Df\| _2$.

Proof

For smooth $f$, the fundamental theorem of calculus gives $f(x+h)-f(x)=\int _0^1 Df(x+sh)\cdot h\, ds$. Square, apply Cauchy–Schwarz in $s$, and integrate in $x$; translation invariance of Lebesgue measure gives the first estimate. For $H^1$ functions apply the calculation to their smooth mollifications and pass to the limit in $L^2$ for the functions and their weak derivatives. Approximation by mollifiers follows from translation continuity in $L^2$; it does not require any compactness theorem. Finally average the first estimate against $\rho _\delta (h)$ and use the triangle inequality for the $L^2$ integral to obtain the second. $\square$
