Definition 6.1.1 (Varifold and weight)4CCDC6F2267C

Definition 6.1.1 (Varifold and weight).4CCDC6F2267C Let $1\leq m<n$. The Grassmannian $G(n,m)$ is the space of unoriented $m$-dimensional linear subspaces of $\mathbb {R}^ n$, with the topology induced by the orthogonal projection matrices. An $m$-varifold in $U$ is a nonnegative Radon measure on $G_ m(U)=U\times G(n,m)$. Its weight is the Radon measure on $U$ defined by

\[ \| V\| (A)=V(A\times G(n,m))\qquad (A\subset U\text{ Borel}). \]

Varifold convergence means weak convergence of these measures against $C_ c(G_ m(U))$. See [Sim18], Chapter 8, Section 1, pp. 235–236.
