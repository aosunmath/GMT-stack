Theorem 10.5.4 (Global energy controls every local area ratio)6850DAD0C60F

Theorem 10.5.4 (Global energy controls every local area ratio).6850DAD0C60F For every $y\in \mathbb R^ N$ and $s>0$,

\[ \mu \bigl (F^{-1}(B_ s(y))\bigr )\leq \mathcal W(F)s^2. \]

The constant is independent of the total area, diameter and number of components. This is a consequence of the radial first-variation mechanism of [SimW93], pp. 284–285; the following proof retains separate inside and outside energies.

Proof

Fix a regular value $s$ and let $\rho \to \infty $ in Proposition 10.5.1. Compactness eliminates the outer radius terms. Let $a=A(s)/s^2$, $w=\mathcal W(M_ s)$ and $W=\mathcal W(F)$. Dropping the exterior square term gives

\[ a\leq \frac{W-w}{4}-\frac1{2s^2}\int _{M_ s}X\cdot \vec H \leq \frac{W-w}{4}+\sqrt{aw}. \]

The last estimate again uses $r\leq s$ and Cauchy–Schwarz. Consequently

\[ \left(\sqrt a-\frac{\sqrt w}{2}\right)^2\leq \frac W4, \qquad \sqrt a\leq \frac{\sqrt w+\sqrt W}{2}\leq \sqrt W. \]

This proves $a\leq W$. For arbitrary $s$, take regular values increasing to $s$ and use convergence of the open-ball area. No limit surface or weak mean-curvature theory enters the argument. $\square$
