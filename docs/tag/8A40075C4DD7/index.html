Proposition 7.10.7 (Accumulating minimal normal graphs force degeneracy)8A40075C4DD7

Proposition 7.10.7 (Accumulating minimal normal graphs force degeneracy).8A40075C4DD7 Let $\Sigma ^ n$ be a smooth closed two-sided embedded minimal hypersurface, $n\geq 1$. Suppose nonzero smooth functions $u_ j\to 0$ in $C^\infty (\Sigma )$ define minimal normal graphs. Then $J$ has a nonzero smooth kernel element. Consequently, if $\ker J=0$, the zero graph is isolated among minimal normal graphs in the smooth topology. The functions $u_ j$ need not have a common sign. Compare the nullity mechanism in [Sharp17], Theorem 2.3 and its discussion.

Proof

Apply the averaged linearization of Lemma 7.10.3 with $u_{1,j}=0$ and $u_{2,j}=u_ j$, and set $v_ j=u_ j/\| u_ j\| _{C^0}$. Then $L_ jv_ j=0$, $L_ j\to J$ smoothly and $\| v_ j\| _{C^0}=1$. A finite covering of the closed hypersurface and interior Schauder estimates yield uniform bounds in every $C^ k$ norm. A subsequence converges smoothly to $v$ with $Jv=0$ and $\| v\| _{C^0}=1$. If the zero graph were not isolated in the metrizable smooth topology, a sequence of distinct minimal graphs converging smoothly to zero would contradict the trivial-kernel assumption. This is local isolation of normal graphs, not a global finiteness theorem for minimal hypersurfaces. $\square$
