Definition 14.18.1 (Periodic graphs and normal material time)D5F2CC03369E

Definition 14.18.1 (Periodic graphs and normal material time).D5F2CC03369E Let $\Lambda \subset \mathbb R^ n$ be a full-rank lattice, $\mathbb T^ n=\mathbb R^ n/\Lambda $ its flat torus, and let $u:\mathbb T^ n\times I\to \mathbb R$ be smooth. A periodic graphical mean curvature flow is the graph of $u$ in the flat product $\mathbb T^ n\times \mathbb R$, with $u$ satisfying Proposition 14.3.13. Equivalently, its lift is a periodic entire graph in Euclidean space. With $e$ the upward parallel unit vector, set

\[ \nu =\frac{(-Du,1)}{v},\qquad v=\sqrt{1+|Du|^2},\qquad w=\langle \nu ,e\rangle =v^{-1}>0. \]

We use $A=D\nu $ and $\vec H=-H\nu $. The symbol $\partial _ t^\perp $ denotes differentiation in a parametrization with velocity $-H\nu $, and $\mathcal P=\partial _ t^\perp -\Delta _{M_ t}$. It is not automatically the derivative at a fixed graph coordinate. This is the angle convention of [EH89], Section 3.
