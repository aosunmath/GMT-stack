Lemma 5.9.3 (Smooth scalar chain rule on a rectifiable support)15EB78009E28

Lemma 5.9.3 (Smooth scalar chain rule on a rectifiable support).15EB78009E28 Under the preceding rectifiability hypotheses, let $f$ be locally Lipschitz and $Q\in C^1(\mathbb R)$. Then

\[ \nabla ^ M(Q\circ f)=Q'(f)\nabla ^ Mf \quad \mathcal H^ m\text{-almost everywhere on }M. \]

On each compact set where $f$ and $Q'$ are bounded, the composition is Lipschitz. This is the smooth-outer-function case of the chain rule in [Sim18], Chapter 6, p. 183, equation (4).

Proof

In each $C^1$ chart the Lipschitz function $f\circ F$ is differentiable almost everywhere by Theorem 3.1.2. The ordinary chain rule there gives $D(Q\circ f\circ F)=Q'(f\circ F)D(f\circ F)$. Passing from coordinate differentials to the tangential gradient proves the identity on that chart. The countable decomposition removes a single $\mathcal H^ m$-null set. The mean value theorem bounds the Lipschitz constant of the composition on each relevant compact neighborhood. $\square$
