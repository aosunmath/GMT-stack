Proposition 5.6.2 (Area and orientation of a holomorphic parametrization)CFFF3E4138B6

Proposition 5.6.2 (Area and orientation of a holomorphic parametrization).CFFF3E4138B6 Let $F:D\subset \mathbb C\to \mathbb C^ k$ be holomorphic, and write the domain coordinate as $z=s+it$. Then

\[ F_ t=JF_ s,\qquad J_2DF=|F'(z)|^2, \qquad F^*\omega =|F'(z)|^2\, ds\wedge dt. \]

At every point with $F'\ne 0$, the oriented tangent plane is a positively oriented complex line.

Proof

The Cauchy–Riemann equations give the first identity componentwise. The two derivative vectors are orthogonal and have equal length $|F'|$, so their Gram determinant is $|F'|^4$. The area Jacobian is its square root. Lemma 5.6.1 gives $\omega (F_ s,F_ t)=|F_ s|^2$. At a critical point both sides vanish, so the formulas remain valid there. $\square$
