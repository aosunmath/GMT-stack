Proposition 7.15.9 (A curvature Caccioppoli estimate)CCCBD9EA9FEF

Proposition 7.15.9 (A curvature Caccioppoli estimate).CCCBD9EA9FEF Let $\Sigma ^ n$ be a smooth Euclidean minimal hypersurface, let $p\geq 2$, and let $\phi \in C_ c^\infty (\Sigma )$ have support in its interior. With $u=|A|$,

\[ \int \phi ^2u^{p-2}|\nabla A|^2 \leq 2\int \phi ^2u^{p+2}+4\int u^ p|\nabla \phi |^2, \]
\[ \int \phi ^2|\nabla (u^{p/2})|^2 \leq \frac{p^2}{2}\int \phi ^2u^{p+2}+p^2\int u^ p|\nabla \phi |^2. \]

For $p=2$ the factor $u^{p-2}$ is interpreted as one, including at zeros. These estimates alone do not give an epsilon-regularity theorem.

Proof

Test $\Delta h=-u^2h$ against $\phi ^2u_\varepsilon ^{p-2}h$ and integrate by parts. Differentiating the weight yields a nonnegative term $(p-2)\phi ^2u_\varepsilon ^{p-4}\sum _ k\langle h,\nabla _ kh\rangle ^2$ on the gradient side. Dropping it gives

\[ \int \phi ^2u_\varepsilon ^{p-2}|\nabla h|^2 \leq \int \phi ^2u_\varepsilon ^{p-2}u^4 +2\int |\phi |u_\varepsilon ^{p-2}u|\nabla h||\nabla \phi |. \]

Young's inequality bounds the last integrand by half the left gradient integrand plus $2u_\varepsilon ^{p-2}u^2|\nabla \phi |^2$. Absorb the half and let $\varepsilon \downarrow 0$. All functions are bounded on the fixed compact support and $p\geq 2$, so dominated convergence proves the first estimate. The weak chain rule and Lemma 7.15.5 give $|\nabla (u^{p/2})|^2\leq (p^2/4)u^{p-2}|\nabla A|^2$ almost everywhere, including the zero set. Multiplication by $p^2/4$ proves the second estimate. $\square$
