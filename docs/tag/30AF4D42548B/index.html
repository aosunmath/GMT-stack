Theorem 14.6.8 (Compactness of integral Brakke flows)30AF4D42548B

Theorem 14.6.8 (Compactness of integral Brakke flows).30AF4D42548B Let $\mathcal M_ j$ be integral Brakke flows on $U\times [a,b]$. Suppose for every compact $K\subset U$,

\[ \sup _ j\sup _{t\in [a,b]}\mu _ t^ j(K)<\infty . \]

Then a subsequence converges to an integral Brakke flow in the sense of Definition 14.6.7. Source: [WhiMCF15], Theorem 9.8, pp. 23–24; compare [CCMS20], Section 2.6. The proof combines the localized mass monotonicity, curvature energy bounds, varifold compactness, and lower semicontinuity of the Brakke functional. That proof remains external. This is weak compactness and does not assert smooth convergence.
