Lemma 7.13.4 (One step of integrability improvement)C8B26982BF2A

Lemma 7.13.4 (One step of integrability improvement).C8B26982BF2A Let $u\geq 0$ be a weak subsolution on $B_2$, and suppose $u\in L^ p(B_ s)$ for $p\geq 2$ and $1\leq r<s\leq 2$. With $\chi $ as in Theorem 7.13.1,

\[ \| u\| _{L^{p\chi }(B_ r)} \leq \left(\frac{C p^2}{(s-r)^2}\right)^{1/p}\| u\| _{L^ p(B_ s)}, \]

where $C$ depends only on $n$ and $\Lambda /\lambda $.

Proof

Choose $\eta =1$ on $B_ r$, supported in $B_ s$, with $|D\eta |\leq C/(s-r)$. Apply the Sobolev gain to $\eta v_ L$ and then Lemma 7.13.3. The right side is bounded by $Cp^2(s-r)^{-2}\int _{B_ s}u^ p$, because $v_ L^2=u^2u_ L^{p-2}\leq u^ p$. As $L\to \infty $, Fatou's lemma gives

\[ \left(\int _{B_ r}u^{p\chi }\right)^{1/\chi } \leq \frac{Cp^2}{(s-r)^2}\int _{B_ s}u^ p. \]

Taking the $p$th root proves the claim. In particular the higher integrability is a conclusion of the argument, not an assumption permitting an untruncated power test. $\square$
