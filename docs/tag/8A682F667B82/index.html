Proposition 11.5.4 (Tangents to fixed-volume CMC families)8A682F667B82

Proposition 11.5.4 (Tangents to fixed-volume CMC families).8A682F667B82 Let $\Sigma _ t$ be a smooth family of closed CMC boundaries with constant enclosed volume, and write $H_{\Sigma _ t}=c(t)$. Its initial normal speed $f$ satisfies

\[ \int _{\Sigma _0}f\, dA=0,\qquad Jf=-c'(0). \]

It is therefore a constrained Jacobi speed. This identifies the geometric meaning of the constant in [BdCE88], equation (2.10).

Proof

Differentiate volume and use the transport formula to obtain zero mean. Proposition 11.3.4 gives the derivative of mean curvature for a normal variation as $-Jf$. A tangential component of velocity contributes the tangential derivative of $H_{\Sigma _0}$, which vanishes because it is constant. Thus $c'(0)=-Jf$ at every point, and Proposition 11.5.2 applies. This is a necessary condition for an actual CMC family; it does not assert that every constrained Jacobi field integrates to such a family. $\square$
