Corollary 14.20.10 (The total curvature dissipation equals the initial area excess)D29C250B9C03

Corollary 14.20.10 (The total curvature dissipation equals the initial area excess).D29C250B9C03 For an all-time smooth periodic graphical flow,

\[ \int _0^\infty \int _{M_ t}H^2\, d\mu _ t\, dt =\mathcal A(u(0))-\operatorname {Vol}(\mathbb T^ n). \]

More generally, the remaining dissipation after a time $s$ is exactly $\mathcal F(u(s))$ and is bounded by $\mathcal F(u(0))e^{-\gamma s}$.

Proof

Integrate Proposition 14.20.5 from $s$ to a finite $T$. The right-hand side is $\mathcal A(u(s))-\mathcal A(u(T))$. Theorem 14.20.7 implies that the latter area tends to the base torus volume as $T\to \infty $. Monotone convergence for the nonnegative time integrand gives the exact identity. The exponential bound is the same theorem evaluated at $s$. This is a finite total dissipation statement, not a pointwise estimate for $H$ at every late time. $\square$
