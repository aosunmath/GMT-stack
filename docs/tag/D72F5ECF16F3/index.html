Theorem 9.4.5 (Uniform Lipschitz control of normalized widths)D72F5ECF16F3

Theorem 9.4.5 (Uniform Lipschitz control of normalized widths).D72F5ECF16F3 Let $M^{n+1}$ be smooth, closed and connected, with $n\geq 1$. Fix a smooth metric $g_0$ on $M$ and $0<A\leq B$. There is $K=K(M,g_0,A,B,n)$ such that all smooth metrics $g,h$ with $Ag_0\leq g,h\leq Bg_0$ satisfy

\[ \left|p^{-1/(n+1)}\omega _ p(M,g)-p^{-1/(n+1)}\omega _ p(M,h)\right| \leq K\| g-h\| _{C^0(g_0)} \]

for every $p\geq 1$. Here the norm is $\sup _{x,v:\, g_0(v,v)=1}|(g-h)(v,v)|$. This is the smooth-metric form of [MNS19], Lemma 1.

Proof

Write $\varepsilon =\| g-h\| _{C^0(g_0)}$. Since $g\geq Ag_0$, we have $h\leq (1+\varepsilon /A)g$. Proposition 9.4.3 gives

\[ \omega _ p(h)-\omega _ p(g) \leq \left((1+\varepsilon /A)^{n/2}-1\right)\omega _ p(g) \leq \left((1+\varepsilon /A)^{n/2}-1\right)B^{n/2}\omega _ p(g_0). \]

The norm is at most $2B$ on this band. The derivative of $t\mapsto (1+t)^{n/2}$ is bounded on $[0,2B/A]$, so the parenthesis is at most a constant times $\varepsilon $. Reverse $g,h$ for the other inequality. Finally Theorem 9.1.5 bounds $p^{-1/(n+1)}\omega _ p(g_0)$ for large $p$. The finitely many smaller widths are finite and bounded by a sufficiently large one using Lemma 9.1.3. This gives a bound uniform in all $p$. This last step uses the external Weyl theorem; the source instead invokes the external Gromov–Guth growth bound, [LMN], Theorem 2.8. $\square$
