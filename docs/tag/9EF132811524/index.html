Remark 15.1.1 (1958–1960: compactness as a design principle).9EF132811524 Fleming recalls that he joined Brown in 1958 and worked with Federer during the academic year 1958–1959 on the paper Normal and integral currents, published in 1960. The objective was more specific than merely enlarging the class of smooth surfaces: generalized domains of integration had to retain enough geometric structure while admitting compactness for variational problems. This helps explain the simultaneous appearance of mass and boundary-mass bounds in the compactness theory. See [Fle15], Section 3, pp. 13–16, and [Fle07], Part I, Section 1. The corresponding definitions are in the currents chapter.
