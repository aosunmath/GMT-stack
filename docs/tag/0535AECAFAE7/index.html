Theorem 3.7.5 (Pointwise plane-cone bounds give a countable graph cover)0535AECAFAE7

Theorem 3.7.5 (Pointwise plane-cone bounds give a countable graph cover).0535AECAFAE7 Let $\mu $ be Radon on an open $U\subset \mathbb R^ n$, and let $S\subset U$. Suppose that for each $x\in S$ there is an $m$-plane $P_ x$ for which

\[ \liminf _{r\downarrow 0}\frac{\mu (B_ r(x))}{r^ m}>0,\qquad \lim _{r\downarrow 0}\frac{\mu (C_{1/4}(x,P_ x)\cap B_ r(x))}{r^ m}=0. \]

Then $S$ is contained in countably many whole Lipschitz graphs over $m$-planes. No measurability of the plane selection is needed for this containment assertion.

Proof

For positive integers $k,j$, retain those points for which $B_{1/j}(x)\subset U$ and the lower bound with $c=1/k$ and cone upper bound with $\delta =c/(2\cdot 64^ m)$ both hold for all $0<r<1/j$. The two limits imply that these countably many classes cover $S$: first choose a smaller positive lower constant, then a sufficiently small common scale at the given point. Split each class using the finite $1/8$-net of planes from Lemma 3.7.2, allowing overlap. Intersect with a countable cover of space by balls of diameter less than $1/(2j)$. Each resulting piece satisfies Proposition 3.7.4. There are countably many pieces and hence countably many graphs. This construction uses uniform inequalities only on the defined pieces, not an unsupported uniform convergence assertion on $S$ itself. $\square$
