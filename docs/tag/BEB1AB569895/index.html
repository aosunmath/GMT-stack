Theorem 14.12.7 (F-instability lowers entropy when no line splits)BEB1AB569895

Theorem 14.12.7 (F-instability lowers entropy when no line splits).BEB1AB569895 Let $\Sigma ^ n\subset \mathbb R^{n+1}$, $n\geq 1$, be connected, complete, smooth, properly embedded, two-sided, without boundary, and a self-shrinker with polynomial area growth. Suppose it has no nonzero constant tangent vector and is $F$-unstable in Definition 14.9.3. Then there exist $f\in C_ c^\infty (\Sigma )$ and $\eta >0$ such that the normal graphs $\Sigma _ s=\{ x+sf(x)\nu (x):x\in \Sigma \} $ satisfy

\[ \lambda (\Sigma _ s)<\lambda (\Sigma )\qquad (0<|s|<\eta ). \]

These perturbations can be arbitrarily small in every fixed $C^ m$ norm. This is a proper embedded formulation of [CM12], Theorem 0.15, p. 763, proved on pp. 789–792.

Proof

Choose $f$ from $F$-instability. Lemma 14.12.6 supplies a joint quadratic deficit near $(0,1,0)$. Lemma 14.12.5 supplies a compact parameter box $K=\{ |c|\leq R,\ \tau \leq t\leq T\} $ outside which all sufficiently small perturbations have a fixed deficit $d>0$.

Choose a parameter neighborhood $U$ of $(0,1)$ with closure in $K$, sufficiently small that the local quadratic estimate holds on $U$ for all sufficiently small $s$. On the compact set $K\setminus U$, the unperturbed surface has a uniform positive deficit by Theorem 14.11.8. Uniform convergence of $G(c,t,s)$ to $G(c,t,0)$ there retains a fixed positive deficit $d_1$ after reducing $|s|$. On $U$ the quadratic estimate gives deficit at least $\gamma s^2$. Consequently, for each small nonzero $s$,

\[ \sup _{c,\ t>0}G(c,t,s) \leq \lambda (\Sigma )-\min \{ d,d_1,\gamma s^2\} <\lambda (\Sigma ). \]

The graph family remains embedded for small amplitude by Definition 14.12.1. Since $f$ is fixed, smooth and compactly supported, the graph function $sf$ tends to zero in each fixed $C^ m$ norm. The exclusion of escaping parameters is what permits taking the global supremum. $\square$
