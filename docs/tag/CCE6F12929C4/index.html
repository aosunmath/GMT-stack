Proposition 4.4.9 (The BV lattice inequality)CCE6F12929C4

Proposition 4.4.9 (The BV lattice inequality).CCE6F12929C4 If $u,v\in BV_{\mathrm{loc}}(U)$, then their maximum and minimum are locally BV and

\[ |D\max \{ u,v\} |+|D\min \{ u,v\} |\leq |Du|+|Dv| \]

as measures.

Proof

For smooth $u,v$, approximate the maximum and minimum by

\[ M_\delta =\frac{u+v+\sqrt{(u-v)^2+\delta ^2}}2, \qquad m_\delta =\frac{u+v-\sqrt{(u-v)^2+\delta ^2}}2. \]

Their gradients are complementary convex combinations of $\nabla u,\nabla v$, so $|\nabla M_\delta |+|\nabla m_\delta |\leq |\nabla u|+|\nabla v|$. Let $\delta \downarrow 0$ using local $L^1$ convergence and lower semicontinuity. For BV inputs, mollify cutoffs agreeing with $u,v$ near a ball whose boundary has zero $(|Du|+|Dv|)$ measure. The maxima and minima converge in $L^1$, because these operations are Lipschitz in their two scalar arguments. The same localized mollification estimate as above proves the bound on that ball. Approximate radii, integrate the ball inequalities against nonnegative smooth functions of their centers, and shrink the radius. This gives the measure inequality on smooth test functions and hence on Borel sets by regularity. This is an approximation consequence of the BV framework in [Giu84], Chapter 1. $\square$
