Proposition 5.6.5 (Exact density of the calibrated cusp)213862B49C97

Proposition 5.6.5 (Exact density of the calibrated cusp).213862B49C97 Let $V$ be the rectifiable varifold associated to the cusp current in Example 5.6.4. For $0<r<\sqrt2$, let $R\in (0,1)$ solve $R^4+R^6=r^2$. Then

\[ \| V\| (B_ r(0))=2\pi R^4+3\pi R^6, \qquad \frac{\| V\| (B_ r(0))}{\pi r^2} =\frac{2+3R^2}{1+R^2}. \]

In particular $\Theta ^2(\| V\| ,0)=2$.

Proof

The norm squared of $F(z)$ is $|z|^4+|z|^6$, strictly increasing in $|z|$. Thus the preimage of the ambient ball is the parameter disk of radius $R$. Injectivity and the area formula give

\[ \| V\| (B_ r(0))=2\pi \int _0^ R(4t^2+9t^4)t\, dt =2\pi R^4+3\pi R^6. \]

Divide by $\pi (R^4+R^6)$ and let $R\downarrow 0$. The critical point contributes no area atom. $\square$
