Theorem 14.3.3 (Euclidean Simons identity)18C726B29E0F

Theorem 14.3.3 (Euclidean Simons identity).18C726B29E0F For a smooth Euclidean hypersurface with the convention in Definition 14.3.1,

\[ \Delta h_{ij}=\nabla _ i\nabla _ jH+Hh_ i{}^ kh_{kj}-|A|^2h_{ij}. \]

This is the Euclidean specialization of [HP99], Corollary 2.2, p. 49. The formula is valid without a minimality or convexity hypothesis.

Proof

Apply Theorem 7.15.3 with $c=0$. Its proof gives the Gauss–Codazzi commutation explicitly, so no external curvature-commutation input is needed for the evolution identities below. $\square$
