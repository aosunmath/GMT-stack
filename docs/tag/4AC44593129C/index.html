Proposition 4.14.2 (A lower density bound controls Hausdorff measure)4AC44593129C

Proposition 4.14.2 (A lower density bound controls Hausdorff measure).4AC44593129C Let $\mu $ be a positive Radon measure on $U\subset \mathbb R^ n$, let $m>0$, and let $S\subset U$ be Borel. Suppose that for each $x\in S$ and every sufficiently small $r>0$,

\[ \mu (B_ r(x))\geq c r^ m \]

with a fixed $c>0$; the permitted scale may depend on $x$. Then for every Borel $A\subset S$,

\[ \mathcal H^ m(A)\leq \frac{5^ m\omega _ m}{c}\mu (A). \]

Here $m$ is an integer between one and $n$ so that $\omega _ m$ has the normalization of Definition 2.2.1. The bound is not sharp.

Proof

First suppose $A$ is bounded and lies in a compact subset of $U$. Take an open $O\subset U$ containing $A$ and a positive diameter scale $\delta $. For each $x\in A$ use all sufficiently small balls centered at $x$ that lie in $O$, have radius less than $\delta /10$, and satisfy the stated mass lower bound. This family covers $A$ and has bounded centers and radii. Lemma 2.5.1 provides disjoint selected balls $B_{r_ i}(x_ i)$ whose fivefold dilations cover $A$. Their diameters are less than $\delta $, so the normalized Hausdorff content satisfies

\[ \mathcal H^ m_\delta (A) \leq \sum _ i\omega _ m(5r_ i)^ m \leq \frac{5^ m\omega _ m}{c}\sum _ i\mu (B_{r_ i}(x_ i)) \leq \frac{5^ m\omega _ m}{c}\mu (O). \]

Let $\delta \downarrow 0$ and then use outer regularity of $\mu $ to take the infimum over $O$. It is enough to use open sets inside a fixed relatively compact neighborhood of $A$, so their measures are finite in this step. Finally exhaust $U$ by compact sets and apply the result to their intersections with $A$. These sets increase to $A$, and both Borel measures are continuous from below, proving the general assertion. The constants come from fivefold dilation and the normalized diameter definition, not a sharp density comparison theorem. $\square$
