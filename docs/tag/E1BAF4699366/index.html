Theorem 11.9.4 (CMC reconstruction on the immersion locus)E1BAF4699366

Theorem 11.9.4 (CMC reconstruction on the immersion locus).E1BAF4699366 Under the preceding hypotheses, $X_\varepsilon $ is a conformal immersion on the open set $\{ \lambda _\varepsilon ^2>0\} $. Its induced metric is $\lambda _\varepsilon ^2(dx^2+dy^2)$, and its trace scalar mean curvature relative to the chosen normal $u$ is $H=\varepsilon $. Its mean curvature vector is $-\varepsilon u$. At a point where $\lambda _\varepsilon =0$, its entire differential vanishes; no immersion assertion is made there. This is the reconstruction of [Hel01], pp. 19–21, with signs and normalization made explicit.

Proof

The positive conformal factor gives a rank-two differential by Lemma 11.9.3. Since both derivatives are perpendicular to $u$, this is a unit normal. For the induced metric, $\Delta _ g X_\varepsilon =\lambda _\varepsilon ^{-2}\Delta X_\varepsilon =-\varepsilon u$. The immersion identity $\Delta _ gX=\vec H=-Hu$ yields $H=\varepsilon $. The parameter orientation instead gives unit normal $-\varepsilon u$, relative to which the scalar trace is $-1$. At a zero of the conformal factor, both coordinate derivatives have norm zero. Thus the formula can produce a degenerate map, even a constant map, as well as an immersion. $\square$
