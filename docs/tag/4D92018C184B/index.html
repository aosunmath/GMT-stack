Theorem 7.5.6 (Interior Harnack inequality)4D92018C184B

Theorem 7.5.6 (Interior Harnack inequality).4D92018C184B If $v\in H^1(B_{2R}(a))$ is nonnegative and solves the equation in Definition 7.5.2, then

\[ \operatorname *{ess\, sup}_{B_ R(a)}v\leq C\operatorname *{ess\, inf}_{B_ R(a)}v, \qquad C=C(n,\Lambda /\lambda ). \]

The sign assumption is essential, and the estimate is on a smaller ball. This is the zero-lower-order-term specialization of [SimonPDE], Chapter 18, Theorem 1, p. 211. The proof remains external.
