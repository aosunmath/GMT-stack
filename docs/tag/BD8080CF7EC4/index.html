Example 7.9.7 (Product-sphere links and the dimension-seven threshold)BD8080CF7EC4

Example 7.9.7 (Product-sphere links and the dimension-seven threshold).BD8080CF7EC4 Let $p,q\geq 1$, set $n=p+q+1$, and put

\[ L=\mathbb S^ p\left(\sqrt{\frac p{p+q}}\right) \times \mathbb S^ q\left(\sqrt{\frac q{p+q}}\right)\subset \mathbb S^ n. \]

Writing its radii as $a,b$, a unit normal is $\nu (x,y)=(bx/a,-ay/b)$. Its principal curvatures are $b/a$ with multiplicity $p$ and $-a/b$ with multiplicity $q$. Hence $H_ L=0$ and $|A_ L|^2=p+q=n-1$. The Rayleigh quotient is minimized by constants, so $\lambda _0=-(n-1)$. Proposition 7.9.6 shows that its cone is stable away from zero exactly when $n-1\leq (n-2)^2/4$, or $n\geq 7$ in this family. This is a stability calculation, not a classification of which product cones minimize area. Compare the cone examples in [ChMin], Sections 13, 17, and 26.
