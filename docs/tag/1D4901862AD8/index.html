Remark 10.1.3 (What the catenoid estimate supplies)1D4901862AD8

Remark 10.1.3 (What the catenoid estimate supplies).1D4901862AD8 For a closed two-sided unstable embedded minimal surface in a three-manifold, the first negative direction for area produces nearby sheets with a quadratic area decrease. Ketover–Marques–Neves [KMN], Section 2 and Theorem 2.4, control the cost of inserting and opening connecting necks, obtaining suitable families whose areas remain strictly below twice the central area. Their logarithmic neck estimate resolves the critical two-dimensional comparison. A complete formulation of the deformation and its topology is still an external dependency here. Applications must retain the particular sweepout class; the estimate is not a general theorem that all min-max limits have multiplicity one.
