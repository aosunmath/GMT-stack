Definition 7.1.4 (Stability and Morse index)6E18930B316D

Definition 7.1.4 (Stability and Morse index).6E18930B316D For the hypersurface in Theorem 7.1.3, stability means $Q(f,f)\geq 0$ for every smooth $f$. The Morse index is the largest dimension of a linear subspace on which $Q$ is negative definite. A Jacobi field is a normal field in the kernel of the Jacobi operator. For a one-sided hypersurface, use sections of its normal line bundle, equivalently the appropriate equivariant functions on the two-sided cover. These are unrestricted area variations; CMC stability will use a volume constraint. See [MM], Section 9, and [MNIndex], Section 1.
