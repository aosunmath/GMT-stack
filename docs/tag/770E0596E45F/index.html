Proposition 4.2.2 (Multiplication by a smooth cutoff)770E0596E45F

Proposition 4.2.2 (Multiplication by a smooth cutoff).770E0596E45F If $u\in BV_{\mathrm{loc}}(U)$ and $\eta \in C_ c^1(U)$, then the function $\eta u$, extended by zero outside $U$, belongs to $BV(\mathbb R^ n)$ and

\[ D(\eta u)=\eta Du+uD\eta \, \mathcal L^ n, \qquad |D(\eta u)|(\mathbb R^ n) \leq \int |\eta |\, d|Du|+\int |u||D\eta |\, dx. \]

The compact support of the cutoff prevents a boundary measure from appearing at $\partial U$.

Proof

For a compactly supported test field $X$, use $\eta \operatorname {div}X=\operatorname {div}(\eta X)-D\eta \cdot X$. The defining identity for $Du$ then gives the asserted vector measure. Its total variation is bounded by the sum of the total variations of the two terms. Both are finite on the compact support, and $\eta u$ is integrable. Compare the product estimate in [Sim18], Chapter 2, the proof of Lemma 2.8. $\square$
