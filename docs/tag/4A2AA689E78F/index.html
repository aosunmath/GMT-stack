Theorem 2.5.4 (A strong maximal bound above the endpoint)4A2AA689E78F

Theorem 2.5.4 (A strong maximal bound above the endpoint).4A2AA689E78F For $1<p<\infty $ and $f\in L^ p(\mathbb R^ n)$,

\[ \| Mf\| _ p^ p\leq \frac{2^ p5^ n p}{p-1}\| f\| _ p^ p. \]

This consequence of the covering estimate associated with [Sim18], Lemma 3.4, is proved here by integrating distribution functions.

Proof

Fix $\lambda >0$. Split $|f|$ into the part bounded by $\lambda /2$ and the tail $g_\lambda =|f|\mathbf1_{\{ |f|>\lambda /2\} }$. The first part has maximal function at most $\lambda /2$. The tail is integrable since $|f|\leq (\lambda /2)^{1-p}|f|^ p$ on its support. Subadditivity and Proposition 2.5.3 give

\[ \mathcal L^ n(\{ Mf>\lambda \} ) \leq \frac{2\cdot 5^ n}{\lambda }\int _{\{ |f|>\lambda /2\} }|f|\, dx. \]

The identity $a^ p=\int _0^ a p\lambda ^{p-1}\, d\lambda $, followed by Tonelli's theorem, gives

\[ \int (Mf)^ p\leq 2\cdot 5^ n p\int |f(x)| \left(\int _0^{2|f(x)|}\lambda ^{p-2}\, d\lambda \right)dx. \]

Evaluation of the inner integral proves the claim. Tonelli applies before finiteness is known because the integrands are nonnegative. Local integrability of $f$, needed to define $Mf$, follows from Holder's inequality on bounded sets. $\square$
