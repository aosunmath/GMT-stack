Proposition 12.7.6 (Area-order energy bound for bounded minimizers)92B0918DB383

Proposition 12.7.6 (Area-order energy bound for bounded minimizers).92B0918DB383 If a smooth entire solution $u:\mathbb R^ n\to [-1,1]$ minimizes energy on bounded balls, then

\[ E_1(u;B_ R(a))\leq C(n)R^{n-1}\quad (R>0) \]

for every center $a$.

Proof

Lemma 12.5.1 gives a universal gradient bound. For $R\geq 2$, choose a radial cutoff $\eta $ equal to zero on $B_{R-1}(a)$ and one near $\partial B_ R(a)$, with $|D\eta |\leq C$. The competitor $v=1+\eta (u-1)$ has the same trace, takes values in $[-1,1]$, and has bounded gradient. It equals a zero-energy well in the inner ball, so its energy is at most a fixed constant times the volume of the width-one annulus, at most $C(n)R^{n-1}$. Minimality gives the same bound for $u$. For $R<2$, the bounded energy density gives $E_1(u;B_ R(a))\leq CR^ n\leq 2CR^{n-1}$. This proves [ChAC], Exercise 5.4. In dimension three the bound meets the growth hypothesis of Theorem 12.6.10, since minimization implies stability by second variation. $\square$
