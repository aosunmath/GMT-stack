Proposition 12.4.1 (A flat layer and its limiting energy measure)FB823D886F0F

Proposition 12.4.1 (A flat layer and its limiting energy measure).FB823D886F0F Let $e\in \mathbb S^{n-1}$, $b\in \mathbb R$, and $u_\varepsilon (x)=q((x\cdot e-b)/\varepsilon )$ on $\mathbb R^ n$, where $q(t)=\tanh (t/\sqrt2)$. This is a smooth Allen–Cahn solution, and locally

\[ u_\varepsilon \to 2\chi _{\{ x\cdot e>b\} }-1,\qquad \mu _\varepsilon ^{u_\varepsilon }\rightharpoonup c_ W\mathcal H^{n-1}\llcorner \{ x\cdot e=b\} . \]

The phase convergence is in every finite $L^ p$ on bounded sets.

Proof

The chain rule and $q''=W'(q)$ give the equation. The phase convergence follows pointwise off the hyperplane and then by bounded convergence. Write $x=y+(b+\varepsilon t)e$ with $y\in e^\perp $. Equipartition gives energy density $\varepsilon ^{-1}(q'(t))^2$. For a continuous compactly supported test function $\zeta $, Fubini gives

\[ \int \zeta \, d\mu _\varepsilon =\int _{e^\perp }\int _{\mathbb R} \zeta (y+(b+\varepsilon t)e)(q'(t))^2\, dt\, d\mathcal H^{n-1}(y). \]

Its integrand is dominated by an integrable function using the bounded transverse projection of the support and $\int (q')^2=c_ W$. Dominated convergence proves the measure limit. The fixed translation belongs inside the scaled argument: $q((x\cdot e-b)/\varepsilon )$ keeps the interface at $x\cdot e=b$. This clarifies the rescaling in [ChAC], Section 2.3.1. $\square$
