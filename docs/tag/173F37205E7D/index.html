Lemma 3.8.4 (Recovering a tangent from a full-density restriction)173F37205E7D

Lemma 3.8.4 (Recovering a tangent from a full-density restriction).173F37205E7D Let $\mu $ be Radon, $A$ Borel, $x\in A$, and suppose

\[ \frac{(\mu \llcorner A)(B_ r(x))}{\mu (B_ r(x))}\to 1, \qquad r^{-m}(\eta _{x,r})_\# (\mu \llcorner A) \rightharpoonup q\mathcal H^ m\llcorner P \]

for $q\in (0,\infty )$ and an $m$-plane $P$. Then $r^{-m}(\eta _{x,r})_\# \mu $ has the same limit, and

\[ \mu (B_ r(x)\setminus A)=o(r^ m). \]
Proof

The plane-tangent ball calculation gives $(\mu \llcorner A)(B_ r)/(\omega _ mr^ m)\to q$. Dividing by the first assumed ratio gives $\mu (B_ r)/(\omega _ mr^ m)\to q$. Subtracting the two expressions gives the stated small mass outside $A$. The difference of the two scaled integrals against a test supported in $B_ R$ is bounded by its supremum norm times $r^{-m}\mu (B_{Rr}(x)\setminus A)$, which tends to zero. Thus the unrestricted tangent is the same. This conclusion needs the positive finite density of the restricted tangent as well as relative density one. $\square$
