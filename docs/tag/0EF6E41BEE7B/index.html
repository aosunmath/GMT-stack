Lemma 7.15.6 (Improved Kato inequality for constant mean curvature)0EF6E41BEE7B

Lemma 7.15.6 (Improved Kato inequality for constant mean curvature).0EF6E41BEE7B If $H$ is constant on a hypersurface in a space form, then, wherever $|A|>0$,

\[ |\nabla A|^2\geq \left(1+\frac2n\right)|\nabla |A||^2. \]

The same assertion holds with $A$ replaced by its trace-free part $A-(H/n)I$ where that tensor is nonzero. Compare the Euclidean minimal case of [SSY75], equations (1.29)–(1.33), pp. 279–280; the constant-mean-curvature extension follows from the same algebra below.

Proof

At a point diagonalize the tensor under consideration, with eigenvalues $\lambda _ i$. Its derivative $T_{ijk}=\nabla _ kh_{ij}$ is fully symmetric by symmetry of $h$ and Codazzi, and $\sum _ iT_{iik}=\partial _ kH=0$. For fixed $k$, write $a_ i=T_{iik}$. Then $a_ k=-\sum _{i\ne k}a_ i$, so Cauchy–Schwarz implies

\[ a_ k^2\leq \frac{n-1}{n}\sum _ i a_ i^2. \]

Each component with exactly two equal indices occurs three times in $|T|^2$, and each component with three equal indices occurs once. Discarding the nonnegative contributions from three distinct indices therefore gives

\[ |T|^2\geq \sum _ k\left(3\sum _ iT_{iik}^2-2T_{kkk}^2\right) \geq \left(1+\frac2n\right)\sum _{i,k}T_{iik}^2. \]

On the other hand $\partial _ k|h|=\sum _ i\lambda _ iT_{iik}/|h|$, so $|\nabla |h||^2\leq \sum _{i,k}T_{iik}^2$. This proves the first claim. When $H$ is constant, subtracting $(H/n)g$ leaves the derivative unchanged and gives another Codazzi tensor with constant trace; the same argument proves the second claim. $\square$
