Proposition 10.5.3 (A local comparison with an explicit constant)2124DA17B73F

Proposition 10.5.3 (A local comparison with an explicit constant).2124DA17B73F For $0<\sigma <\rho $,

\[ \frac{A(\sigma )}{\sigma ^2}\leq 3\frac{A(\rho )}{\rho ^2}+\frac52\mathcal W(M_\rho ). \]

If $y$ has $m$ preimages, then

\[ \pi m\leq 3\frac{A(\rho )}{\rho ^2}+\frac52\mathcal W(M_\rho ). \]

These estimates do not assume embeddedness or a pointwise bound on mean curvature.

Proof

First use regular radii in Proposition 10.5.1 and discard its square term. Put $a_ s=A(s)/s^2$ and $E=\int _{M_\rho }|\vec H|^2$. The two radius terms are bounded in absolute value by $\tfrac 12\sqrt{a_\rho E}$ and $\tfrac 12\sqrt{a_\sigma E}$, using $r\leq s$ on $M_ s$ and Cauchy–Schwarz. The annular energy term is at most $E/16$. Since

\[ \tfrac 12\sqrt{aE}\leq \tfrac 12a+\tfrac 18E, \]

we obtain $a_\sigma \leq a_\rho +E/16+(a_\rho +a_\sigma )/2+E/4$. Rearrangement gives $a_\sigma \leq 3a_\rho +5E/8$, which is the claim. Approximation from below by regular radii gives the formula at arbitrary radii; open sublevel sets and their integrals converge from below. Letting $\sigma \downarrow 0$ gives the multiplicity consequence by the local sheet area expansion. $\square$
