Theorem 7.13.5 (Moser local boundedness for nonnegative subsolutions)DB183F3A4CE7

Theorem 7.13.5 (Moser local boundedness for nonnegative subsolutions).DB183F3A4CE7 Let $n\geq 2$, let $A$ be measurable and symmetric on $B_{2R}(a)$ with $\lambda I\leq A\leq \Lambda I$ for $0<\lambda \leq \Lambda $, and let $u\in H^1(B_{2R}(a))$ be a nonnegative weak subsolution of $\operatorname {div}(A Du)=0$. Then

\[ \operatorname *{ess\, sup}_{B_ R(a)}u \leq C(n,\Lambda /\lambda )\left(R^{-n}\int _{B_{2R}(a)}u^2\right)^{1/2}. \]

This is the zero-lower-order, $p=2$ case of [SimonPDE], Lecture 17, Theorem 1, p. 200.

Proof

Translation and dilation reduce to $a=0,R=1$ without changing the ellipticity ratio. Let $r_ j=1+2^{-j}$ and $p_ j=2\chi ^ j$. Starting from $u\in L^2(B_2)$, Lemma 7.13.4 successively gives

\[ \| u\| _{L^{p_{j+1}}(B_{r_{j+1}})} \leq \bigl (Cp_ j^2 2^{2j+2}\bigr )^{1/p_ j} \| u\| _{L^{p_ j}(B_{r_ j})}. \]

The logarithm of the product of these constants is bounded by a multiple of $\sum _ j(1+j)\chi ^{-j}$, which is finite. Thus $\| u\| _{L^{p_ j}(B_1)}\leq C\| u\| _{L^2(B_2)}$ for every $j$. If $u>A$ on a subset of $B_1$ of positive measure $m$, then $\| u\| _{L^{p_ j}(B_1)}\geq A m^{1/p_ j}$. Letting $j\to \infty $ bounds every such level $A$ by $C\| u\| _2$, and hence bounds the essential supremum. This does not assume $u\in L^\infty $ in advance. Scaling the volume integral back gives the factor $R^{-n/2}$, including in dimension two. $\square$
