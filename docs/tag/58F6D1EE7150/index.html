Proposition 7.8.2 (A nonnegative stability potential vanishes under small-energy cutoffs)58F6D1EE7150

Proposition 7.8.2 (A nonnegative stability potential vanishes under small-energy cutoffs).58F6D1EE7150 Suppose a smooth manifold has the cutoff property in Definition 7.8.1, and let $q\geq 0$ be a continuous function satisfying

\[ \int q\phi ^2\leq \int |\nabla \phi |^2 \quad \text{for every }\phi \in C_ c^\infty . \]

Then $q=0$ everywhere. In particular, a two-sided stable minimal hypersurface in Euclidean space with this cutoff property is totally geodesic.

Proof

Compactly supported locally Lipschitz tests are admissible by approximation in $W^{1,2}$ on a fixed compact neighborhood of their support; $q$ is bounded there, so the potential integral also converges. Use $\eta _ j$ as a test and apply Fatou's lemma to the nonnegative functions $q\eta _ j^2$. This gives $\int q\leq \liminf _ j\int |\nabla \eta _ j|^2=0$. Continuity yields pointwise vanishing. For a Euclidean minimal hypersurface the stability potential is $q=|A|^2$. $\square$
