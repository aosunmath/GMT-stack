Theorem 14.20.4 (Conditional convergence to one horizontal slice)033EF6B85768

Theorem 14.20.4 (Conditional convergence to one horizontal slice).033EF6B85768 Let a periodic graphical mean curvature flow be smooth for all $t\geq 0$, and put $V_0=\max \sqrt{1+|Du(\cdot ,0)|^2}$. There is a constant $c_\infty $ between its initial minimum and maximum such that

\[ \| u(\cdot ,t)-c_\infty \| _\infty + \| Du(\cdot ,t)\| _\infty +\| D^2u(\cdot ,t)\| _\infty \leq C t^{-1/2}\quad (t>0). \]

The constant depends only on $V_0$ and the flat torus. This is a consequence of the slope-based curvature estimate of [EH89], Proposition 4.4, with periodicity used to control the height oscillation.

Proof

Proposition 14.18.4 bounds $v$ by $V_0$. Theorem 14.19.4 and Lemma 14.20.1 give

\[ \| D^2u(\cdot ,t)\| _\infty \leq V_0^3\sqrt{\frac{V_0^2-1}{2t}}. \]

Lemma 14.20.2 gives the same rate for the gradient and oscillation. The nested closed height intervals in Proposition 14.20.3 are nonempty, bounded, and have length tending to zero. Their intersection is therefore one number $c_\infty $. At each time the distance of any height to this number is at most that interval's length. Combining these estimates proves the assertion. If $V_0=1$, the solution is already a constant and the estimates have zero right-hand side. The proof needs no conservation of the ordinary spatial average. $\square$
