Lemma 4.5.1 (Locality of perimeter under compact perturbations)3BAFA27CC6BD

Lemma 4.5.1 (Locality of perimeter under compact perturbations).3BAFA27CC6BD Suppose $E,F$ have locally finite perimeter in $U\subset \mathbb R^ D$ and $\chi _ E=\chi _ F$ almost everywhere on $U\setminus K$, where $K\Subset U$ is compact. Then their distributional derivatives and perimeter measures agree on $U\setminus K$. If $K\subset W\Subset U$ is open and $\phi \in C_ c^1(W)$ equals one near $K$, then

\[ P(F;W)-P(E;W)=\int \phi \, d(|D\chi _ F|-|D\chi _ E|). \]
Proof

Tests supported outside $K$ have identical integrals against the two characteristic functions, hence identical distributional derivatives there. Uniqueness of the representing vector measure and its total variation gives equality of the perimeter measures on that open set. The signed difference is therefore supported in $K$. Since the measures are finite on $\overline W$, integration against $\phi $ agrees with their total difference on $W$. $\square$
