Proposition 12.5.9 (Stress-energy conservation)A8022BB0F3F8

Proposition 12.5.9 (Stress-energy conservation).A8022BB0F3F8 For a smooth solution of $\varepsilon ^2\Delta u=W'(u)$ on $U$, the tensor in Definition 12.5.8 satisfies

\[ \operatorname {div}T=0,\qquad \int _ U T:DX=0\quad (X\in C_ c^1(U;\mathbb R^ n)). \]
Proof

Differentiate the two terms in $T$. The mixed Hessian terms cancel and leave $(\operatorname {div}T)_ j=(\varepsilon ^{-1}W'(u)-\varepsilon \Delta u)D_ j u=0$. Integration by parts gives the test-field identity. Compact support removes the boundary term. The conclusion uses the Euler equation, not just an energy bound. $\square$
