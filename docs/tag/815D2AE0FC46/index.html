Lemma 14.12.4 (Small-scale control for compact perturbations of a shrinker)815D2AE0FC46

Lemma 14.12.4 (Small-scale control for compact perturbations of a shrinker).815D2AE0FC46 Let $\Sigma _ s$ be a compact normal graph family over a connected complete smooth properly embedded two-sided boundaryless self-shrinker $\Sigma $ with polynomial area growth. Fix $R<\infty $. There are constants $A,B,C\geq 0$, independent of small $s$, $|c|\leq R$, and $0<t\leq 1$, such that

\[ \partial _ tG(c,t,s)\geq -A-BG(c,t,s). \]

In particular, for $0<t\leq \tau \leq 1$,

\[ G(c,t,s)\leq e^{B\tau }G(c,\tau ,s)+C\tau . \]
Proof

On the unchanged part of the surface, $|H|^2=|x^\perp |^2/4\leq |x|^2/4$. On the changed compact region the curvatures are uniformly bounded by some $C_ H$. Each perturbed surface has polynomial area and curvature growth, so Lemma 14.12.2 applies and gives

\[ \partial _ tG\geq -\frac1{16}\int _\Sigma |x|^2\rho _{c,t}\, dA -\frac{C_ H^2}{4}G. \]

Theorem 14.11.3 gives finite entropy of $\Sigma $. Proposition 14.10.5 bounds the first integral by $2\lambda (\Sigma )(R^2+C_ nt)$, proving the differential inequality with uniform constants. Multiply by $e^{Bt}$ and integrate from $t$ to $\tau $ to obtain

\[ G(c,t,s)\leq e^{B(\tau -t)}G(c,\tau ,s) +A e^{-Bt}\int _ t^\tau e^{Bu}\, du \leq e^{B\tau }G(c,\tau ,s)+Ae^ B\tau . \]

This proves the claim with $C=Ae^ B$. It is the uniform small-scale estimate in [CM12], (7.49)–(7.51); no curvature bound at infinity is assumed for the original shrinker. $\square$
