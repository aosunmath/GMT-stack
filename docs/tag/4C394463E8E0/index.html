Proposition 16.1.8 (Power-law bounds for the Cantor measure)4C394463E8E0

Proposition 16.1.8 (Power-law bounds for the Cantor measure).4C394463E8E0 Write $s=\log 2/\log 3$. For every $x\in C$ and $0<r<1$,

\[ \frac18r^ s\leq \mu _ C(B_ r(x))\leq 8r^ s. \]

In particular $\mu _ C(B_ r(x))/(2r)\to \infty $ at every $x\in C$, despite the absence of atoms.

Proof

Choose $k\geq 1$ with $3^{-k}\leq r<3^{-k+1}$. The level-$k+2$ interval containing $x$ has diameter strictly less than $r$ and hence lies in $B_ r(x)$. Its mass is $2^{-k-2}$. Since $r^ s<2\cdot 2^{-k}$, this is at least $r^ s/8$. Distinct level-$k$ intervals have length $L=3^{-k}$ and gaps at least $L$. An interval of length $2r<6L$ meets at most eight of them. Their total mass is at most $8\cdot 2^{-k}\leq 8r^ s$. Finally $s<1$, so the lower bound divided by $2r$ diverges. The constants are deliberately nonsharp. $\square$
