Proposition 14.5.1 (Logarithmic rescaling at a finite time)F139BDB06172

Proposition 14.5.1 (Logarithmic rescaling at a finite time).F139BDB06172 Fix a point $x_0$ and a time $T$ later than the times under consideration. Set

\[ \tau =-\log (T-t),\qquad \widetilde F(p,\tau )=(T-t)^{-1/2}(F(p,t)-x_0). \]

Then the normal velocity of the rescaled hypersurfaces is

\[ (\partial _\tau \widetilde F)^\perp = \vec H_{\widetilde\Sigma _\tau }+\tfrac 12\widetilde F^\perp . \]

If the original flow is Type I at $T$, its rescaled curvature is uniformly bounded. This last assertion supplies a curvature bound, not a claim of convergence or uniqueness of a limiting shrinker.

Proof

Since $dt/d\tau =T-t$ and $(T-t)^{-1/2}=e^{\tau /2}$, differentiation yields $\partial _\tau \widetilde F=\widetilde F/2+(T-t)^{1/2}\vec H$. The second term is the mean curvature vector of the dilated hypersurface. Project normally to get the equation. Proposition 14.3.12 gives $|\widetilde A|^2=(T-t)|A|^2$, proving the curvature assertion. Huisken [Hui90], Section 2, uses instead $(2(T-t))^{-1/2}$ and $-\frac12\log (T-t)$; his rescaled equation has coefficient one instead of $1/2$. $\square$
