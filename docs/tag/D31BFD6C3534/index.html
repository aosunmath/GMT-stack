Corollary 8.6.6 (Uniform loss away from stationary varifolds)D31BFD6C3534

Corollary 8.6.6 (Uniform loss away from stationary varifolds).D31BFD6C3534 For every $\eta >0$ such that $K_\eta =\{ V\in K_ C:d(V,S_ C)\geq \eta \} $ is nonempty, there is $d_\eta >0$ with

\[ \| \mathscr H(1,V)\| (M)\leq \| V\| (M)-d_\eta \quad (V\in K_\eta ). \]

In particular, if the mass losses of a sequence tend to zero, every convergent subsequence of the original varifolds has stationary limit.

Proof

Take $d_\eta =\min _{K_\eta }D>0$ using compactness and positivity from Proposition 8.6.5. If $V_ j\to V$ and the actual losses tend to zero, the bound $0\leq D(V_ j)\leq \| V_ j\| (M)-\| \mathscr H(1,V_ j)\| (M)$ implies $D(V)=0$. Thus $V\in S_ C$. A fixed deformation and its fixed positive defect are used here; arbitrarily small losses for a changing sequence of unrelated deformations would not yield this conclusion. $\square$
