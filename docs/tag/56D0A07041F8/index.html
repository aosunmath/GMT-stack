Lemma 7.14.1 (An iterated Holder inequality for coordinate projections)56D0A07041F8

Lemma 7.14.1 (An iterated Holder inequality for coordinate projections).56D0A07041F8 For $i=1,\ldots ,n$, let $F_ i$ be a nonnegative integrable function of the $n-1$ coordinates $\widehat x_ i$ obtained by omitting $x_ i$. Then

\[ \int _{\mathbb R^ n}\prod _{i=1}^ n F_ i(\widehat x_ i)^{1/(n-1)}\, dx \leq \prod _{i=1}^ n\left(\int _{\mathbb R^{n-1}}F_ i\right)^{1/(n-1)}. \]
Proof

For $n=2$ this is the product identity from Tonelli. Suppose it is known in dimension $n-1$. Integrate in $x_ n$ first and apply Holder to the first $n-1$ factors, with all exponents equal to $n-1$. Put $G_ i(\widehat x_ i,\widehat x_ n)=\int _{\mathbb R}F_ i(\widehat x_ i)\, dx_ n$ for $i<n$, where the argument of $G_ i$ consists of the remaining $n-2$ coordinates. The integral in the statement is at most

\[ \int _{\mathbb R^{n-1}}F_ n(x')^{1/(n-1)} \prod _{i<n}G_ i(\widehat x_ i,\widehat x_ n)^{1/(n-1)}\, dx'. \]

Holder with exponents $n-1$ and $(n-1)/(n-2)$ bounds this by

\[ \| F_ n\| _1^{1/(n-1)} \left(\int _{\mathbb R^{n-1}}\prod _{i<n}G_ i^{1/(n-2)}\, dx'\right)^{(n-2)/(n-1)}. \]

Apply the induction hypothesis to the integral and use $\| G_ i\| _1=\| F_ i\| _1$. This gives the result. Zero factors are immediate; truncation to bounded functions on bounded boxes and monotone convergence justify the argument without any initial finiteness assumption on the product integral. $\square$
