Proposition 4.11.3 (Compactness of every sequence of reduced-boundary dilations)FB1F9CB663FD

Proposition 4.11.3 (Compactness of every sequence of reduced-boundary dilations).FB1F9CB663FD Let $x\in \partial ^*E$ and let $r_ j\downarrow 0$ be any sequence. A subsequence of $\chi _{E_{x,r_ j}}$ converges in $L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathbb R^ n)$ to $\chi _ F$ for a set $F$ of locally finite perimeter. Its rescaled perimeter measures satisfy, for each fixed $R>0$ and all sufficiently large $j$,

\[ |D\chi _{E_{x,r_ j}}|(B_ R)\leq 2n\omega _ n R^{n-1}. \]

This is the compactness passage preceding equation (7) in [Sim18], p. 98.

Proof

By Lemma 4.9.6 the left side is $r_ j^{1-n}\mu (B_{r_ jR}(x))$. Proposition 4.9.5 gives the displayed bound as soon as $r_ jR<r_0$. The characteristic functions have $L^1$ norm at most $|B_ R|$ on that ball. Use Corollary 4.2.8 on successively larger balls and extract a diagonal subsequence. The dilated domains contain each fixed ball eventually; discard a finite initial segment before applying compactness on that ball. This does not require finite perimeter outside the original open set. The local limits agree on overlaps and are characteristic functions. Lower semicontinuity gives local finite perimeter of $F$. $\square$
