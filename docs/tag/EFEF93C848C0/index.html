Lemma 9.4.1 (Mass and flat norms under metric comparison)EFEF93C848C0

Lemma 9.4.1 (Mass and flat norms under metric comparison).EFEF93C848C0 If $a^2g\leq h\leq b^2g$ as quadratic forms, where $0<a\leq b$, then every rectifiable mod-two $k$-chain $T$ satisfies

\[ a^ k\mathbf M_ g(T)\leq \mathbf M_ h(T)\leq b^ k\mathbf M_ g(T). \]

For the flat norm of an $n$-chain,

\[ \min \{ a^ n,a^{n+1}\} \mathcal F_ g(T) \leq \mathcal F_ h(T) \leq \max \{ b^ n,b^{n+1}\} \mathcal F_ g(T). \]

In particular the two flat topologies agree.

Proof

On an approximate tangent $k$-plane the eigenvalues of $h$ relative to $g$ lie in $[a^2,b^2]$. The square root of their product, the area-density ratio, lies in $[a^ k,b^ k]$. Integrate against the rectifiable multiplicity to obtain the mass bounds, also on every measurable subset. For each flat decomposition $T=R+\partial S$, with $R$ of dimension $n$ and $S$ of dimension $n+1$, apply the upper mass bounds to the two terms and take the infimum. This gives the upper flat bound. Interchanging the two metrics gives the lower bound. Smooth uniformly equivalent metrics have the same rectifiable and finite-mass chain classes, so these infima use the same decompositions. $\square$
