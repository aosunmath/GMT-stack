Lemma 14.16.4 (A parallel normalized shape tensor has rank one)4280CB2B07C9

Lemma 14.16.4 (A parallel normalized shape tensor has rank one).4280CB2B07C9 Let $U$ be a connected open region of a translator where $H\ne 0$ and $B=A/H$ is parallel. Then $B$ is the orthogonal projection onto a one-dimensional parallel distribution. Thus $A$ has rank one on $U$, $|A|^2=H^2$, and the induced metric is flat.

Proof

The symmetric parallel tensor $B$ has constant eigenvalues on connected $U$ and trace one. Codazzi applied to $A=HB$ gives

\[ (\nabla _ iH)B_{jk}=(\nabla _ jH)B_{ik}. \]

If $\nabla H$ is nonzero at a point, choose the first frame vector in its direction. Taking $i=1$ and $j\ge 2$ shows $B_{jk}=0$ for all such $j,k$. Symmetry and trace one make $B$ a rank-one orthogonal projection there, and parallel transport gives the same eigenvalues throughout $U$.

If no such point exists, $H$ is a nonzero constant on $U$. But $J_ vH=0$ would then give $|A|^2H=0$, contradicting $H=\operatorname {tr}A\ne 0$. Thus the preceding case must occur. The nullity and range distributions of a parallel projection are parallel. In the Gauss equation every sectional curvature is a difference of two products of entries of a rank-one form and is zero, so the induced metric is flat. $\square$
