Corollary 7.12.7 (Stability passes to a smooth positive-multiplicity limit)3A92CD67456B

Corollary 7.12.7 (Stability passes to a smooth positive-multiplicity limit).3A92CD67456B Under the hypotheses of Theorem 7.12.6, if every $\Sigma _ j$ is stable, then $\Sigma $ is stable, irrespective of the positive multiplicity $m$.

Proof

The theorem gives $\operatorname {index}(\Sigma )\leq 0$, which is stability. Equivalently, the second variation of any ambient extension of a normal test on $\Sigma $ is the limit of nonnegative second variations, and the positive factor $m$ can be divided out. If the limiting support is singular, this corollary does not assert that it is a smooth stable hypersurface. $\square$
