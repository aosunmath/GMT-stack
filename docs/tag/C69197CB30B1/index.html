Corollary 2.5.7 (The Lebesgue density theorem for measurable sets)C69197CB30B1

Corollary 2.5.7 (The Lebesgue density theorem for measurable sets).C69197CB30B1 For every Lebesgue measurable $E\subset \mathbb R^ n$,

\[ \lim _{r\downarrow 0}\frac{\mathcal L^ n(E\cap B_ r(x))}{\omega _ n r^ n} =\mathbf1_ E(x)\quad \text{for almost every }x\in \mathbb R^ n. \]

Neither finite total measure nor a regular boundary is assumed. See [Sim18], Corollary 3.10.

Proof

The indicator belongs to $L^1_{\rm loc}$. Apply Theorem 2.5.6 to it. At a point in $E$, its absolute oscillation average is the complementary volume fraction; outside $E$, it is the volume fraction of $E$ itself. The conclusion is almost everywhere in ambient Lebesgue measure, and makes no assertion at every topological boundary point. $\square$
