Definition 3.2.1 (Area and coarea Jacobians)ECFDA4F8C960

Definition 3.2.1 (Area and coarea Jacobians).ECFDA4F8C960 For a linear map $L:\mathbb {R}^ m\to \mathbb {R}^ n$ with $m\leq n$, define its area Jacobian by

\[ J_ mL=\sqrt{\det (L^*L)}. \]

For $L:\mathbb {R}^ n\to \mathbb {R}^ m$ with $m\leq n$, define its coarea Jacobian by

\[ J_ mL=\sqrt{\det (LL^*)}. \]

Here $L^*$ is the Euclidean adjoint. Each quantity is zero when $L$ has rank less than $m$. For a locally Lipschitz map, use the appropriate expression with $L=Df(x)$ at points of differentiability, and set it to zero on the exceptional null set. See [Sim18], Chapter 2, equation (3.2), p. 57, and Chapter 3, equation (2.7), p. 93.
