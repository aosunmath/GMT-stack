Proposition 16.13.1 (Global arclength coordinates for the unit grim reaper)DA07A2F7D6E1

Proposition 16.13.1 (Global arclength coordinates for the unit grim reaper).DA07A2F7D6E1 The curve $y=-\log \cos x$, $|x|<\pi /2$, has the global arclength parametrization

\[ X(s)=(\arctan (\sinh s),\log \cosh s),\qquad s\in \mathbb R. \]

With upward normal $\nu =(-\tanh s,\operatorname {sech}s)$, its scalar curvature is $H=-\operatorname {sech}s$. For velocity $v=e_2$, its weight and Jacobi operator are

\[ \rho _ v=\cosh s,\qquad J_ v=\frac{d^2}{ds^2}+\tanh s\frac d{ds}+\operatorname {sech}^2s. \]
Proof

The identity $\cos (\arctan (\sinh s))=\operatorname {sech}s$ places the point on the graph. Its derivative is $(\operatorname {sech}s,\tanh s)$ and has length one; the first coordinate increases onto $(-\pi /2,\pi /2)$. Differentiating the normal gives $\nu '=-\operatorname {sech}s\, X'$, proving the signed curvature. The height is $h=\log \cosh s$, whose derivative is $\tanh s$, and $|A|^2=\operatorname {sech}^2s$. Definition 14.14.1 gives the displayed operator. $\square$
