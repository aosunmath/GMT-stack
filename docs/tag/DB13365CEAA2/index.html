Proposition 8.4.2 (A parameter-dimension obstruction)DB13365CEAA2

Proposition 8.4.2 (A parameter-dimension obstruction).DB13365CEAA2 A $p$-sweepout on a finite cubical complex $X$ requires $\dim X\geq p$. Having dimension at least $p$ is not sufficient for a family to be a $p$-sweepout.

Proof

The cellular cochain groups above $\dim X$ vanish, and cellular cohomology agrees with singular cohomology. Hence $H^ p(X;\mathbb Z_2)=0$ when $p>\dim X$, contradicting detection. Conversely a constant family has zero pullback in every positive cohomological degree, regardless of the dimension of its parameter space. The cellular comparison theorem is the external algebraic-topology input. $\square$
