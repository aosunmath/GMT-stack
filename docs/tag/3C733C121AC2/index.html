Lemma 10.4.2 (The puncture flux at a smooth immersed sheet)3C733C121AC2

Lemma 10.4.2 (The puncture flux at a smooth immersed sheet).3C733C121AC2 Let $F:M^2\to \mathbb R^ N$ be a smooth compact immersion, possibly with smooth boundary, and let $y\notin F(\partial M)$. Its preimage is a finite set of interior points, of cardinality $m$. For small $\varepsilon >0$, remove from each such sheet the region $|F-y|<\varepsilon $. Along the resulting inner boundary, the outward conormal $\eta _\varepsilon $ of the remaining surface satisfies

\[ \int _{\text{inner boundary}}\frac{X^ T}{r^2}\cdot \eta _\varepsilon \, ds\longrightarrow -2\pi m. \]

Near each puncture, $X^\perp =O(r^2)$; hence $X^\perp /r^2$ is bounded and locally square integrable.

Proof

An immersion is an embedding on a neighborhood of each parameter point. The preimage of $y$ is closed in the compact domain and each of its points is isolated. An infinite preimage would have an accumulation point contradicting local injectivity, so it is finite. Compactness excludes any further image points near $y$ outside the chosen sheet neighborhoods.

Translate $y$ to zero and write a sheet as a graph $F(z)=(z,u(z))$ over its tangent plane, with $u(0)=0$, $Du(0)=0$, $u(z)=O(|z|^2)$ and $Du(z)=O(|z|)$. Its radial function is $r=|z|(1+O(|z|^2))$, and its gradient has $|\nabla _ Mr|=1+O(r^2)$. The small radial level is a smooth perturbation of a circle, with length $2\pi \varepsilon +O(\varepsilon ^3)$. The graph expansion also gives $|X^\perp |=O(r^2)$, since the leading linear vector lies in the tangent plane and the tangent plane changes by $O(r)$.

At the inner level, $\eta _\varepsilon =-\nabla _ Mr/|\nabla _ Mr|$ and $X^ T=r\nabla _ Mr$. Thus the integrand is $-|\nabla _ Mr|/\varepsilon $, and its integral is $-2\pi +O(\varepsilon ^2)$ for each sheet. Sum over the finite preimage. If $m=0$, a small ball misses the image and there is no puncture flux. $\square$
