Corollary 14.8.7 (Orthogonality of the geometric modes on a closed shrinker).21CF7F379644 On a smooth closed two-sided self-shrinker,

\[ [H\langle v,\nu \rangle ]=0 \]

for every constant vector $v$. Nonzero rotation modes are likewise orthogonal to $H$ and all translation modes.

Proof

All smooth functions are compactly supported on a closed manifold. The bilinear symmetry in Theorem 14.8.4 gives $[uLw]=[wLu]$. Apply the pointwise equations of Propositions 14.8.5 and 14.8.6. Distinct eigenvalues $1,1/2,0$ force the respective inner products to be zero. $\square$
