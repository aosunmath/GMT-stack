Proposition 5.1.4 (Lower semicontinuity of mass)D219E445117D

Proposition 5.1.4 (Lower semicontinuity of mass).D219E445117D If $T_ j\rightharpoonup T$ in $U$, then for every open $W\subset U$,

\[ \mathbf{M}_ W(T)\leq \liminf _ j\mathbf{M}_ W(T_ j). \]

Proof. For each test form $\omega $ in the supremum defining $\mathbf{M}_ W(T)$, $T(\omega )=\lim _ jT_ j(\omega )\leq \liminf _ j\mathbf{M}_ W(T_ j)$. Take the supremum over $\omega $. This proof applies even if the limiting lower bound is infinite. Compare [Sim18], Chapter 6, Section 2. $\square$
