Proposition 7.2.1 (The minimal graph equation)118E021473BF

Proposition 7.2.1 (The minimal graph equation).118E021473BF Let $U\subset \mathbb R^ n$ be open and $u\in C^2(U)$. The graph of $u$ is stationary for compactly supported vertical variations if and only if

\[ \operatorname {div}\left(\frac{Du}{\sqrt{1+|Du|^2}}\right)=0. \]

Equivalently, $\bigl (\delta _{ij}-u_ i u_ j/(1+|Du|^2)\bigr )u_{ij}=0$, where repeated indices are summed. Compare [SimonPDE], Chapter 19, and [ChMin], Section 18.

Proof. For a relatively compact domain containing the support of a test function $\phi $, the graph area is $\int \sqrt{1+|Du|^2}$ by the area formula. Its derivative at $u+t\phi $ is $\int Du\cdot D\phi /\sqrt{1+|Du|^2}$. Integration by parts gives the stated equation. Expanding the divergence gives the nondivergence expression. Since $u$ is $C^2$, the weak and pointwise expressions agree. $\square$
