Remark 7.2.2 (Regularity techniques and their hypotheses)5BFC45FF0297

Remark 7.2.2 (Regularity techniques and their hypotheses).5BFC45FF0297 Minter's Spring 2024 course [Minter] is the guide for a later proof sequence: tangent cones and stratification (Section 1), stable hypersurfaces (Section 2), Allard regularity and excess decay (Section 3), cylindrical tangent cones (Section 4), and Wickramasekera's sheeting theory (Section 5). Simon [SimonPDE], Chapters 5–9, 12, and 17–19, supplies Sobolev, elliptic, Schauder, and De Giorgi–Nash–Moser tools.

These are distinct regularity inputs. Stationarity by itself does not imply smoothness. An application of Allard's theorem must state its density, excess, and first-variation assumptions; an application of stable-varifold regularity must also state its structural and dimension assumptions. Detailed formulations and proofs of these theorems have not yet been added here.
