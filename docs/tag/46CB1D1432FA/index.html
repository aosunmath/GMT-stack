Example 12.5.6 (A local solution need not satisfy Modica's bound)46CB1D1432FA

Example 12.5.6 (A local solution need not satisfy Modica's bound).46CB1D1432FA The initial-value problem $u''=u^3-u$, $u(0)=0$, $u'(0)=2$ has a smooth solution on a neighborhood of zero, with $|u|<1$ after restricting the interval. At zero its defect is $P(0)=2-1/4=7/4>0$. Thus boundedness on a small domain and the Euler equation alone do not imply Theorem 12.5.4. This also distinguishes the pointwise estimate from the energy inequality, which remains valid for this function.
