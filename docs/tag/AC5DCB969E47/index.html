Corollary 12.11.3 (The entire critical set is compact for fixed epsilon)AC5DCB969E47

Corollary 12.11.3 (The entire critical set is compact for fixed epsilon).AC5DCB969E47 For a fixed $\varepsilon >0$ and fixed smooth closed $(M,g)$, every sequence of smooth Allen–Cahn critical points has a subsequence converging in $C^ k(M)$ for every finite $k$ to a smooth critical point. The constants in these compactness estimates may depend on $\varepsilon $.

Proof

The maximum bound and Proposition 12.11.1 supply all assumptions of Theorem 12.11.2, with residual zero. For the stronger convergence, the equation has uniformly bounded right-hand side after division by the fixed $\varepsilon ^2$. Global estimates obtained from the finitely many local elliptic charts give uniform $W^{2,p}$ bounds for every finite $p$. Choose $p>n$ to control $C^{1,\alpha }$, and bootstrap the smooth polynomial nonlinearity to uniform bounds of all derivative orders. These are the same external elliptic estimates used in the theorem. Arzela–Ascoli and a diagonal subsequence give every $C^ k$ convergence. The limit equation follows either from this convergence or from the preceding weak argument. These estimates do not assert compactness as $\varepsilon \downarrow 0$. $\square$
