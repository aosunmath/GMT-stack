Theorem 7.7.3 (Topology and area of a stable surface in positive scalar curvature)478117A3A30D

Theorem 7.7.3 (Topology and area of a stable surface in positive scalar curvature).478117A3A30D Let $\Sigma $ be a nonempty smooth closed connected orientable two-sided stable minimal surface in a Riemannian three-manifold. If $R_ M>0$ along $\Sigma $, then $\Sigma $ has genus zero. If moreover $R_ M\geq \sigma >0$ there, then

\[ \operatorname {Area}(\Sigma )\leq \frac{8\pi }{\sigma }. \]

The genus conclusion is the closed orientable form of [ChMin], Theorem 24.3, attributed there to Schoen–Yau. Both the closedness and the orientability hypotheses enter the proof.

Proof

The constant test function is admissible because $\Sigma $ is closed. Corollary 7.7.2 and Gauss–Bonnet give

\[ 0<\int _\Sigma (R_ M+|A|^2) \leq 2\int _\Sigma K_\Sigma =4\pi \chi (\Sigma )=8\pi (1-g). \]

Thus the nonnegative integer $g$ is zero. The same inequality with $R_ M\geq \sigma $ gives $\sigma \operatorname {Area}(\Sigma )\leq 8\pi $. Gauss–Bonnet and the classification of closed orientable surfaces are the topological inputs. This argument applies to an immersion as well, using its induced metric and global normal. $\square$
