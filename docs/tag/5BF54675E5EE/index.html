Proposition 16.15.5 (The compact mean-curvature threshold)5BF54675E5EE

Proposition 16.15.5 (The compact mean-curvature threshold).5BF54675E5EE On every connected closed Euclidean self-shrinker $M^ n$, in any codimension,

\[ \frac1{\operatorname {Vol}(M)}\int _ M|\vec H|^2\, d\mu =\frac n2. \]

If $|\vec H|^2$ is everywhere at most $n/2$, everywhere at least $n/2$, or constant, then the image lies in $S^{N-1}(\sqrt{2n})$ and is minimal there. See [CL12], Proposition 5.2, for the source normalization.

Proof

The immersion identities give

\[ \Delta |X|^2=2n+2\langle X,\vec H\rangle =2n-4|\vec H|^2. \]

Integrating on a closed manifold gives the average identity. Each stated pointwise condition forces $|\vec H|^2=n/2$ everywhere, so $|X|^2$ is harmonic and hence constant on the connected compact manifold. It follows that $X^ T=\nabla |X|^2/2=0$. The shrinker equation now gives $|X|^2=4|\vec H|^2=2n$, and Proposition 16.15.1 gives spherical minimality. This conclusion does not say that an arbitrary-codimension example is a round hypersphere. $\square$
