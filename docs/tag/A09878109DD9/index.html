Proposition 7.15.1 (Gauss and Codazzi in a space form)A09878109DD9

Proposition 7.15.1 (Gauss and Codazzi in a space form).A09878109DD9 With $R(X,Y)Z=\nabla _ X\nabla _ YZ-\nabla _ Y\nabla _ XZ-\nabla _{[X,Y]}Z$, one has

\[ R(X,Y)Z=c\bigl (\langle Y,Z\rangle X-\langle X,Z\rangle Y\bigr ) +h(Y,Z)AX-h(X,Z)AY, \]
\[ (\nabla _ Xh)(Y,Z)=(\nabla _ Yh)(X,Z). \]

In a principal orthonormal frame, the sectional curvature of the plane spanned by $e_ i,e_ j$, $i\ne j$, is $K_{ij}=c+\kappa _ i\kappa _ j$. Compare [HP99], Section 2, p. 49.

Proof

Differentiating $\langle \nu ,Y\rangle =0$ gives $\overline\nabla _ XY=\nabla _ XY-h(X,Y)\nu $, while $\overline\nabla _ X\nu =AX$. Insert these decompositions into the ambient curvature expression. Its tangential part is

\[ (\overline R(X,Y)Z)^ T=R(X,Y)Z-h(Y,Z)AX+h(X,Z)AY. \]

The ambient space-form formula yields the stated Gauss equation. The normal part is $-(\nabla _ Xh)(Y,Z)+(\nabla _ Yh)(X,Z)$ and vanishes because the ambient curvature expression is tangent. This is Codazzi. Finally take $X=e_ i$, $Y=Z=e_ j$, and pair with $e_ i$ in the Gauss equation. $\square$
