Proposition 11.5.6 (The constant-potential stability criterion)DFACAFB09577

Proposition 11.5.6 (The constant-potential stability criterion).DFACAFB09577 Suppose $q$ is constant on the closed connected hypersurface and let $\lambda _1>0$ be the first positive eigenvalue of $-\Delta _\Sigma $. Then

\[ \Sigma \text{ is volume-constrained stable}\quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad q\leq \lambda _1. \]

If $q>0$ and an ambient Killing field has a nonzero normal speed on $\Sigma $, with the hypotheses of Proposition 11.5.5, this is equivalent to $q=\lambda _1$. This is the precise nontrivial-mode setting of [BdCE88], Proposition 2.13 and its preceding paragraph.

Proof

The Rayleigh characterization of the first eigenvalue gives $\int |\nabla f|^2\geq \lambda _1\int f^2$ for every zero-mean smooth $f$. Thus $q\leq \lambda _1$ implies nonnegativity of $Q$. If $q>\lambda _1$, a first eigenfunction gives $Q(f,f)=(\lambda _1-q)\int f^2<0$. Existence and smoothness of this eigenfunction use the compact Laplace spectral theorem. For a nonzero Killing normal speed, $Jf=0$ gives $-\Delta f=qf$, and $f$ has zero mean. Hence $q$ is a positive eigenvalue and $q\geq \lambda _1$. Combining the inequalities gives the last equivalence. Without such a mode, equality is not part of the general criterion. $\square$
