Definition 7.1.1 (Mean curvature and minimality)28E53582FC82

Definition 7.1.1 (Mean curvature and minimality).28E53582FC82 For a smooth immersed hypersurface $\Sigma \subset M$, let

\[ B(X,Y)=(\nabla ^ M_ XY)^\perp ,\qquad \vec H=\operatorname {tr}_{T\Sigma }B. \]

We call $\Sigma $ minimal when $\vec H=0$. If a global unit normal $\nu $ exists, $\Sigma $ is two-sided. Our scalar convention is $H=\operatorname {div}_{\Sigma }\nu $, so that $\vec H=-H\nu $. With the outward normal, a Euclidean sphere of radius $R$ has $H=n/R$. Here $H$ is the sum of principal curvatures; in the Willmore energy we explicitly use their average. See [MM], Section 2, and [ChMin], Section 7.
